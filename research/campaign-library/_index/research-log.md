### 2026-08-17 - Deep extraction/discovery pass 02: active dossiers and licence-scope auditing
- Search scope: move beyond title collection into source/canon dossiers; distinguish `whole adventure openly licensed` from `only a font/map/SRD/template is CC`; identify longer openly adaptable adventures; capture community feedback separately from canon.
- Search surfaces: primary itch creator pages, creator Patreon posts/devlogs, creator websites, official OPD rules, Reddit r/osr/rDnD/r/onednd, itch comments, creator catalogues, web exact-title searches.
- Active dossiers created: `castle-inspection/`, `well-what-now/`, `island-of-trials/`, `whispered-caverns-june-serin/`, `overthrow-the-tyrant/`.
- Castle Inspection findings: current v1.2; OPD 2025 second place; undead heroes serve lich Radagast; Imperial Insurance Inspector Ignatius the Intransigent; Butler Bones; confirmed crises include fire, loose monsters, unraveling rituals, haunted library, crying monster babies, goats; v1.2 notes prove flame-elemental->phoenix, librarians, earthquakes/debris and werewolves exist in source sections. Reddit feedback identifies participation risk from constant party-splitting; creator acknowledges alternative pacing/escort ideas. Partial crisis state graph created, but full PDF/map/solutions/endings remain blockers.
- Well, what now? findings: OPD 2025 Overall Winner; merchant dragged into mysterious well during escort; twisting cave-maze rescue; English/Catalan/Spanish files; actual-play comments mention likely source entities `Maria`, an apron-wearing pig-man and frogs. Those entities remain community-observed until PDF verification. Broader Pork Mort setting content kept legally/canonically separate from the CC BY-SA contest submission. Partial opening graph created.
- Island of Trials findings: exact source configuration is four level-5 D&D 5e characters, a few hours; involuntary arrival on mysterious island; unknown test; free/PWYW PDF. Itch labels Asset license CC BY 4.0, but platform documentation confirms this is creator-selected metadata and the PDF's internal scope still needs inspection. Dossier is therefore licence-provisional for full adaptation.
- Whispered Caverns findings: Pandion Games expressly releases the adventure module itself CC BY 4.0; 1-4 players, ~4-10 hours / 1-4 sessions; Landry Horn sends coded plea; miners lose entire-shift memories while bringing back 3-4x unusually pure ore; investigation/social emphasis. Mystery-safe graph created that forbids the AI from choosing a cause before source extraction.
- Overthrow the Tyrant findings: eight-page system-neutral OSR zine; creator expressly licenses zine CC BY 4.0; Blancaseda region/city, consequences from `The Heroes Who Departed`, wilderness events, city locations, factions, Tyrant, Dead Parrots' Tavern / Undead Kings lead. Political/faction graph scaffold created without inventing unresolved source terms.
- Additional licence-verified candidates discovered: Under the Rusted Sun (adventure CC BY 4.0), The Cultists' Keep of Kthrone (adventure/map CC BY 4.0), Cargoth: Ruinous Edition (adventure CC BY 4.0; paid content), Cheeseur the Geezer and the Smell Below (adventure CC BY 4.0; paid), De farra a Ruïnaceleste (CC BY 4.0; Catalan hexcrawl/three-level dungeon, 8 special locations, tables and six magic items).
- Licence traps captured: Lakeside Under Moonlight credits its *homebrew template* as CC BY 4.0 while the adventure remains © creator; A.C. Luke one-page adventures discovered in this pass credit CC fonts/icons but do not state that the adventure text itself is CC. They remain source-verified/pending, not adaptable by assumption.
- Additional leads preserved: Curse of the Witch Meat, Daring to Disturb Doreen the Witch Queen, The Whimsical World of Waxia Woolsworth and various OPD-related entries; verify individually.
- Community intelligence rule reinforced: actual-play comments, Reddit advice and reviews are stored separately and never promoted to canon without source verification.
- AI-DM architecture insight: mysteries require immutable truth/clue graphs; political adventures require faction/rumour state; concurrent-crisis adventures require independent timers/states; dungeon crawls require hard map topology locks.
- Follow-up targets: full PDF extraction for all five active dossiers; start dossiers for Under the Rusted Sun, De farra a Ruïnaceleste and Cultists' Keep; locate more long-form CC adventures outside itch/OPD; investigate GitHub/open repositories and older blog archives; determine public packaging strategy for CC BY-SA campaign derivatives.
- Files added/updated: candidate registry, source registry, this research log, and multiple source/canon/NPC/location/encounter/story-graph/QA files across the five active campaign folders.
