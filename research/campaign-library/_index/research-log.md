### 2026-08-17 - Deep discovery pass 01: open adventures, one-page archives, creator pages, Reddit
- Search scope: discover legally adaptable fantasy adventures with emphasis on D&D 5e compatibility, system-neutral fantasy, explicit Creative Commons licensing, and community-vetted one-page adventures; also identify misleading cases where only SRD/mechanics are open.
- Search engines / communities / repositories used: web search, itch.io, DungeonContest.com, Campaign Wiki references, Reddit r/osr, Reddit r/DnD, D&D Beyond forums, RPGnet reviews, AdventureLookup, creator blogs/sites.
- Representative queries used: `site:reddit.com/r/DnDBehindTheScreen free 5e adventure homebrew campaign pdf levels`; `site:itch.io tabletop adventure "CC BY 4.0" 5e campaign`; `"Creative Commons" "5e adventure" RPG campaign PDF`; `Reddit best one page dungeon contest entries`; `site:itch.io "5e adventure" "CC BY 4.0"`; exact-title follow-ups for Island of Trials, Curse of Corvus Farm, Deal With The Devil, Well what now, Thawed Through the Everflame, White Scales, Solomon's Screaming Tomb.
- Candidates discovered/registered: Island of Trials; OVERTHROW THE TYRANT; Curse of Corvus Farm; Deal With The Devil; 2025 Dungeon Anthology; Well, what now?; Thawed Through the Everflame; White Scales; Temple of Rhythm; OPD 2025 full compendium; OPD 2010-2017 back catalogue; Solomon's Screaming Tomb; Trials of the Undying; Escape from the Shadow Realm; Quest for the Vorpal Sword; The Ethereal Proclamation; I Think No One's Home; 2004: Cubicle Farm; Subway Dark.
- Primary sources verified: itch.io creator pages for Island of Trials, OVERTHROW THE TYRANT, Curse of Corvus Farm, 1pagedungeons titles, Pork Mort title, Day 20 title, Riley Clark titles, RootDwarf title; creator blog for Deal With The Devil; official One Page Dungeon Contest rules/store.
- Licence findings: Island of Trials is explicitly CC BY 4.0; OVERTHROW THE TYRANT explicitly CC BY 4.0; 2025 OPD rules require CC BY-SA 4.0 and original work; current 1pagedungeons OPD titles/anthology explicitly CC BY-SA 4.0; older official OPD store descriptions identify 2010-2017 compendiums as CC BY-SA 3.0; Curse of Corvus Farm creator says Creative Commons but exact variant is unresolved; Deal With The Devil creator says Creative Commons but exact variant is unresolved; The Ethereal Proclamation is CC BY-ND 4.0 and cannot be transformed; I Think No One's Home text is CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 and is unsuitable for commercial DMOS redistribution; Trials of the Undying and Riley Clark adventures only license incorporated SRD content, not the original adventures.
- Community intelligence captured: Reddit r/osr maintains and recommends Campaign Wiki as a comprehensive OPD archive; Simon Carryer's 2025 OPD review specifically values emergent/non-teleological dungeons and selected Horror's Hollow Hand and The Demoniac of Taphos, while criticizing entries requiring too much unstated system prep. Reddit r/DnD recommendations point repeatedly toward the One Page Dungeon Contest and curated lists, while noting that good one-page adventures often leave mechanical details to the GM. Pork Mort itch comments include at least one actual-play response and positive design/art feedback for Well, what now?. RPGnet review of Solomon's Screaming Tomb praises its weird creatures, global effects and replay value but calls out formatting and rules-language problems.
- Rejected / blocked candidates: Solomon's Screaming Tomb is metadata-only because its OGL declaration expressly reserves proper names, plot, storylines, locations and characters as Product Identity; The Ethereal Proclamation is metadata-only because NoDerivatives prevents DMOS transformation; I Think No One's Home is metadata-only because of NonCommercial; Trials of the Undying, Escape from the Shadow Realm and Quest for the Vorpal Sword are metadata-only absent separate permission for original adventure content.
- High-value discovery pool: One Page Dungeon Contest. Official 2025 rules require CC BY-SA 4.0 and original content, and Reddit points to Campaign Wiki as a year-by-year archive. This gives DMOS a large legally structured pool for quality screening rather than random scraping.
- Follow-up targets: obtain/read full licensed PDFs for Island of Trials, OVERTHROW THE TYRANT, Well what now, Thawed Through the Everflame, White Scales and selected OPD winners; inspect exact PDF legal text for Curse of Corvus Farm and Deal With The Devil; investigate Simon Carryer's curated OPD recommendations and OPD 2025 entries Horror's Hollow Hand / The Demoniac of Taphos; expand Reddit, RPGnet and blog searches for actual-play failure modes before final templating.
- Files added or updated: `_index/candidates.md`, `_index/source-registry.md`, `_index/research-log.md`; campaign folders begin in the next extraction pass.
