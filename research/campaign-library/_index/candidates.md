## Status policy

Use one of: `discovered`, `source-verified`, `licence-verified`, `researching`, `extracted`, `templated`, `qa`, `approved`, `metadata-only`, `rejected`.

Every row that progresses beyond `discovered` should receive its own folder under `../campaigns/<slug>/`.
