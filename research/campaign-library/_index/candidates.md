# Campaign Candidate Registry

All discovered adventures/campaigns go here immediately, including weak, inaccessible, rejected, metadata-only, and licence-blocked candidates. Nothing is silently discarded.

| Campaign | Creator / Publisher | Discovery Source | Primary Source | Ruleset | Level Range | Licence | Status | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Status policy

Use one of: `discovered`, `source-verified`, `licence-verified`, `researching`, `extracted`, `templated`, `qa`, `approved`, `metadata-only`, `rejected`.

Every row that progresses beyond `discovered` should receive its own folder under `../campaigns/<slug>/`.
