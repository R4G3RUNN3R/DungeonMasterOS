Purpose: preserve official adventure metadata and source links without republishing protected campaign text. ## Current official 2026 research leads

### Dungeon Masters actual-play campaigns
D&D Beyond launched an official actual-play anthology series in 2026. Campaign 1 is Ravenloft-focused; Campaign 2 uses Arcana Unleashed content in the Anauroch Desert. Public pages describe play-along packs and episodic encounters, but this does not make the full campaign story reusable. Store as discovery/architecture references only unless explicit reusable terms are published.

### Ravenloft: The Horrors Within / D&D in a Castle campaigns
Official 2026 event pages describe four exclusive Ravenloft campaigns at castle events. These are event experiences, not public open modules. Do not template from summaries.

## Copyright / implementation policy

For official/commercial adventures:
- public website may show title, level range, spoiler-light description, publisher/source link and ownership/import requirement
- do not reconstruct complete locations, NPC dialogue, maps, encounters, secrets, scene text or branching outcomes from web summaries
- future `Import Campaign` can parse material supplied by a user who owns/accesses it and create a private per-user/per-campaign structured graph
- do not publish the resulting private graph as shared DMOS catalogue content unless separate rights permit it
- community fixes/reviews can be stored as metadata/intelligence but cannot substitute for source content
