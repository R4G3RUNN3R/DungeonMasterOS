This append-only batch captures newly verified open adventures and long-form campaign leads discovered after the first OPD/GitHub sweep. It exists so discoveries are not lost while the master registry is periodically consolidated.

## High-priority long-form / multi-location candidates

### Knoll of Gnolls
- Creator: Infinite Fractal
- Primary source: https://infinite-fractal.itch.io/knoll-of-gnolls
- Format: dungeon-delving adventure module
- Intended tier: low-level characters
- System: designed for Embark; explicitly easy to adapt to old-school systems
- Size/structure: over 40 keyed locations
- Premise: a secret cave hidden among rolling knolls has been claimed by gnolls rumoured to be vicious and mutated; ancient creatures and secret magics remain below
- Table support: labelled referee maps, unlabeled player maps, stat-block reference page, stat blocks repeated at relevant rooms, reference maps on every two-page spread
- Licence: **all game content CC BY-SA 4.0**; art separately CC0, CC BY-SA 4.0, or public domain
- Files: 31 MB single-page PDF and 31 MB spreads PDF
- Status: licence-verified / high-priority dossier
- DMOS value: large keyed-location dungeon with strong topology, source-defined creatures, room state and reusable maps

### Monkey Isle - current 3rd Edition only
- Creator: J. D. Neal / Basic Fantasy Project
- Current publisher/source metadata: Basic Fantasy Project Lulu listing
- Format: 60-page adventure setting/module
- System: Basic Fantasy RPG / old-school fantasy
- Recommended levels: **4-6**
- Premise: lush lost-world island with prehistoric beasts, strange creatures and scattered ancient ruins
- Structure: island map; numerous wilderness and dungeon adventure locations; extensive new-monster section; intended as a place groups can revisit
- Licence: current **3rd Edition CC BY-SA**, explicitly advertised as 100% OGL-free and AI-free
- Access note: Basic Fantasy Project states its PDFs are provided free on its website; the official site is blocked to this research browser by robots, so current 3rd-edition PDF still needs provenance-safe acquisition
- Critical version warning: indexed 2nd Edition / Release 21 copies are OGL-era and display older copyright/licence notices. Do **not** mistake those for the new CC 3rd Edition when extracting/packaging DMOS content.
- Status: licence-verified current edition metadata / high-priority long-form dossier
- DMOS value: wilderness + keyed locations + dungeons + monster ecosystem + explicit level range

### The Equinoctial Threshold
- Creator/writer: Marlowe Rehac / Godbotherers; edited by Dylan Garofalo
- Primary source: https://godbotherers.itch.io/equinoctialthreshold
- System: Cairn 2E compatible
- Format: puzzle-dungeon / Fae threshold and Night Market
- Licence: **CC BY-SA 4.0**; creator explicitly states the work is AI-free and openly licensed
- Current file: v1.3 (4.2 MB); earlier jam v1.0 also remains downloadable
- Core location: one of eight portals to the Fae, hidden under a grassy forest hill among roots of an ancient tree
- Access rule: Boggart magic opens its stone door only briefly each night and seals mortal/Fae visitors inside during the day
- Historical canon: vicious warlord Laidre the Duskkiller and his Satyr Bullies once garrisoned the Threshold; Ste. Brumaire, Saint of Boundaries, confronted him, died, and Laidre was cursed never to leave
- Night Market: goblin merchants trade not in coins but currencies such as luck, faces, time and talents
- Source-supported goals include: buy a peculiar item; loot Laidre's vault for the Gloomblade; avenge Ste. Brumaire; seek Fae passage; destroy the Boundary Stone and send away the Threshold/Night Market
- Status: licence-verified / high-priority nonlinear dossier
- DMOS value: multiple competing objectives, unusual transaction state, timed access, faction/history state and no single mandatory ending

## Licence traps / do-not-assume-open

### Il Maniero del Vecchio Bertoldo
- Italian low-level OSR adventure
- Source pitch suggests level 1 / one session
- Search result credits a Dyson Logos map under CC BY 4.0
- No verified statement yet that the **adventure text itself** is CC
- Status: source lead / licence-pending
- Lesson: a CC map embedded in a copyrighted adventure does not open the adventure.

### Older Monkey Isle editions
- Indexed copies of 2nd Edition / Release 21 show the OGL 1.0a and older copyright language.
- The current 3rd Edition is separately advertised as CC BY-SA and OGL-free.
- DMOS should treat editions as distinct legal/source artifacts and extract the current edition for the built-in library.

## Basic Fantasy broader campaign pool

The Basic Fantasy ecosystem contains a substantial catalogue of adventure modules and anthologies. Indexed catalogues include:
- Adventure Anthology One, Two and Three
- Morgansfort
- Fortress, Tomb and Tower
- Strongholds of Sorcery
- Castle by the Sea
- The Dark Temple
- Tales from the Laughing Dragon
- The Chaotic Caves
- Monkey Isle
- Saga of the Giants
- The Blackapple Brugh
- The Endless Tunnels of Enlandin
- and many shorter community modules

**Do not bulk-ingest this catalogue.** Basic Fantasy has been producing newer Creative-Commons/OGL-free editions, while older indexed PDFs can still be OGL-era. Verify the exact current edition and content licence of every module before extraction.
