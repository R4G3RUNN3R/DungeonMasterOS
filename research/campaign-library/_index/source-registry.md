# Campaign Research Source Registry

This registry tracks every website, repository, archive, community, marketplace, forum, index, and creator location used to discover or verify campaign material.

| Source / Community | Type | URL | What It Is Useful For | Authority Level | Licence Notes | Last Checked |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Authority levels

- `primary` - original author/publisher/repository/publication
- `official-metadata` - official catalogue/listing but not necessarily full content
- `mirror` - copy/archive; useful only when provenance can be established
- `community` - Reddit/forum/blog/community reports and recommendations
- `index` - discovery catalogue or search surface
- `archive` - historical preservation source

Community and index sources must not be treated as proof of licence, canon, authorship, or authoritative content without verification.
