## Vampire: The Masquerade / World of Darkness

### Storytellers Vault legal boundary
- Primary policy: https://help.storytellersvault.com/hc/en-us/articles/12780357275927-Ownership-and-License-Questions
- Official policy explicitly says Storytellers Vault is NOT an Open Game License.
- World of Darkness/Vault material remains governed by the community content agreement and platform rules.
- Vault content cannot be treated as generic open content for DMOS.
- Official format policy currently says apps/computer games are not a permitted Storytellers Vault publication format.
- DMOS policy: VTM/Vault chronicles are metadata + private import unless a separate licence/partnership specifically permits application use.

## Research policy reinforced by this batch

1. `Ruleset` is mandatory; `level` is not. Non-level systems use their real progression model.
2. Setting and ruleset are distinct. Ravenloft is a setting/domain framework running on a D&D edition, not a standalone rules engine.
3. Explicit creator compatibility claims are recorded separately from DMOS conversions.
4. A rules engine being Creative Commons does not make an adventure using it Creative Commons.
5. Storytellers Vault is not an open licence and currently does not permit publishing apps/computer games through that program; therefore VTM/WoD campaign integration requires metadata/private-import or separate commercial permission.
6. CC BY-SA campaign adaptations require ShareAlike compliance for derivative campaign material.
7. CC0/open adventures are highest-priority built-in candidates because they give DMOS the widest legal room for structured transformation.
