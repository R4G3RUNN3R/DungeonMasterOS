## Strong current-year / explicit-adventure-license candidates

### The Sunken Scriptorium
- Creator: Lars Ohnesorge / negativeblack
- Primary source: https://negativeblack.itch.io/the-sunken-scriptorium-a-dungeoncrawl-for-shadowdark-rpg
- Format: one-page dungeon crawl
- Compatibility: Shadowdark, D&D 5e and OSR-style games
- Recommended levels: **1-3**
- Premise: explore an ancient scriptorium inhabited by mutated cultists, ghosts and dangerous secrets
- Licence: **adventure explicitly CC BY-SA 4.0**
- Files: adventure PDF plus separate map PDF
- Year/source page: 2026
- Status: licence-verified / high-priority low-level dossier

### The Glorious City of Gelbenhügel
- Creator: David Garrett
- Primary source: https://carpedavid.itch.io/gelbenhugel
- Format: developing fantasy city/setting rather than a single adventure
- Compatibility: Shadowdark / Cairn / OSR ecosystem
- Licence: **CC0 public-domain dedication for the city setting**
- Includes map, Adventurer's Guide, sewer map and permissive third-party compatibility licence
- Status: open setting resource / campaign-background candidate
- DMOS value: unusually permissive reusable city that could host multiple openly licensed adventures or original DMOS campaign templates

### Third-party system licence != adventure licence
- Many Shadowdark/MÖRK BORG/Cairn/5e products state that the **system/SRD** is Creative Commons or third-party licensed while the adventure itself remains copyrighted.
- Registry rule remains: promote only when the creator explicitly licences the adventure/text/game content, or another authoritative licence applies to the submitted work (e.g. OPD contest rules).
