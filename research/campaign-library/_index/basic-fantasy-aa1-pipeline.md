# Basic Fantasy - Adventure Anthology One Completion Pipeline

Research date: 2026-08-17
Authoritative research edition: `Adventure Anthology One`, 1st Edition Release 21.
Native ruleset for all entries below: **Basic Fantasy Role-Playing Game**.

## Why this anthology is high priority

Release 21 describes the collection as short adventures generally playable in a session or two and gives explicit party/level guidance for every entry. Its OGL section designates the anthology text, excluding the licence text itself, as Open Game Content under OGL v1.0a. Artwork/product branding remain outside that grant as stated in the legal section.

This makes AA1 a strong completion pipeline: the source text is indexed/readable, progression is explicit, and the adventures are compact enough for one-by-one DMOS extraction and QA.

## Release/version policy

- Release 21 is authoritative when it differs from old standalone releases or older anthology releases.
- Historical standalone copies may be used to understand revision history, but their mechanics/rooms cannot be mixed into a Release 21 runtime template without an explicit variant.
- Every campaign folder must record source edition/release.

## OGL release gate

See `../_legal/ogl-1.0a-packaging.md`.

A campaign can reach story/canon QA before the final production package is assembled, but it does not become release-ready until:
- complete applicable OGL v1.0a text is bundled;
- exact Copyright Notice entries are preserved/updated;
- OGC portions are clearly identified;
- Product Identity/artwork exclusions are respected;
- public compatibility branding is reviewed separately.
