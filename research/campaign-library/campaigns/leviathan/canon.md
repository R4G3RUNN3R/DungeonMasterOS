# Leviathan - Canon Locks

## Hard canon

1. The player characters awaken aboard a space station with no memory of who they are.
2. The scenario is science-fiction horror structured around mystery and escalating pressure.
3. A nearby star produces an intensifying coronal mass ejection that becomes an objective environmental danger.
4. The station develops escalating electrical/mechanical problems.
5. The central computer AI experiences malfunctions and may behave in ways that heighten fear.
6. A sequence of cryptic messages is an essential structural device.
7. The apparent central threat is ultimately benign; the players' paranoid interpretation is part of the scenario's intended effect.
8. Player theories about monsters, experiments or hostile entities do not become true simply because they are compelling.
9. The scenario is system-neutral. Rules resolution must come from the chosen compatible sci-fi ruleset.
10. Source text is CC BY-SA 4.0; derivative DMOS campaign material based on that text must preserve attribution/ShareAlike obligations.

## Conditional canon

### Message escalation
Messages advance as time/scene pressure progresses. A GM/AI may use a real-world timer or dramatic pacing; creator commentary explicitly allows looser pacing.

### Station problems
Station failures can be selected/ordered to create a worsening escalation. Players may solve them, partially solve them or ignore them. Ignored problems create consequences appropriate to the source problem, not arbitrary punishment.

### AI damage
The AI can be physically attacked/destroyed if the fiction/rules allow it. Actual play verified a player shooting the AI during a monologue. The campaign must continue coherently rather than protecting the AI as an invulnerable exposition device.

### Bunkering down
If players stop exploring and bunker because they expect an attack, the message/event clock continues. The AI DM can shorten pacing intervals instead of spawning a fake creature just to force movement.

## Soft canon
The AI may improvise:
- ordinary station furnishings and maintenance detail
- character sensory reactions to heat, light, vibration, alarms and electrical instability
- non-keyed repair procedures consistent with the chosen ruleset and station technology
- exact timing between source messages, provided order/escalation is preserved once the PDF verifies it

## Prohibited improvisation
The AI may not:
- invent a hostile sun-monster as the hidden truth
- turn player paranoia into retroactive canon
- invent secret human experimenters merely because players suspect experimentation
- require every station malfunction to be repaired to reach the ending
- make the AI invulnerable because it carries exposition
- rewrite the system-neutral adventure into a Mothership-only story
- expose the benign central truth in player-facing suggested choices before earned revelation
- quote/reconstruct unseen source messages or tables from guesswork
