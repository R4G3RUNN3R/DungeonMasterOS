## Actual-play / creator comments
The primary itch page contains a detailed GM report and player comment.

Verified run:
- 2 players
- Mothership rules
- everyone enjoyed the session
- paranoia generated theories involving a beast in the sun and experimentation
- AI countdown caused panic/hiding
- one PC shot/destroyed the AI during its monologue
- airlock breach/final messages led players to bunker down and anticipate an attack
- one PC sealed themself in a cryopod/sleep pod while the other prepared to protect/eject it

GM procedure feedback:
- selected station problems and AI malfunctions in advance instead of rolling randomly so escalation became progressively worse
- retained a nominal 15-minute timer but found message delivery at dramatically appropriate moments worked better
- suggested shortening intervals late if players bunker down
- clues generated many competing theories without needing extra invented clues
- Mothership created horror expectation/tension
- few rolls were actually required because the session was investigation-heavy
- GM granted automatic success when actions sensibly worked and rolled when failure had meaningful consequences
- players ignored some station problems such as exploding beakers and a solar-panel problem because other priorities felt more important

Creator response:
- explicitly agreed that not following the timer strictly can work well
- described the real-world timer as a preferred technique rather than an inviolable story requirement
