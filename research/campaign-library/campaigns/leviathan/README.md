## Player-facing pitch
You wake on a space station with no memory of who you are or why you are there. A nearby star is growing dangerous, the station is failing in increasingly alarming ways, the central AI is malfunctioning, and cryptic messages keep arriving. Work out what is happening before fear makes the decisions for you.

## Structural design
Leviathan is driven by escalating pressure rather than a linear room sequence.

Core state:
1. `messageIndex` - how far the cryptic message sequence has progressed.
2. `solarThreat` - intensifying coronal mass ejection / environmental pressure.
3. `stationFailures` - unresolved electrical/mechanical problems.
4. `aiState` - central computer behaviour/malfunctions/damage.
5. `partyTheory` - not canon; AI DM may track player suspicions without making them true.
6. `truthRevealed` - whether the final benign-twist explanation has been legitimately reached.

## Hidden structural truth
Verified actual-play commentary states the apparent central threat is ultimately benign. The scenario derives much of its horror from amnesia, ambiguous evidence, real-time pressure, AI malfunctions and players constructing a more frightening explanation than reality.

This is SECRET GM canon. It must never appear in player-facing catalogue copy, opening narration or suggested choices.

## AI-DM principles
- Escalate environmental and informational pressure without inventing a hostile monster merely because players expect one.
- Player theories are not truth flags.
- Problems may be ignored. Ignoring a station problem creates consequences but does not invalidate the scenario.
- The AI may choose/sequence source station problems and AI malfunctions to improve escalation rather than randomising blindly; this is supported by actual-play experience.
- The source's real-time message concept can be adapted to scene pacing. The creator explicitly endorsed non-strict timer pacing as a valid approach after actual-play feedback.
- Mothership is a verified playable ruleset through actual play, but Leviathan remains system-neutral at its core.
