### `We fix everything before investigating anything.`

### `We ignore every problem and only read messages.`

### `We smash the viewports because the thing outside is watching us.`
PASS structurally.

### `We don't trust the messages and delete them.`
PASS with source-completeness caveat.

### `We leave the station immediately.`
PASS with source-completeness caveat.
If a source-defined escape vehicle/route exists and is usable, allow it. Do not invent an escape craft solely to satisfy the choice. Exact station escape capability requires PDF verification.

### `I try to contact another ship/station.`
PASS with source-completeness caveat.
Use source communications capabilities once verified. Do not invent rescue contacts or external NPCs without source support.
