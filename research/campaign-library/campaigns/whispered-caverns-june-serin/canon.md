# The Whispered Caverns of June Serin - Canon register

## Hard canon currently confirmed

- The adventure is `The Whispered Caverns of June Serin` by Pandion Games.
- It is written for the Quest RPG.
- Mining teams working in the lowest levels of the Whispered Caverns lose their memories of entire shifts.
- Those same shifts produce roughly three to four times the miners' normal ore output.
- The ore is suspiciously pure.
- Landry Horn is an old friend of the player characters.
- Landry travelled to the Frontier Lands seeking fortune and became a miner in June Serin.
- Landry sends the player characters a coded letter pleading/begging for help.
- The adventure is intended for 1-4 players and approximately 4-10 hours / 1-4 sessions.
- Investigation, environmental clues and social interaction are more prominent than combat.
- The characters are assumed to begin as ordinary people helping a friend and face extraordinary circumstances.

## Mystery hard lock

The cause of the memory loss, increased output and pure ore is **not established by the public hook**. Until the full source reveals it, the AI DM must not select or imply a definitive cause.

Player theories are allowed. NPC theories may be improvised only as beliefs/rumours, never as hidden truth, unless source canon later supports them.
