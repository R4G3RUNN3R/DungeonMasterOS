# The Whispered Caverns of June Serin

Status: `researching`

## Executive summary

`The Whispered Caverns of June Serin` is an investigation-focused adventure module by Pandion Games for the Quest RPG. Mining teams working in the lowest levels of the Whispered Caverns are losing all memory of entire work shifts while returning with three to four times their normal loads of suspiciously pure metal ore. The party's old friend, Landry Horn, is among the miners and has sent a coded letter begging for help.

The creator explicitly releases **the adventure module itself** under **CC BY 4.0**. It supports **1-4 players** and is estimated at **4-10 hours / 1-4 sessions**. The published style ratings emphasise social interaction and investigation/environmental problem solving over combat.
