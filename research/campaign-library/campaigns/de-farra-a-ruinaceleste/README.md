# De farra a Ruïnaceleste

Status: `researching`

Language: Catalan source

## Executive summary

`De farra a Ruïnaceleste` is a strange-fantasy OSR adventure by Oriol G.V. / Rolets_CAT. The creator explicitly pitches it for **4-6 adventurers of levels 4-8**, using the reader's preferred fantasy RPG, and structures it as a **three-act adventure** in a skyborne setting.

The creator page exposes substantially more structure than most campaign listings: three major adventure spaces, an eight-location hexcrawl, a three-floor lair/dungeon, random encounter/object/event tables, six magic items, and a new `Arcaòloga` class. The text/layout/cartography are credited to Oriol G.V.; art/cover to Sergi San Julián.

The creator page explicitly states **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International** and says the material may be shared and adapted for any purpose with attribution. This makes it one of the strongest larger-format candidates currently found.

## Confirmed major spaces

1. `La gran casa de les portes` - central hub/meeting place for the area's inhabitants.
2. `Les terres de Ruïnaceleste` - hexcrawl map with eight special locations, each with its own events/scenes.
3. `L'altar de les armes deposades` - three-floor lair/dungeon with traps and enemies.

## Additional confirmed content
