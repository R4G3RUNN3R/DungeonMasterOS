Community evidence is not source canon.

A Catalan Enrolats community wiki lists a special game titled `De farra a Ruïnaceleste` with five player characters:
- Cregbaf - Oriol
- Stabilo - Martí
- Esmó Corindó - Adolf
- Sir Nod Gorcole - Jordi

This is a strong signal that the adventure has been played with five participants/characters, which fits the creator's stated 4-6-player range. It does **not** establish that these are official pregenerated characters or source NPCs.
