- three-floor dungeon/lair with traps and enemies

Licence discrepancy note:
- Lulu metadata currently labels copyright as `Creative Commons ShareAlike (CC BY-SA)`
- the creator itch page explicitly states `Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International` and explains sharing/adaptation with attribution, i.e. CC BY 4.0
- this discrepancy must be resolved by inspecting the actual PDF licence notice before public DMOS release
- until resolved, follow the **more restrictive plausible interpretation** for packaging decisions rather than assuming the easier one
