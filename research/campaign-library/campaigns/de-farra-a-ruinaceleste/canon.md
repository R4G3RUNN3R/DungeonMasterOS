# De farra a Ruïnaceleste - Canon register

## Hard canon currently confirmed
- Strange-fantasy OSR adventure for 4-6 adventurers of levels 4-8.
- Adventure has three acts.
- `La gran casa de les portes` is the central hub/meeting place for the area's inhabitants.
- `Les terres de Ruïnaceleste` is a hexcrawl with exactly 8 special locations, each with events/scenes.
- `L'altar de les armes deposades` is a three-floor dungeon/lair with traps and enemies.
- Adventure contains encounter, object and event tables.
- Adventure contains six magical/weird items.
- Adventure contains a new class called `L'Arcaòloga` involving magic and remnants of ancient history.
- The creator pitch frames the adventure as saving the world from the sky.
