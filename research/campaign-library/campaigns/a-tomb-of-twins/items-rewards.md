# A Tomb of Twins - Important Items and Rewards

## Drezis Soul Vial
- Location: Room 11 pedestal under anti-magic field
- Function: anchors Drezis's lich-state immortality
- Removal without valid anti-magic protection: contributes to Drezis rising
- Emptying: permanently destroys Drezis
- Drinking contents: causes source-defined life-drain effect
- Value: extremely high to external buyers
- Plot critical: YES

## Woxis Soul Vial
Same state logic as Drezis's vial for Woxis.

## Anti-Magic Ritual Scroll
- Location: hidden compartment at base of Room 11 pedestal
- Describes static and movable anti-magic field variants
- Uses entrance glyphs, blood, Room 9 basin liquid and ritual action
- Movable variant is the safe source method for transporting vials
- Source deliberately leaves ritual execution difficulty adjustable to table style
- Plot critical: YES

## Moon Amulet
- Location: central Faceless Figure in Room 4
- Function: one half of safe Room 10 door solution
- Plot critical: YES unless alternative trap-bypass solution succeeds

## Sun Amulet
- Associated with western mirrored path / swampkin-looted Room 7/Room 12 state
- Function: second half of Room 10 safe-door solution
- Plot critical: YES unless alternative bypass succeeds

## Drezis Disguise Set
- Pieces: hat, robe, shoes across Room 3 treasure
- Function: complete set prevents Room 4 Faceless Figures from attacking merely on entry
- Optional puzzle/combat avoidance tool

## Woxis Disguise Set
- Pieces in Room 6 treasure
- Originally mirrored western guardian logic; western encounter has been disrupted by swampkins
- Still valuable/identifying treasure

## Drezis Journal
- Location: Room 3 treasury
- Reveals lich plan, hatred of Woxis and secret poisoning plan
- Reading takes substantial in-world time
- Strong social leverage if twins awaken

## Woxis Journal
- Location: Room 6 treasury
- Mirrors Drezis journal: lich plan, grievances against Drezis and betrayal/poisoning intent
- Strong leverage against twin cooperation

## Treasury cold water
- Rooms 3 and 6
- Disables body-heat chest trap
- Applied to weapons, permits harm to ghosts
- Environmental resource rather than inventory item unless bottled/improvised

## Room 9 healing liquid
- Heals wounds once per day per source procedure
- Removed from basin, loses power after a short random period
- Can permit weapons to harm ghosts
- Required ingredient in anti-magic ritual

## Drezis's magic utility items
Source examples include:
- magical wax seal that makes a door temporarily impossible to open
- endless glowing red string fixed to surfaces until owner releases it; especially useful for swampkin tunnel navigation
- other valuables/coins/gems

## Woxis's magic utility items
Source examples include:
- black candle that brilliantly lights a room but becomes immovable once lit
- magic-resistant paste providing temporary protection against magical damage
- valuables/coins/gem-encrusted treasure

## Glowing Orb
- Location: Room 9 ceiling
- Can be removed and used as portable light

## Bogumila reward
- Cash reward for the soul vials
- High immediate value, dangerous downstream custody

## Marzena reward
- Services of skilled Arcane Academy enchanters for equipment if vials are safely recovered
- Less immediate cash but safe containment consequence

## Swampkin alliance share
- Juris/Pristina may demand approximately half the tomb treasure for cooperation
- This is negotiable faction economy, not an automatic deduction

## Item-state principle
DMOS must track soul-vial custody continuously after leaving Room 11. The adventure's most important consequences occur after treasure leaves the dungeon. `Quest complete` must never erase who physically holds the vials or whether anti-magic protection is still active.
