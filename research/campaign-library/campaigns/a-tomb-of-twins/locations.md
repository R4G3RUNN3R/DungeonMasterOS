# A Tomb of Twins - Location Register

## Dreklow
- Type: fishing/coastal village beneath the tomb's cliff
- Important social site: Moot Hall
- Key NPCs: Bogumila, Marzena, Galduz
- Function: hooks, rumours, employer choice, post-adventure consequences

## Moot Hall
- Long communal stone hall centred on a large hearth
- Villagers gather for food/drink
- Bogumila conducts fencing/trade from a secluded end
- Marzena waits out her priesthood assignment near the fire
- Galduz lurks and seeks capable outsiders
- Rumours can be learned by spending time/drinks with villagers

## Tomb Exterior
- Featureless polished black dome on sea cliff overlooking Dreklow
- Well-worn village trail reaches it
- Large mirror-like silver exterior doors, closed but unlocked

## Room 1 - Entrance
- Small threshold room
- Poor tripwire/cowbell alerts swampkins if triggered
- Cryptic glyphs above inner doors are puzzle key for Rooms 5/8
- Mild unnatural weightlessness/unease telegraphs tomb magic

## Room 2 - Painted Scenes
- Main branch point
- Hidden east door -> Room 3
- Hidden west door -> Room 6
- Gold-disguised sconces act as secret-door controls
- Carvings encode disguise/amulet logic: east moon path and west sun path

## Room 3 - East Treasury
- Three trapped black-stone chests + cold-water basin
- Contains Drezis-linked outfit pieces, journal and magic utility treasure
- Door -> Room 4

## Room 4 - Faceless Banquet
- Seven faceless guardians around banquet table
- Central guardian bears moon amulet
- Full eastern disguise suppresses attack-on-entry
- Removing amulet/neutralising central figure stops group hostility
- Door -> Room 5

## Room 5 - East Strange Glyphs
- Nine floor glyphs
- correct entrance-glyph sequence -> safe passage
- wrong sequence -> descending/crushing ceiling
- exit to Room 9

## Room 6 - West Treasury
- Mirrored treasury with active low-gravity/magnetic trap
- trapped swampkin grunt currently floating at ceiling
- contains Woxis-linked outfit/journal and magic treasure
- cold basin has same anti-trap/ghost-weapon function
- door -> Room 7

## Room 7 - Ruined Banquet
- Original western mirrored guardian scene destroyed by swampkins
- Pristina present
- if bell was rung: six grunts prepare improvised ambush/disguise
- if no alarm: Pristina alone/cautious and can negotiate
- doors -> Room 6, Room 8; breach/cave connection -> Room 12

## Room 8 - West Strange Glyphs
- Mirrored glyph-floor challenge
- corpse/evidence of crushed swampkin demonstrates trap consequence
- exit -> Room 9

## Room 9 - Respite
- east/west paths reunite
- removable glowing orb provides light
- purple healing basin works once per day and loses power after being removed for a short time
- basin liquid can make weapons capable of harming ghosts
- paired doors -> Room 10

## Room 10 - Antechamber
- bright final approach
- ornate bronze doors to Room 11
- moon/sun recesses accept amulets from Rooms 4/12-related west path
- forcing/incorrect bypass triggers severe flame trap

## Room 11 - Burial Chamber
- central pedestal with twin soul vials held in anti-magic field
- hidden compartment contains anti-magic ritual scroll
- sarcophagi contain Drezis and Woxis
- removing vials without protection awakens the liches
- emptying a vial destroys linked lich
- location is central custody/aftermath decision node

## Room 12 - Swampkin Cave
- recently excavated cave connected to Room 7 and deeper swampkin tunnels
- occupied by Juris, Cestina, brutes and grunts
- loot from Room 7 present
- swampkins have not solved deeper tomb protections

## Swampkin Tunnel Maze
- rough labyrinth leading toward marsh south of Dreklow
- navigation can return to Room 12, strand travelers for days before emerging in marsh, lead to occupied chambers/dead ends/rope bridge and additional swampkins
- Drezis's endless red string can provide a reliable route back to the point where it was laid

## Global tomb spatial rule
Room 2 splits into east (3-4-5) and west (6-7-8) routes. Both rejoin at Room 9, which leads to Room 10 then Room 11. Room 7 also accesses Room 12/swampkin tunnels. The AI must respect these connections and may not teleport between mirrored rooms because they serve similar functions.
