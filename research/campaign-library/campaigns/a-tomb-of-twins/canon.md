# A Tomb of Twins - Canon Locks

## Hard canon
1. Drezis and Woxis were twin necromancers defeated centuries ago; their tower was dismantled and their tomb overlooks Dreklow.
2. Both planned lich transformation and secretly planned to poison the other.
3. Their corpses remain in Room 11 while their soul vials are suspended under anti-magic containment.
4. The Priesthood of Zorander renews the containment monthly and resets/cleans the tomb.
5. Marzena is the current novice assigned to the annual vial-security duty and genuinely wants them safely contained.
6. Galduz is a corrupted priest who wants outsiders to remove the vials so the twins can rise.
7. Bogumila wants the vials for their monetary value and intends to sell them.
8. The tomb traps spirits; most ghosts cannot leave its outer entrance or Burial Chamber boundary.
9. PCs who die inside the tomb may continue as trapped ghosts under the source rules.
10. Room 2 splits into east and west paths that can be explored in either order and rejoin at Room 9.
11. The eastern disguise set allows safe interaction with the Room 4 Faceless Figures; the western path's original mirrored guardian encounter was destroyed by swampkins.
12. The moon and sun amulets together safely open Room 10's final doors.
13. Rooms 5 and 8 use the glyph sequence shown above the inner entrance door; wrong sequences trigger crushing ceilings.
14. Cold basin water in the treasuries disables chest traps and can allow weapons to harm ghosts.
15. Room 9's purple liquid heals and can also enable ghost-harming weapons; removed liquid loses efficacy after limited time.
16. Removing soul vials from anti-magic containment without moving/recreating that protection awakens Drezis and Woxis as liches.
17. Emptying one vial permanently destroys its linked lich.
18. If both liches awaken together, their immediate hatred causes bickering and magical conflict; absent intervention they eventually call a truce and pursue conquest.
19. The twins can potentially be manipulated into temporarily siding against each other.
20. Swampkins recently tunneled into Room 12 and wrecked/looted Room 7. The priesthood has not yet discovered this incursion.
21. Swampkins are not inherently hostile and can negotiate, although leaders have conflicting goals and Juris may betray a profitable alliance.
22. Ringing the Room 1 cowbell alerts Room 7 swampkins and changes their preparation/state.
23. If the twins escape, Dreklow suffers immediate catastrophic consequences and the twins become a growing regional/world threat.

## Conditional canon
- If `bellRung=true`, swampkin grunts prepare an ambush in Room 7.
- If a full eastern disguise is worn, Room 4 Faceless Figures do not attack solely for entry.
- If the moon/sun amulets are used in Room 10, the final doors open without triggering the flame trap.
- If a glyph sequence is entered incorrectly in Room 5 or 8, the ceiling begins descending and escape becomes time-limited.
- If soul vials are removed while properly protected by a movable anti-magic field, the liches stay suspended while that protection persists.
- If vials are delivered to Marzena, the Arcane Academy keeps them safely contained.
- If vials are delivered to Bogumila without permanent containment knowledge, she sells them and the twins eventually rise when protection expires.
- If vials are delivered to Galduz, he intentionally releases the twins and kills himself.
- If PCs keep the vials, they must maintain monthly anti-magic protection or equivalent containment.
- If a PC dies in the tomb and chooses ghost continuation, their perspective/abilities change but the player remains active.

## Soft canon
The AI may improvise ordinary Dreklow villagers, exact ghost dialogue, minor dungeon sounds, and social phrasing consistent with established motivations.

## Prohibited improvisation
The AI may not:
- turn Galduz into a trustworthy immortality merchant
- make Marzena secretly want the twins released
- force swampkins to attack regardless of negotiation/state
- require east path before west path or vice versa
- delete the ghost-PC continuation option
- make the soul vials harmless treasure
- make giving vials to Bogumila safe without valid permanent containment
- make killing one twin automatically destroy the other
- protect the twins from being turned against each other
- invent a consequence-free immortality ending
