# A Tomb of Twins - Encounters, Traps and Procedures

## Ghost procedure
- Check for larger ghost encounters every other room entry or extended lingering under the source procedure.
- Ghostly insects/rodents are persistent ambient evidence.
- Generated ghost motive matters as much as combat ability.
- Cold treasury water or Room 9 purple liquid can temporarily enable weapons to harm ghosts.
- PCs killed inside may continue as ghosts.

## Room 1 alarm
- Tripwire is intentionally poorly made/telegraphed.
- Trigger -> cowbell -> `bellRung=true` -> Room 7 swampkins prepare ambush.
- Disarming/avoiding it preserves an unalerted western faction encounter.

## Treasury chest trap
- Body heat on handles triggers strong magnetism and low gravity.
- Environmental clues telegraph the effect.
- Cold objects/water bypass it.
- Room 6 begins with the trap already active, creating immediate movement/physics problem.

## Room 4 Faceless Figures
Possible solutions:
- wear full Drezis disguise
- seize/remove moon necklace from central figure
- defeat/neutralise central figure
- fight all figures
- retreat
They do not pursue outside the room.

## Rooms 5/8 glyph traps
- Exact safe sequence is copied from Room 1 glyph order and can be read in either direction per source.
- Correct steps progressively clarify the path.
- Wrong step starts crushing ceiling.
- Characters have a short action window to escape.
- Exit door reset is stateful.

## Room 7 swampkin encounter
If bell rung:
- six grunts prepare improvised ambush, including comic faceless-figure disguises
- Pristina is still a social actor and surrender/parley remains possible
If bell not rung:
- Pristina can greet cautiously and negotiate

## Room 10 door trap
- Correct moon + sun amulets open safely.
- Forcing/manipulating without correct solution triggers severe magical fire.
- Creative bypasses are permitted if physically/magically plausible under chosen ruleset; the AI should not declare the door metaphysically indestructible unless source rules support that.

## Room 11 soul-vial resolution
Key encounter types:
- investigate sarcophagi/pedestal
- discover anti-magic ritual scroll
- perform static/movable anti-magic ritual
- remove one/both vials safely
- remove vials unsafely and awaken liches
- deliberately spill/empty vial to destroy linked lich
- drink vial contents
- manipulate awakened twins using journals/resentment
- flee and deal with world-state consequences

## Awakened twins
- immediate bickering/conflict
- source gives a period before they call a truce
- players can attack, flee, bargain/manipulate, exploit hostility or target vials
- if they escape, this becomes a wider campaign consequence rather than instant arbitrary game over

## Room 12 swampkin diplomacy
Possible approaches:
- fight
- negotiate treasure split
- rescue/share knowledge with Pristina
- exploit Juris insecurity
- clash with Cestina
- use swampkin tunnels as alternate exit/adventure route

## Swampkin maze
Use source random navigation outcomes. Endless red string is an explicit strong navigation solution.

## Combat philosophy
The creator explicitly states combat is potentially deadly but avoidable. AI-DM must therefore surface noncombat affordances where the source provides them, rather than presenting every statted creature as a mandatory attack button.
