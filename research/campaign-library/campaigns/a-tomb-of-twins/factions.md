# A Tomb of Twins - Factions

## Dreklow Villagers
- Goal: live ordinary village life under the shadow of the tomb
- Resources: rumours, community knowledge, Moot Hall social network
- Knowledge: mostly folklore; truth value varies by rumour
- Relationship to tomb: fear/fascination rather than organised control

## Priesthood of Zorander
- Goal: keep Drezis/Woxis contained and the tomb's secrets controlled
- Procedure: monthly entry, renew anti-magic protection, clean bodies/reset traps
- Resources: secret knowledge, ritual, access to Arcane Academy
- Internal fracture: Galduz has become corrupted and secretly works against containment
- Current operative: Marzena, annual novice assignment

## Arcane Academy
- Goal: safe magical containment when alerted to the soul-vial danger
- Relationship: connected to Marzena/Priesthood
- Aftermath role: can maintain vials indefinitely in proper anti-magic field

## Swampkin Horde
- Origin: marshes south of Dreklow
- Current foothold: Room 12/tunnel network; looted Room 7
- Goal: secure treasure and return with status/resources
- Leadership:
  - Juris: warlord seeking legitimacy
  - Pristina: curious/cooperative priestess who championed expedition
  - Cestina: hostile/skeptical priestess who wants out
- Diplomacy: not inherently hostile; can ally for treasure share
- Internal tension: leaders disagree, so faction decisions can change with who dominates negotiation

## Drezis and Woxis
- State: not an active faction at campaign start
- Activation: both or either becomes active only through soul-vial/lich conditions
- Shared strategic endpoint: after initial quarrel, surviving/active twins seek conquest and undead domination
- Internal hostility: twins can fight one another or be manipulated separately
- If both escape: Dreklow becomes first target; expansion follows

## Bogumila's buyer network
- Type: off-screen criminal/collector market
- Goal: acquire valuable magical contraband
- Importance: explains why giving the vials to Bogumila does not equal safe disposal
- AI rule: do not invent named final buyer unless needed for a sequel; core fact is sale followed by containment expiry risk

## Ghosts of the Tomb
- Not a unified faction
- Shared condition: trapped by tomb magic
- Individual motives vary from escape, secrecy, curiosity, pranks, apathy and violence
- AI rule: never give all ghosts identical alignment/knowledge
