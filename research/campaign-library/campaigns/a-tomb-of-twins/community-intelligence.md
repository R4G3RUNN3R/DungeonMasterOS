# A Tomb of Twins - Community / Playtest Intelligence

## Creator development history
Reddit development posts show the adventure went through an extended public playtest/revision period rather than appearing as a single untested draft.

Verified creator posts include:
- 2022 playtest edition seeking blunt feedback; described mirrored paths, trapped spirits, deadly-but-avoidable encounters, secret doors and magic items
- January 2023 Mörk Borg stat release before a creator-run Mörk Borg playtest
- February 2023 new playtest draft
- later creator post describing it as the author's first serious OSR adventure and noting a long period of playtesting across different systems

This supports treating the final multi-system stat support as deliberate rather than an incidental conversion tag.

## Cairn play evidence
A public itch profile/comment surfaced in search stating a player used Cairn rules and found it easy to pick up, generate a character and begin play. This is modest community evidence rather than a detailed encounter report.

## Mörk Borg evidence
The creator publicly stated an intention to run/playtest the adventure using Mörk Borg and released Mörk Borg-compatible stat blocks during development. Final release includes those stats.

## No detailed independent actual-play report found
Current deep search did not uncover a sufficiently detailed independent session report describing which traps, employers or vial outcomes commonly cause trouble.

Do not invent consensus about:

## DMOS adaptation implications
1. Keep combat avoidable and telegraph hazards.
2. Preserve both mirrored routes and allow order choice.
3. Present the three Dreklow employers as genuinely competing incentives without UI-tagging one as `correct`.
4. Track vials after leaving the dungeon; downstream custody is the adventure's most important delayed consequence.
5. Ghost-PC continuation should be offered immediately after qualifying PC death, not buried in GM notes.
6. System choice should be explicit at campaign start: CRACK!/B/X-compatible, Cairn or Mörk Borg can use source stats; other OSR games require adapter conversion.
