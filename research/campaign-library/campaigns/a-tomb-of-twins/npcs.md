# A Tomb of Twins - NPC Register

## Bogumila
- Role: retired cat burglar / fence / employer option
- Location: Moot Hall, Dreklow
- Presentation: loud, prosperous, socially embedded in village
- Goal: obtain the twin soul vials and sell them for profit
- Offer: large cash payment for delivery
- Knowledge: secret doors exist in Room 2 but she does not know the opening mechanism
- Consequence: if given protected vials without permanent containment knowledge, she sells them; when protection lapses the twins rise

## Marzena
- Role: Priesthood of Zorander novice / employer option
- Location: Moot Hall
- Presentation: bookish, timid, irritated by assignment
- Goal: get the soul vials out safely and into Arcane Academy containment
- Offer: access to high-quality enchanting services
- Knowledge: key puzzle sequence is etched above the first internal tomb doorway
- Consequence: if given properly protected vials, Arcane Academy recognises danger and keeps them contained indefinitely

## Galduz
- Role: corrupted senior priest / deceptive employer
- Location: Moot Hall
- Presentation: shifty, terse, secretive
- True goal: have adventurers remove the soul vials so Drezis/Woxis can rise
- Cover story: claims the vials can grant immortality through a ritual he can perform
- Knowledge: extensive knowledge of tomb; may reveal selected room information if useful to manipulation
- Consequence: if given vials he removes their protection to release the twins, then kills himself

## Drezis
- Role: twin necromancer / conditional lich antagonist
- State at start: dead body in sarcophagus, lich emergence suppressed by anti-magic around soul vial
- History: wanted lichdom, hated Woxis, secretly planned to poison him
- Associated path: east/moon imagery and disguise; journal in Room 3
- If raised with Woxis: immediately quarrels with twin; can potentially be manipulated into temporary anti-Woxis alliance; eventually reconciles enough to pursue conquest
- Permanent destruction: empty/destroy linked soul vial under source logic

## Woxis
- Role: twin necromancer / conditional lich antagonist
- State at start: same suspended condition as Drezis
- History: wanted lichdom, hated Drezis, also planned betrayal/poisoning
- Associated path: west/sun imagery and disguise; journal in Room 6
- If raised: mirrors Drezis's conflict/conquest behaviour

## Pristina
- Role: swampkin priestess of Great Toad
- Location: Room 7
- Relationship: twin sister of Cestina
- Personality: kind-hearted, fascinated by humans
- Goal: secure treasure/progress for swampkin raid; she convinced Juris to come
- Belief: expects a key/item in western route may unlock the tomb's protections
- Diplomacy: can cooperate with adventurers for a fair treasure share, particularly if not pre-alerted into an ambush state

## Cestina
- Role: swampkin priestess of Great Toad
- Location: Room 12
- Relationship: twin sister of Pristina
- Personality: hostile/meaner, skeptical of raid
- Goal: leave the tomb/swampkin adventure behind; prefers killing intruders who threaten them
- Function: provides religious counsel/messages from Great Toad to horde through Pristina

## Juris
- Role: swampkin warlord
- Location: Room 12
- Personality: insecure/new leader, eager to prove worth
- Goal: return with treasure and strengthen standing
- Reliance: depends heavily on Cestina/Pristina
- Diplomacy: willing to work with adventurers for half of treasure; may double-cross if opportunity seems safe/profitable

## Trapped swampkin grunt
- Location: Room 6 ceiling due active treasury trap
- Goal: get down
- Information: willing to reveal useful swampkin details in exchange for rescue

## Faceless Figures
- Role: magical construct guardians
- Two original mirrored groups were created by divided souls; western group has been destroyed by swampkins, eastern group remains in Room 4
- Group shares soul/state through central necklace-bearing figure
- Behaviour: attacks intruders lacking proper wizard disguise, does not pursue beyond room
- Central figure retains more original identity/memory and is miserable; disabling/removing necklace neutralises group

## Ghost encounter personas
Generated source categories, not fixed named NPCs:
- villager ghost: wants release/passage to afterlife, trades information
- wizard ghost: wants secrets of Burial Chamber, trades knowledge for help
- goblin ghost: enjoys ghosthood/pranks, may be coerced into help
- priest ghost: guards secrets and may attack trespassers
- thief ghost: despondent, avoidant, but can be enticed into information
- rat ghost: animal behaviour, no useful information

## Dead player-character ghosts
- PCs who die inside the tomb may remain playable as ghosts using source stat block
- They cannot simply walk out through the tomb's spirit barriers
- Their personal knowledge remains what they learned while alive
- DMOS should preserve player agency rather than automatically converting them to AI-controlled NPCs
