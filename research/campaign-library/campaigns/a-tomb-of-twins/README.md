## Player-facing pitch
The ruined tomb of the twin necromancers Drezis and Woxis overlooks the fishing village of Dreklow. Every month a secretive priesthood enters the tomb to renew an unknown ritual. Villagers whisper about impossible treasure, imprisoned spirits and immortality. Several people are willing to pay very different prices for whatever lies in the burial chamber, and at least one of them is lying about why they want it.

## Central campaign truth
The monthly priestly ritual renews an anti-magic field around the twin necromancers' soul vials, preventing Drezis and Woxis from rising as liches. Multiple quest-givers want those same vials for incompatible reasons. The final danger is not merely opening the sarcophagi; removing the soul vials incorrectly, giving them to the wrong person, or failing to renew their protection can release the twins later.

## DMOS design rule
The AI must not decide there is one correct employer or one correct route through the tomb. The eastern and western paths can be explored in either order, swampkins can be fought or negotiated with, ghosts can provide clues for varying motives, and the soul-vial aftermath depends on player custody and delivery choices. Death inside the tomb can even become continued ghost play rather than immediate removal from the session.
