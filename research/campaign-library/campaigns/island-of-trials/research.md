# Island of Trials - Source-derived research

This file contains only facts supported by the currently accessible creator pages. The full PDF has not yet been extracted.

## Premise

A band of adventurers is whisked away to a mysterious island and faces an unknown test. The adventure is explicitly designed as a D&D 5e one-shot for four level-5 characters. The creator says it can be inserted into an existing campaign or used as a jumping-off point for a new one.

## Confirmed structural facts

- The island is the central adventure environment.
- Arrival is involuntary or externally caused: the party is `whisked away` rather than simply travelling there in the public pitch.
- The party faces some form of test/trial whose nature is intentionally not revealed in the spoiler-free synopsis.
- Source mechanics are D&D 5e rather than system-neutral.
- Intended group assumptions are unusually specific: four level-5 characters.
- Intended duration is a few hours.

## Important negative knowledge

The public synopsis does **not** establish:
- who transports the party
- why the party is selected
- the island's geography
- number or nature of trials
- NPCs
- factions
- monsters
- puzzles
- treasure
- final antagonist
- success conditions
- failure conditions
- whether departure from the island is possible before completion
- ending states

None of those may be invented as source canon.

## DMOS implementation direction after PDF extraction

Because the adventure has explicit 5e level/party assumptions, DMOS should preserve them as the default campaign configuration rather than rebalancing prematurely. The eventual story graph should model each trial and island location independently, expose 3-5 immediate choices where appropriate, and allow free-text actions while preventing the AI from bypassing source-defined trial conditions or revealing the purpose of the test early.
