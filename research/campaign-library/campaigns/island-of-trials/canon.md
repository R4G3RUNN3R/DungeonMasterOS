# Island of Trials - Canon register

## Hard canon currently confirmed

- The adventure is `Island of Trials` by Kreig Durham / `kreigd`.
- It is written for D&D 5e.
- The default source party is four level-5 characters.
- It is designed as a one-shot lasting a few hours.
- The player characters are whisked away to a mysterious island.
- The characters face an unknown test on the island.
- The adventure can be inserted into an existing campaign or used as a campaign jumping-off point.

## Hard secrecy rule

The public pitch deliberately leaves the nature of the test unknown. Until the PDF establishes when and how that information is revealed, the AI DM must not explain the test's purpose, architect, rules or expected solution simply to orient the players.

## Not canon yet

- island name beyond the adventure title
- transporter/summoner identity
- test creator or purpose
- locations beyond the island itself
- NPCs/factions
- creature roster
- number/order of trials
- exact win/fail conditions
- rewards
- exits/endings
