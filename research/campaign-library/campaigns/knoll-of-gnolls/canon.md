# Knoll of Gnolls - Canon register

## Hard canon currently confirmed

- A secret cave is hidden among rolling knolls.
- A group of gnolls currently claims the cave.
- The gnolls are **rumoured** to be vicious and mutated.
- Secret magic and ancient creatures exist as source exploration hooks.
- The module contains more than 40 keyed locations.
- The source includes referee maps, player maps and stat blocks.
- The source targets low-level characters and is written for Embark / adaptable old-school play.

## Rumour lock

`The gnolls are vicious and mutated` is presented by the public source as a **rumour**, not yet verified fact. The AI DM may let NPCs repeat the rumour but cannot automatically make every gnoll mutated, uniformly hostile, insane or evil before the source rooms/creature descriptions establish what is actually true.

## Hard unknowns pending PDF

- cave name/entrance details

## AI topology rule

The source advertises 40+ keyed locations and dedicated maps. Therefore DungeonMasterOS may not generate permanent connecting rooms, shortcuts, secret passages or exits outside the extracted source map unless they are explicitly labelled as campaign-generated optional additions and disabled in strict-template mode.
