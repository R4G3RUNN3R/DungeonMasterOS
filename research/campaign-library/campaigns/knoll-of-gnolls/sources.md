# Knoll of Gnolls - Sources and provenance

Accessed: 2026-08-17

## Primary creator page
URL: https://infinite-fractal.itch.io/knoll-of-gnolls

Supports:
- creator: Infinite Fractal
- low-level dungeon-delving module
- source system Embark, adaptable to old-school systems
- over 40 keyed locations
- secret cave among rolling knolls
- gnolls currently claim the cave and are rumoured to be vicious and mutated
- secret magics and ancient creatures are present as central exploration hooks
- labelled referee maps and unlabeled player maps
- dedicated stat-block reference page and repeated stat blocks at relevant rooms
- reference maps on every two-page spread
- **all game content CC BY-SA 4.0**
- art by Mariana Ruiz Villarreal (CC0 1.0), Oozjar (CC BY-SA 4.0), and public-domain sources
- no generative AI used
- two downloadable 31 MB PDFs (normal and spreads)

Authority: primary creator publication and explicit content-licence statement.

## Discovery/reception signal

A 2026 OSR itch collection (`Donjons OSR`) includes Knoll of Gnolls among curated items. The primary page currently shows a 5/5 rating from a single rating. These are weak curation signals only and provide no detailed actual-play evidence.

Exact-title Reddit/web searches in this pass did not locate a substantive session report or long review.

## Rights conclusion

The game content is unusually clear: CC BY-SA 4.0, explicitly inviting adaptation/remix/editing with attribution and ShareAlike. Asset-level art licences are separately declared, so DMOS can choose whether to reuse compatible art or create its own art while retaining source attribution.
