# The Zombraire's Estate

Status: `researching`

`The Zombraire's Estate` is a gothic/undead adventure by Russ Westbrook from `Adventure Anthology One`, current research edition **1st Edition Release 21**. The source is for **3-6 characters of levels 2-5** using the Basic Fantasy Role-Playing Game.

Long ago the Wrenwald wizard family ruled the nearby settlement harshly but protected it from the marsh. Lord Justin Wrenwald III insulted the swamp witch Julianne, who cursed the family and estate. Some died; others became undead; the estate itself became corrupted. Justin remains as the feared `zombraire`, an undead magic-user occupying the manor's hidden tower.

The source provides four distinct hooks: desperate family finances/golden eggs; a thieves/assassins' guild demanding undead cow milk; a healer seeking Lord Wrenwald II's buried Staff of Healing; or a religious order ordering the estate purged.

The estate reacts to noise. A loud fight in the garden or major graveyard disturbance can alert the Zombraire, who can release trained stirges as reinforcements/spies and will then be ready for later intruders. The estate also contains cursed undead plants, level-draining well sludge/milk, zombie livestock, fragile liquid-gold eggs, hidden manor chambers, a trained stirge flock, a cursed dryad and multiple tombs.

## Important DMOS design constraints
- Hook determines what counts as mission success; there is no single source-mandated reason to enter.
- Noise can alert the Zombraire and release/move stirges before the party reaches him.
- The Zombraire can speak but never surrenders and fights intelligently to the death.
- Luacia the dryad **believes** killing all undead will free her, but the source explicitly leaves whether that actually ends her curse to the GM. DMOS must not promote her belief into hard objective truth.
- Cursed food, milk, sludge and seeds have persistent transformation/energy-drain consequences.
- Several treasures are fragile, trapped, hidden or morally dubious to remove.
