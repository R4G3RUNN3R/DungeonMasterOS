# The Zombraire's Estate - Community Intelligence

## Leicester's Ramble actual play / mini-review (2022)
Source: https://leicestersramble.blogspot.com/2022/10/razing-zombraires-estate-plus-mini.html

- Used as a Halloween one-shot with five characters under B/X-OSE.
- GM reports the adventure ported from Basic Fantasy to B/X-OSE with no significant conversion.
- GM found AA1 material generally practical for one/two-session play.
- During play the GM accidentally transposed two manor rooms, demonstrating that room geography is easy to muddle when running from prose.

DMOS use:
- reinforces strict keyed-location/topology representation;
- supports future B/X-OSE conversion investigation but not automatic approved support.

## Cirsova campaign use (2015)
Source: https://cirsova.wordpress.com/2015/05/05/shadow-over-alfheim-pt-16b-the-zombraires-estate/

## Source-canon separation
None of the actual-play additions, GM improvisations, alternate character groups or conversion details become estate canon. Community reports are used only for pacing, usability, balance and conversion intelligence.
