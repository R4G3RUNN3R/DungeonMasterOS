## Actual-play / review sources

### Leicester's Ramble - 2022 Halloween one-shot
https://leicestersramble.blogspot.com/2022/10/razing-zombraires-estate-plus-mini.html

A five-character B/X-OSE game used the estate as a one-shot. The GM reports the Basic Fantasy adventure ported to B/X-OSE with no significant conversion and calls it easy to run even after not reading it for roughly two months. The report also notes an accidental room transposition during play, useful evidence that the manor's keyed topology must be explicit in DMOS.

### Cirsova - Shadow Over Alfheim campaign use, 2015
https://cirsova.wordpress.com/2015/05/05/shadow-over-alfheim-pt-16b-the-zombraires-estate/

Campaign actual play integrated the estate into a broader campaign. The GM reports it remained fun/easy enough to run despite having not reviewed it recently and notes accidentally swapping bedroom/kitchen placement, again supporting hard map/topology locks.

### Living 4 Crits - DCC family game, 2016
https://ilive4crits.blogspot.com/2016/02/dccrpg-judge-carries-devastation.html

The adventure was run using Dungeon Crawl Classics rules by a family group. This is conversion/portability evidence only and does not establish DCC as source-native or QA-approved support.
