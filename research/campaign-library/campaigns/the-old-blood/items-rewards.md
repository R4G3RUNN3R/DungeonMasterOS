# The Old Blood - Important Items, Evidence and Rewards

## Whispering Blade of Blood
- Type: significant weapon/relic associated with Edric
- Initial location: Sealed Crypt if Edric has not awakened
- State dependency: can be absent/claimed once Edric awakens depending on sequence
- Plot relevance: physical evidence/relic of Edric's old power; interacts with his blood-linked history
- Rules implementation: mechanical stat block should come from source/native adapter QA rather than being guessed.

## Ceremonial Chalice of Saint Arlen
- Location: ruined chapel area within Edric's keep
- Appearance: silver ceremonial chalice with small rubies
- Interaction:
  - respectful offering at altar can grant a source-defined temporary boon to the first qualifying PC
  - removing/desecrating the chalice/altar invokes Saint Arlen's curse
  - curse can be resolved through return, sincere offering/repentance and appropriate religious action
- Source value: 20 gold
- DMOS role: optional risk/reward interaction; not required for central mystery

## Victor's distillation coil
- Type: mundane alchemical equipment / clue
- Normal expected location: Apothecary
- Actual state: moved to Secret Laboratory
- Clue logic:
  - conspicuous empty space/scuff marks in shop
  - Victor claims it broke and he awaits a replacement
  - glassworker can contradict this by confirming no replacement was ordered
- Plot critical: supporting evidence, not sole proof

## Victor's ritual dagger
- Type: ritual implement / clue
- Provenance: recovered/found in Ruins and sold/provided to Victor through local commerce
- Investigation value: unusual purchase for an ordinary alchemist; points from Victor toward older Brannam history

## Victor's notes
- Location: Secret Laboratory
- Content themes: blood research, grief, doubt and repeated references to a `Voice from Below`
- Plot value: high-confidence bridge between murder mystery and deeper alien influence
- Reveal lock: only after legitimate lab discovery or equivalent evidence access

## Evelyn Marlowe's body
- Type: corpse / critical evidence, not loot
- Original state/location: buried in Graveyard
- Current state/location: exhumed and hidden in Secret Laboratory
- Plot value: explains Victor's obsession and confirms grave desecration/hidden operation

## Sealed Ledger
- Type: illicit records / faction quest item
- Associated faction: Smugglers / Devin Crowl
- Reward path: returning it can earn a favour/boon
- Authority path: Brannam authorities are not especially interested, but outside/regional authorities may be
- DMOS role: optional factional side objective

## King Below warning tablet / Ruins relic
- Location: source extraction places relevant warning material with/near Chapel caretaker resources
- Content: warns/references the King Below
- Plot value: historical clue toward pre-Edric source of corruption
- QA note: exact wording/location must be verified before final approval.

## Blood Rite instructions/book
- Type: ritual knowledge
- Function: enables Sacrificial Blood Rite resolution if players discover and understand it
- Plot critical: one of two long-term Brannam resolution paths
- AI rule: do not expose exact ritual procedure until discovery conditions are met

## Ordinary loot/equipment
The Ruins, Sewer bodies, abandoned commercial buildings and crypts can contain ordinary treasure and useful gear. Only source-keyed plot-critical items belong in the campaign's canonical item list. Random mundane loot should be generated through the appropriate ruleset/treasure tables without overriding keyed contents.
