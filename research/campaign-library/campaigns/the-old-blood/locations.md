# The Old Blood - Location Register

The source presents Brannam as a city-scale investigation with keyed districts plus three major dungeon areas. Ordinary buildings may be improvised inside a district; keyed clues and dungeon relationships may not.

## Brannam
- Type: decaying city / campaign hub
- Region: Aulach River trade route
- Historical peak: approximately 23,000 residents
- Current state: severely depopulated, large abandoned districts, civic decline
- Connections: city districts, Graveyard, Sewers, Ruins, surrounding wilderness
- Dynamic state: `collapseStage`

## Eastward
- Type: inhabited district
- Character: densest remaining population; narrow leaning buildings; ordinary civic life barely surviving
- Day danger: Unsafe
- Night danger: Unsafe
- Key sites:
  - Common Well
  - Mayor's Office / Guard base
  - Warded Lantern
  - Broken Hearth
  - Rillek Harst residence
  - ordinary shops including cobbler
- Key NPCs: Mayor Elana Fost, Captain Ren Halvard, Feyra Lrowyn, Rillek Harst (when present)
- Clue: Feyra knows regional history and can connect the name `King Below` with a tale involving a Flooded Shrine.
- Collapse behaviour: lodging/services can close; mayor may stop seeing visitors at later collapse stage.

## Crowmarket
- Type: inhabited market district
- Character: last major commercial heart of Brannam; desperation mixed with salvage trade
- Day danger: Unsafe
- Night danger: Risky
- Key sites:
  - Belltower
  - Victor Marlowe's Apothecary
  - butcher
  - fishmonger
  - herbalist
  - pawnbroker
  - courier activity
- Key NPCs: Victor Marlowe, Jorik Stann, Grella Fenn, Janna and other merchants
- Evidence threads:
  - Victor's missing distillation coil
  - Victor's unusual requests and recent purchases
  - animal blood/fats supplied for his earlier work
  - ritual dagger obtained from material recovered in the Ruins
- Dynamic: Belltower becomes part of Edric's Heralds of Return if Edric begins feeding.

## Tradesman's Ward
- Type: working district
- Key sites:
  - Ulric's Smithy
  - Glassblower
  - Rusted Tankard
  - Weaver's Guildhall
- Key NPCs: Ulric Tarn, Mira Tarn, Tira/glassblower, Calla Bryne, Nerris Velth, Cassard Vole, Dren Varick
- Evidence:
  - glassblower can contradict Victor's claim that his missing distillation coil is awaiting replacement
  - Victor requested unusual/specialised laboratory glassware
  - guild/weavers have evidence of large dark-cloth orders for allegedly delicate laboratory work

## Gardenrow
- Type: decayed former aristocratic district
- Character: overgrown estates and the surviving chapel
- Day danger: Unsafe
- Night danger: Risky
- Key sites:
  - Saint Arlen's Chapel
  - Caretaker's Cottage
  - Old Fountain
  - exterior Catacomb grate
- Key NPCs: Father Brenwick, Lisbet Harrow
- Clues:
  - Father Brenwick officiated Evelyn Marlowe's funeral
  - clergy traditions mention former rulers worshipping a blood-associated power
  - the caretaker cottage contains/references material recovered from the Ruins, including a warning about the King Below
- Dynamic:
  - Chapel locks at sundown during later collapse
  - Old Fountain can fill with black oily sludge at severe collapse
  - Edric relocates to Catacombs below if awakened

## Graveyard
- Type: cemetery outside/east of the main inhabited wall
- Day danger: Safe
- Night danger: Deadly
- Key graves:
  - Marya Dolen
  - Harrin Velth
  - Evelyn Marlowe
- Core evidence: Evelyn's grave is empty because Victor moved her body to his Secret Laboratory.
- Night hazard: skeleton activity according to source.

## Annex
- Type: abandoned district
- Key sites:
  - abandoned barracks
  - derelict watchtower
  - Victor's Secret Laboratory
- Secret Laboratory:
  - concealed access to sewers
  - contains Evelyn's exhumed body
  - contains moved alchemical equipment including the missing shop equipment
  - contains delicate supplies/equipment and Victor's notes
  - notes refer to a `Voice from Below` and Victor's wavering belief that its guidance must be correct
- Derelict Watchtower: gives some visibility; Victor's movement/light can potentially be noticed at night.

## Highroad
- Type: largely abandoned former commercial artery
- Character: warehouses and merchant buildings from Brannam's prosperous period
- Day danger: Unsafe
- Night danger: Risky
- Key sites:
  - Old Counting House
  - smugglers' operations/warehouse
- Faction: smugglers led/represented by Devin Crowl
- State: smugglers are protective of their business but not automatically hostile.

## Northgate
- Type: abandoned district
- Relevance: Victor used a derelict residence here to complete the murders of Marya Dolen and Harrin Velth after ambushing them in/near Crowmarket.
- Investigation value: potential crime-scene traces connecting initial victims to Victor's alchemy rather than Edric.

# Dungeon/network locations

## Brannam Sewers
- Type: city-wide understructure / investigation and chase network
- Condition: much of network inaccessible through rubble, collapse and standing water
- Functions:
  - Victor's concealed escape system
  - route between Apothecary, Secret Laboratory and abandoned districts
  - access toward Sealed Crypt due to erosion/collapse
  - environmental/monster encounters
  - clues connected to King Below and older infrastructure
- Example source hazards/entities include sewer gas, alchemical traps, giant/mutated rats, skeletons, shadows, ghouls, Cave Creeper, Crawling Mass, Weeping Worm, Lurker and strange King Below carvings.

## Victor's Apothecary sewer hatch
- Hidden beneath/in rear of shop
- Constructed by Victor
- Opens into sewer network
- Investigation clues include scuffing, construction irregularities and hidden latch architecture

## Secret Laboratory sewer hatch
- Concealed ladder/hatch connecting Annex laboratory to sewers
- Gives Victor a second escape route

## Ruined Outflow Passage
- Victor's intended escape route toward the swamp/outside Brannam
- Critical branch point: in panic Victor can take the wrong route and enter the Sealed Crypt instead

## Sealed Crypt
- Type: von Braech family crypt beneath ruined keep
- Initial state: Edric dormant
- Access: formerly sealed by destruction of keep; age/water erosion created sewer breach
- Contents/state notes:
  - Edric's resting place
  - Whispering Blade of Blood can be present if Edric has not awakened
  - if Victor awakens Edric, Victor's corpse can later be found here
  - collapsed stairs once led toward keep
- Hard transition: breaching this location awakens Edric unless state already changed.

## Saint Arlen's Catacombs
- Type: multi-level crypt complex beneath Chapel
- Access:
  - interior spiral stair from Chapel
  - exterior locked grate
- Initial danger: relatively safe upper levels
- Awakened-Edric state: danger increases; Edric uses Catacombs as refuge/lair
- Content:
  - named sarcophagi of former prominent residents and clergy
  - older lower levels
  - collapsed halls
  - ancient stairs and natural/clawed tunnels
  - lower cavern/pool/chitinous alien structure leading toward the deeper ancient influence
- Connection: thematic and physical descent from civic religion into pre-human/alien substrate.

## Ruins of Edric von Braech's Keep
- Type: cursed ruined fortress / dungeon
- Surface: ruined entry, former throne room, chapel and remnants of Edric's rule
- Notable elements surfaced in extraction:
  - damaged murals/history
  - ruined chapel/altar associated with Saint Arlen
  - Ceremonial Chalice with boon/curse interaction
  - storerooms/armour/weapons
  - secret stairway into deeper hidden areas
  - ritual supplies and old material associated with Edric's reign
- Connection: Sealed Crypt beneath; deeper historical records/clues may point toward rites and Flooded Shrine.

## Flooded Shrine
- Type: ancient alien biological device / final causal location
- Association: King Below
- Environment: ancient flooded/chitinous/organic architecture beneath later human structures
- Effects:
  - psychic influence on susceptible minds
  - corruption of arcane magic in Brannam
  - source of Edric's transformation and Victor's manipulation
  - destruction causes violent structural/environmental reaction but ends local influence
- Resolution interactions:
  - destroy shrine
  - perform/restore Sacrificial Blood Rite
  - leave intact
- Hard canon: it is not merely an ordinary religious altar and its purpose is not fully knowable.
