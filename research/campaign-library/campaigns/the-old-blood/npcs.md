# The Old Blood - NPC Register

## Victor Marlowe
- Role: initial murderer; alchemist/apothecary; tragic antagonist
- Home/work: Crowmarket apothecary
- Hidden location: Secret Laboratory in Annex
- Public reputation: trusted community alchemist who supplied remedies and useful chemical solutions
- Public behaviour: initially polite/helpful; encourages caution about the supposed vampire threat
- Private goal: restore his dead wife Evelyn through blood-based alchemy and atone for failing to save her
- Manipulation: unknowingly influenced by Flooded Shrine / Voice from Below
- Victims: Marya Dolen and Harrin Velth
- Methods: alchemical paralysis, blood siphoning, hidden laboratory, sewer escape routes
- Evidence:
  - missing distillation coil/equipment
  - unusual glassware/cloth/tool orders
  - animal blood experiments
  - ritual dagger purchase
  - hidden shop hatch
  - exhumed Evelyn
  - notes about the Voice from Below
- Escape state: if exposed, flees via sewers. He intends to leave Brannam, but can take a wrong turn into Sealed Crypt.
- Edric interaction: if Victor reaches dormant Edric, awakening follows and Edric kills Victor.
- AI rule: Victor is not a cackling cultist. His core is grief, obsession, rationalisation and psychic manipulation.

## Evelyn Marlowe
- Role: Victor's dead wife; motive/campaign evidence
- State: dead before campaign begins
- Funeral: officiated by Father Brenwick
- Grave: secretly exhumed and therefore empty
- Current location: Victor's Secret Laboratory
- AI rule: do not invent resurrection success before source-supported ending/adaptation work.

## Edric von Braech
- Role: historical tyrant, vampire, conditional second major antagonist
- Initial state: dormant in Sealed Crypt
- History: conquered Brannam; poor/violent rule accelerated decline; discovered Flooded Shrine and performed blood rites; became vampire; later overthrown and trapped beneath ruined keep
- Initial awakened state: severely weakened
- Goal after awakening: escape immediate danger, relocate to Catacombs, feed, regain restored state, retake Brannam and rebuild keep
- Temperament: historically haughty/arrogant; his previous downfall has made him more cautious about underestimating foes
- Restoration: feeding advances his supernatural return; source includes herald sequence
- Final consequence if ignored: restores fortress, enslaves survivors for labour/blood, grows into regional threat
- Special item: Whispering Blade of Blood associated with him / Sealed Crypt
- AI rule: Edric is not responsible for the initial murders.

## The Forgotten
- Role: independent undead danger / possible murderer
- Origin: turned by Edric in the past, starved/maddened
- Relationship to Edric: not reliably controllable by Edric
- Function: can participate in night murder checks while active
- AI rule: corpse/evidence pattern differs from Victor and Edric; do not merge them into one vampire faction.

## Father Brenwick
- Role: priest of Saint Arlen's Chapel; historical/religious clue source
- Location: Gardenrow / Saint Arlen's Chapel
- Public goal: protect frightened residents and preserve what remains of sacred order
- Knowledge:

- False rumour about him: residents may claim he previously banished the vampire; source marks this false
- Dynamic: chapel security/closure escalates as panic worsens

# AI-DM NPC policy

NPCs only know what their source role supports. The AI must not distribute omniscient clues across the cast. When a source detail remains unresolved, it should be represented as unknown rather than filled from genre convention. NPCs may change attitude and strategy in response to player actions and collapse state, but core identities, motives and factual knowledge are canon-locked.
