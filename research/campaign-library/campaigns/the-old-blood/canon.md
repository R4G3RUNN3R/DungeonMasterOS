# The Old Blood - Canon Locks

## Hard canon

1. Brannam is a declining former trade city on the Aulach River, with large abandoned districts and a much smaller surviving population.
2. Edric von Braech conquered Brannam roughly two centuries before the present and later became a vampire through the Flooded Shrine's influence and blood rites.
3. Edric's keep was destroyed in an uprising; he begins the campaign dormant in the Sealed Crypt unless a prior state explicitly says otherwise.
4. Marya Dolen and Harrin Velth are the two initial human murder victims.
5. Victor Marlowe killed the initial victims. Edric did not.
6. Victor is a local alchemist/apothecary and a previously trusted member of Brannam's remaining community.
7. Victor's wife Evelyn died approximately one month before the campaign. He secretly exhumed her and moved her body to his Secret Laboratory.
8. Victor believes blood-based alchemy can restore Evelyn. His thinking is being distorted by the Flooded Shrine.
9. The Flooded Shrine is an ancient alien biological device associated with entities earlier rulers called the King Below. Its underlying purpose is not comprehensible to ordinary humans.
10. The Flooded Shrine influenced Edric's transformation and manipulates Victor.
11. Victor deliberately benefits from the city's old vampire fears as misdirection.
12. If exposed and able to flee, Victor can use hidden sewer access from his apothecary or laboratory. His intended goal is escape, not awakening Edric.
13. Victor can accidentally enter the Sealed Crypt while fleeing. If he reaches it while Edric is dormant, Edric awakens and kills Victor.
14. PCs can independently breach the Sealed Crypt and awaken Edric.
15. If awakened, Edric is initially weakened and seeks to escape rather than obligingly fight to the death.
16. Edric relocates to the Catacombs beneath Saint Arlen's Chapel and feeds to restore his power.
17. Edric's final strategic goal is to regain his restored state, dominate Brannam and rebuild his keep.
18. Killing/stopping Victor does not destroy the Flooded Shrine or restore Brannam.
19. Defeating Edric does not by itself resolve the Flooded Shrine's influence or restore Brannam.
20. Destroying the Flooded Shrine breaks its local influence but does not restore Brannam's former prosperity.
21. Restoring the Sacrificial Blood Rite can restore Brannam's prosperity only while recurring annual sacrifice continues; the ritual leader becomes marked/beacon-like to the alien influence.
22. Leaving an awakened Edric unresolved allows him to restore himself, enslave survivors and become a wider regional threat.
23. Rumours in Brannam are deliberately mixed true/false/partially true. The AI must not convert rumours into facts merely because an NPC says them.
24. The adventure is open-ended. No single scene sequence is mandatory.

## Conditional canon

### Victor exposed
If evidence conclusively points to Victor and he is confronted while free, he prioritises escape. Whether he reaches the crypt depends on player action and route state.

### Edric awakens
If the Sealed Crypt is breached while Edric is dormant, set `edricState=awakened_weakened`. If Victor is present, Victor is killed unless the party intervenes in a source-consistent way before Edric can do so.

### Edric feeds
Each successful Edric feeding advances his restoration and triggers the corresponding Herald of Return consequence.

### Nightly murder check
While Victor, Edric or the Forgotten are active and capable of killing, night can produce another murder. The selected murderer controls the corpse pattern and associated evidence.

### Collapse progression
Increasing murders and decay events advance Brannam's panic/collapse state. NPC and location behaviour changes with collapse.

### Shrine destroyed
If the Flooded Shrine is destroyed, its psychic influence ends locally and an active Edric loses shrine-dependent advantages. Brannam remains materially decayed.

### Blood rite restored
If the Sacrificial Blood Rite is successfully restored, Brannam begins returning toward prosperity while the rite is maintained, at the stated annual sacrificial cost.

## Soft canon / presentation freedom
The AI may improvise:
- incidental residents and unnamed businesses within districts
- weather and street-level sensory details consistent with the district
- exact phrasing of rumours and NPC dialogue
- minor possessions and ordinary background objects
- routes through non-keyed ordinary streets, provided district geography is not contradicted
- social reactions that follow established NPC goals and collapse stage

## Prohibited improvisation
The AI may not:
- change the initial killer from Victor
- make Evelyn secretly alive at campaign start
- make Edric awake without a valid state transition
- invent a benign purpose for the Flooded Shrine
- make destroying Edric automatically save/restore Brannam
- remove the moral cost of the blood-rite ending
- invent a guaranteed clue that bypasses the investigation without source/adaptation annotation
- invent a new major faction that controls Brannam
- make Father Brenwick knowingly responsible for Victor's murders
- make rumours automatically true
- protect plot NPCs from player consequences merely because they appear later; use conditional branches instead
