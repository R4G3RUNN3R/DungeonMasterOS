# The Old Blood - Factions and Collective Actors

## Brannam Residents
- Type: fragmented civilian population
- Goal: survive, preserve homes and explain the murders
- Resources: local knowledge, rumours, neighbourhood networks, mobs when panic escalates
- Initial stance toward outsiders: mixed; fear can shift rapidly toward suspicion
- Escalation:
  - early rumours and vampire panic
  - curfew pressure
  - families flee
  - scapegoating/mobs
  - vigilantism, fires and barricades
- AI rule: Residents are not one hive mind. Generate individuals with differing fears while keeping mass behaviour tied to collapse stage.

## Brannam Guards
- Leader: Captain Ren Halvard
- Civic authority: Mayor Elana Fost
- Base: Mayor's Office, Eastward
- Goal: maintain public order with inadequate capacity
- Resources: patrols, questioning, curfew enforcement, attempted recovery of stolen/seized property
- Weakness: fear and institutional decline; patrols may focus on outsiders/scapegoats instead of the real cause
- AI rule: Guards only act on evidence and public pressure they actually possess.

## Saint Arlen's Chapel
- Principal NPC: Father Brenwick
- Supporting NPC: Lisbet Harrow
- Goal: offer sanctuary/religious stability and protect dangerous lower crypt spaces
- Resources: Chapel, traditions, clergy history, access to Catacombs, religious knowledge, minor relics
- Escalation: closes/locks more aggressively as collapse deepens
- Relationship to Edric: historic opposition by tradition, but source rumours exaggerate Brenwick's personal role
- AI rule: church lore can point toward blood worship/older history but does not confer omniscience.

## Crowmarket Merchants / Tradespeople
- Type: loose economic network, not a unified political faction
- Goal: keep commerce functioning and survive Brannam's collapse
- Members/contacts include Victor, Jorik Stann, Grella Fenn, glassworker/tradespeople and guild members
- Value to investigation: distributed evidence about Victor's unusual recent purchases/orders
- Dynamic: shops close or fortify as panic increases

## Smugglers
- Principal contact: Devin Crowl
- Other contact: Janna
- Base/activity: Highroad and Crowmarket observation/courier network
- Goal: protect illegal trade and avoid unnecessary authority attention
- Stance: not automatically hostile to PCs; dislike investigation into their operations
- Resources: routes, information, warehouse, contacts
- Hook: Sealed Ledger can be returned for a favour/boon; outside authorities may value it even when Brannam authorities do not

## Victor's operation
- Type: one-man hidden murder/alchemy operation, not a conventional cult
- Leader/member: Victor Marlowe
- Sites: Apothecary, Secret Laboratory, sewer routes
- Goal: restore Evelyn
- External controller: Flooded Shrine psychic influence, which Victor does not understand as a normal faction relationship
- AI rule: do not invent cult followers unless a later source section explicitly establishes them.

## Edric's returning dominion
- Type: dormant/conditional faction state rather than active starting faction
- Leader: Edric von Braech
- Start: nonexistent as an organised force while Edric sleeps
- If Edric restores:
  - subjugates Brannam
  - uses remaining residents for blood and labour
  - rebuilds keep
  - becomes regional threat
- AI rule: this faction only exists after the correct awakening/restoration transitions.

## The King Below / Flooded Shrine influence
- Type: alien influence, not a human faction
- Known interface: Flooded Shrine; psychic whispers; blood rites; incomprehensible agenda
- Known manipulated figures: historically Edric; currently Victor
- Goal: explicitly not humanly understood in the source
- AI rule: never invent a neat evil-god objective merely to make the plot easier to explain.
