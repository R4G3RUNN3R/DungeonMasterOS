## Sacrificial Blood Rite verification
- The surviving ritual instructions are found in an intact book in the **Library on the second floor of the Ruins**.
- The rite is a genuine long-term restoration path for Brannam, not merely a combat buff or one-time spell.
- The procedure requires strict adherence to the source ritual, a suitable living victim and a ritual leader.
- The first attempt includes a native intelligence challenge under the source rules.
- If successfully restored, the rite must be repeated annually.
- The ritual leader acquires alien consequences/attention associated with the deeper power.
- DMOS may summarize these facts as campaign state, but native Shadowdark-facing mechanics remain in the rules adapter rather than being relicensed as CC0 campaign mechanics.

## Whispering Blade verification
The source establishes it as Edric-associated blood magic, initially tied to the Sealed Crypt/Edric state. It has initiative/blood-upkeep behaviour and a source-defined destruction interaction with the Flooded Shrine. DMOS stores those dependencies and delegates exact numerical mechanics to the Shadowdark adapter.
