# The Old Blood - Encounters, Threats and Clocks

This campaign is not a combat sequence. Encounters exist inside a live investigation with time pressure and multiple active threats.

## Campaign clocks

### `murderCount`
Advances when an active murderer successfully kills during a nightly check or a source-defined event.

Possible active murderers:
- Victor Marlowe
- Edric von Braech, after awakening
- the Forgotten, while active

The corpse/evidence pattern depends on the murderer. Do not render every victim as a neat vampire-drained corpse.

### `collapseStage`
Tracks Brannam's civic deterioration. Murder totals are a major input but decay events can also matter.

Consequences include:
- increased guard activity
- outsider suspicion
- curfew
- residents fleeing
- shops closing
- mobs and scapegoating
- barricades/fires
- institutional collapse
- infrastructure failure

### `edricRestoration`
Begins only when Edric awakens. Each successful feeding moves him toward restored power and triggers Heralds of Return.

## Investigation/social encounter families

### Initial murder investigation
Objectives:
- examine victim pattern and rumours
- learn Brannam/Edric history
- identify contradictions in vampire theory
- build leads toward Victor

Fail-forward:
Missing one clue must not halt the campaign. The source deliberately distributes information through multiple residents, shops, history and physical evidence.

### Victor's Apothecary
Initial posture: ordinary/helpful professional.
Possible player actions:
- ask about vampire attacks
- inspect work area
- question missing equipment
- buy/request remedies
- confront Victor
- secretly search for sewer access
- stake out/follow him

Escalation depends on evidence and Victor's suspicion. He should not flee simply because the PCs enter his shop.

### Tradespeople interviews
Key purpose: corroborate Victor's unusual recent activity.
Potential evidence sources:
- glassware/distillation coil contradiction
- dark cloth orders
- animal blood/bone/fat supplies
- unusual tool/grate orders
- ritual dagger provenance

### Graveyard investigation
Possible discoveries:
- recent victims' graves
- Evelyn's empty grave
- night undead encounter
- links to Father Brenwick/funeral records

### Secret Laboratory discovery
High-value reveal scene.
Required facts once legitimately found:
- Evelyn's body
- transferred/missing laboratory equipment
- Victor's hidden operation
- notes indicating a `Voice from Below`
- concealed sewer access

This can solve Victor's guilt before the Flooded Shrine is understood.

## Chase branch - Victor exposed
Trigger: Victor is conclusively exposed/confronted and retains opportunity to flee.
Route:
Apothecary or Secret Laboratory -> hidden sewer route -> attempt toward Ruined Outflow Passage.

Branch outcomes:
- PCs stop/capture/kill Victor before crypt: Edric stays dormant.
- Victor evades PCs and finds correct way out: Victor escapes Brannam; future murders/state depend on campaign continuation.
- Victor takes wrong route into Sealed Crypt: Edric wakes and kills him.
- PCs enter Sealed Crypt first/during pursuit: Edric wakes independently.

No railroaded chase outcome is allowed.

## Edric awakened encounter loop

### Initial awakening
Edric is weakened and survival-focused. His sensible objective is escape from an immediate superior threat, not a boss-fight pose.

### Catacomb refuge
Edric relocates to Saint Arlen's Catacombs. Players can:
- investigate the Chapel
- negotiate with/pressure Brenwick for access
- use exterior grate
- follow signs/heralds
- descend deeper and potentially discover older alien structures

### Feeding cycle
If players delay, Edric feeds. Every successful kill advances restoration and produces increasingly overt supernatural signs.

### Confronting restored Edric
If allowed to recover, Edric is substantially more dangerous and oriented toward reclaiming Brannam.

Fail-forward:
Retreat is valid. Edric's future conquest becomes world state rather than an artificial game-over screen.

## Ruins encounters
The Ruins combine historical investigation, undead/monster danger, relics and access toward Edric's deeper history. Exact room-by-room encounter assignments remain a final-source-QA item; do not randomly distribute every Ruins table result into keyed rooms.

## Flooded Shrine resolution encounter
This is a causal/moral climax rather than necessarily a conventional boss fight.

Player approaches can include:
- investigation/alien revelation
- destruction of shrine
- attempt to perform/restore Sacrificial Blood Rite
- withdrawal/leave intact

### Destroy shrine
The organic/alien structure reacts violently; collapse/environmental danger accompanies destruction. Local alien influence ends. Brannam remains decayed.

### Blood rite
Success restores Brannam's long-term prosperity while annual sacrifice continues and marks the ritual leader as a beacon to the alien influence.

### Refuse both
Brannam remains exposed to the shrine's influence unless campaign state later changes.

## Suggested-choice policy
For each scene, DMOS should generate 3-5 contextually sensible options from current known facts, never spoilers. Free text remains valid. Example at Victor's shop before suspicion:
1. Ask Victor what he knows about the victims.
2. Browse or purchase ordinary remedies while observing the shop.
3. Ask whether he has treated unusual animal wounds or blood loss recently.
4. Leave and question nearby merchants.
5. Quietly watch who enters/leaves the shop.

`Search his secret laboratory` must not appear until the PCs have actually learned or inferred that such a place exists.
