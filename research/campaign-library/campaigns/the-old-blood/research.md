# The Old Blood - Source-Derived Campaign Research

## Historical timeline

### ~350 years before present
Brannam is founded on the Aulach River and grows from a trade post into a major commercial centre. At its height the population is approximately 23,000.

### ~200 years before present
Edric von Braech conquers Brannam. Trade recovers briefly, but Edric proves a destructive ruler: high taxation, military spending, neglected civic maintenance and conflict with neighbours drive decline.

### ~190 years before present
Edric discovers records about the Flooded Shrine and the Sacrificial Blood Rites. He reaches the buried shrine, communes with its alien influence, performs the rite and becomes a vampire. His reign becomes openly horrific.

### Fall of Edric
Brannam eventually rises against Edric. His keep is destroyed. Edric becomes trapped/dormant in the Sealed Crypt beneath the ruins, sustained over generations by the Flooded Shrine.

### One month before present
Evelyn Marlowe, Victor Marlowe's wife, dies. Victor's grief and prior exposure to the Flooded Shrine's influence make him vulnerable to its psychic manipulation. He eventually exhumes Evelyn and moves her body to a hidden laboratory in the Annex.

### Immediately before campaign
Victor concludes that human blood contains the key to restoring Evelyn. He murders Marya Dolen and Harrin Velth after paralyzing them with alchemy, siphons their blood, and deliberately allows the puncture wounds and blood loss to resemble vampire killings. Their bodies are buried in the Graveyard while rumours of Edric's return spread.

## Campaign start condition
Brannam is already frightened by the discovery of two blood-drained victims. Victor remains a trusted community alchemist. Edric is still dormant unless something has already breached the Sealed Crypt. The Flooded Shrine remains active and hidden.

## Investigation layers

### Layer 1 - The apparent vampire murders
Players can investigate victims, rumours, the Graveyard, the Crowmarket, Victor's shop and local history. Most residents naturally interpret the puncture wounds through Brannam's memory of Edric.

### Layer 2 - Victor's inconsistencies
Potential threads include:
- Victor's missing distillation coil, which he falsely claims is broken/replacement-pending.
- Tira the glassblower can contradict the replacement story.
- Victor recently commissioned unusual dark cloth and specialised equipment.
- Jorik Stann supplied animal blood/fats/bones and knows Victor had been experimenting before the human murders.
- Victor obtained a ritual dagger recovered from the Ruins, which is an odd purchase for an ordinary apothecary.
- Victor has constructed a hidden sewer escape under the apothecary.
- Evelyn's grave is empty.
- Victor's Secret Laboratory contains Evelyn's corpse, transferred equipment, supplies and notes referring to a 'Voice from Below'.

### Layer 3 - The escape/chase
If Victor is exposed and can flee, he uses the hidden sewer route. His intended goal is simply to escape Brannam through the Ruined Outflow Passage. In panic he can take a wrong turn into the Sealed Crypt.

This transition is conditional, not mandatory. PCs can stop him before the crypt. If they do, Edric remains asleep.

### Layer 4 - Edric awakened
If Victor or the PCs breach the Sealed Crypt, Edric wakes in a severely weakened state. If Victor reaches him, Edric kills Victor. Edric tries to avoid an immediate decisive fight and relocates to the Catacombs beneath Saint Arlen's Chapel.

Edric's overriding goal becomes feeding and regaining his restored state. His growing influence is signalled by a sequence of supernatural heralds tied to his kills.

### Layer 5 - The ancient cause
The Flooded Shrine is older than Edric and is the root corrupting influence. It is an alien biological device tied to beings earlier rulers called the King Below. Its purpose is not humanly comprehensible. It exerts psychic influence, corrupted Edric and now manipulates Victor.

The source intentionally allows the party to finish the murder mystery without necessarily discovering the shrine. That is a valid partial success, not a continuity error.

## Brannam panic/collapse engine

A murder check occurs each night while any eligible killer remains active. The active pool can include Victor, Edric and the Forgotten.

Broad collapse progression from source:
- Stage 1: first murder; vampire rumours gain traction; guards increase patrols and harass outsiders.
- Stage 2: roughly 2-3 murders; fear takes hold; poorly enforced curfew; families begin leaving.
- Stage 3: roughly 4-5 murders; mobs seek scapegoats; more people flee; businesses close; some NPC/location behaviours change.
- Stage 4: roughly 6-7 murders; civic order is close to breaking; public unrest and merchant shutdowns intensify.
- Later collapse: increasingly severe decay events, disappearances, fires, vigilantism, infrastructure failure and institutional withdrawal.

The DMOS graph should track `murderCount` and `collapseStage` separately so non-murder decay events can still move the city toward failure where the source allows.

## Edric herald sequence
Edric's feeding produces escalating supernatural signs:
1. After first kill, the Belltower rings at midnight without anyone pulling it.
2. Dogs howl and whimper at night, even where dogs are absent.
3. Lingering morning cold and abnormal candle behaviour appears in Saint Arlen's Chapel.
4. Old depictions of Brannam's past begin weeping dark oily blood, especially at the Chapel.
5. Residents wake weak/drained with missing memories.
6. Unnaturally large flocks of ravens gather and watch the city.

These should be consequence scenes, not arbitrary atmosphere. The AI must tie them to Edric's feeding state.

## Major resolution logic

### Victor stopped, Edric dormant
Murders cease. The immediate mystery is solved. The Flooded Shrine quiets but remains. Brannam stays cursed/stagnant. This is a valid ending or a pause point for later return.

### Edric awakened and defeated
This removes the vampire threat but still does not repair Brannam's deeper condition while the Flooded Shrine remains.

### PCs leave after Edric wakes
Edric eventually regains his full power, rebuilds his former fortress, enslaves remaining residents for labour/blood and becomes a larger regional threat. This is a legitimate failure/consequence ending and a future-campaign seed.

### Destroy the Flooded Shrine
The King Below's local influence is broken and Edric is weakened if still active. Brannam is not magically restored; it remains a declining city and may eventually fade away. This is the cleanest moral ending but not a fairy-tale restoration.

### Resume the Sacrificial Blood Rite
Regular annual sacrifice restores Brannam's former prosperity while the rite continues. The ritual leader becomes a beacon to the alien influence. This is a materially successful but morally catastrophic ending.

## AI-DM implications
- Never reveal Victor as killer merely because PCs ask about a vampire.
- Never wake Edric on a timer. Awakening is state/route dependent.
- Never require the Flooded Shrine to be discovered for the Victor mystery to count as solved.
- Do not restore Brannam simply because Edric dies.
- Do not turn the Flooded Shrine into a conventional demon altar. Its alien/biological incomprehensibility is hard canon.
- Preserve the difference between rumours and truths.
- Preserve the possibility of retreat, abandonment and long-term consequences.
- Treat city deterioration, murders and Edric's restoration as clocks driven by player delay and active threats.
