# Merilla's Magic Tower - Release 21 Mechanics Sheet

Use only with **Adventure Anthology One, 1st Edition Release 21**. This file exists so old standalone-release mechanics are never silently mixed into current play.

## Entry ward
- Trigger: attempt to read east-door runes.
- Fireball: 10d6 damage; save vs. Spells for half.

## Bronze Golem
- AC 20.
- HD 20** (+13).
- Attacks: 1 fist + special.
- Damage: 3d10 + special.
- Move: 80' (10').
- Save: Fighter 10.
- Morale: 12.
- XP: 5,650.
- HP: 60.
- Special: +1d10 heat damage on its successful hit; an attacker that hits it suffers 2d6 molten-metal damage.

## Carpet Trapper
- AC 12.
- HD 7.
- Attacks: 1.
- Damage: 1d8/round while squeezing.
- Move: 0'.
- Save: Fighter 7.
- Morale: 12.
- XP: 670.
- HP: 34.
- Centre-square trigger knocks victim prone and rolls them up.
- Trapped victim suffers 1 damage per 4 damage inflicted on the creature.
- Dispel magic deals 1d6 per caster level to construct; save vs. Spells for half; this dispel damage does not transfer to the trapped victim.

## Rust Monsters (2)
- AC 18.
- HD 5*.
- Attack: 1 rust-metal attack.
- Direct damage: 0; rusts metal.
- Move: 40'.
- Save: Fighter 5.
- Morale: 6.
- XP: 405 each.
- HP: 32 and 28.

## Level 2 fireball chests
- North chest: 15,000 gp; 10d6 fireball; save vs. Spells half.
- Southwest chest: 15,000 gp; 10d6 fireball; save vs. Spells half.
- Dispel magic disables trap.

## Morphganger
- Initial disguise: southeast chest.
- AC 16.
- HD 10 (+9).
- Attacks: 1.
- Damage: 1d12.
- Move: 10'.
- Save: Fighter 5.
- Morale: 8.
- XP: 1,300.
- HP: 60.
- Can imitate nonliving objects; attacks only in humanoid form.
- Surprise chance: 1-5 on 1d6, modified normally.

## Platemail of Life Protection +3
- Level 2 south-wall armour rack.
- Starts with 6 charges.
- Energy-drain level loss consumes armour charges first.
- Capacity: 6 charges.
- Recharged through Sword of Smiting mechanics below.

## Faerie Dragon
- AC 26.
- HD 8.
- Attacks through spells.
- Move: 30'; Fly 80'.
- Save: Magic-User 8, +5 vs any magic.
- Morale: 8.
- XP: 875.
- HP: 32.
- Magic equivalent to 8th-level Magic-User.
- Source spell list: charm person, magic missile, sleep, detect invisibility, mirror image, web, hold person, invisibility 10' radius, confusion, polymorph other.
- Mentally linked to Merilla.
- Attempts to awaken Merilla: 5% chance per round.

## Pipe Beasts (2)
- AC 18.
- HD 6+6*.
- Attacks: 2 claws + 1 tail, or acid spray.
- Physical damage: 2d6 / 2d6 / 1d10.
- Acid spray: 4d8.
- Move: 30'.
- Save: Fighter 6.
- Morale: 12.
- XP: 555 each.
- HP: 30 and 25.
- Acid cone: every 4 rounds, maximum 3/day; 60' long, 20' wide at far end; save vs. Dragon Breath for half.
- Fire has no effect.
- Guard behaviour: attack anyone except creator Merilla entering guarded area until threat or beast is destroyed.

## Sword of Smiting +1
- On a successful hit against an energy-draining creature, adds one charge to worn Armor of Life Protection, up to normal maximum.
- If wielder is not wearing Armor of Life Protection, charge effect is lost.
- Otherwise behaves as a normal magical sword.

## Ring of Wonder
- At dawn each day, function changes randomly.
- GM secretly rolls native magical Rings table.
- Re-roll Ring of Spell Storing results.

## Superseded standalone Release 3 mechanics
Do **not** use in Release 21 play:
- three Blast Spores on Level 4;
- chainmail +2 / Stores Life Energy on Level 3;
- assassination/energy-drain-on-natural-20 Sword of Smiting mechanic;
- Ring of Wonder changing each time equipped;
- random magical-scroll/tome/potion percentage search on ground floor;
- older assassin/golem/rust-monster stat lines.
