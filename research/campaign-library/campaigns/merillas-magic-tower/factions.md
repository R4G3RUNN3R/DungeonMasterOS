# Merilla's Magic Tower - Factions / Alignment of Interests

## Merilla and her lawful protectors

Members/actors:
- Merilla;
- tower guardians/constructs as her defensive assets;
- potential local lawful authorities after the incident.

Goals:
- keep Merilla and her research/items secure;
- prevent theft/assassination;
- punish unjustified looting or wanton destruction when appropriate.

State distinctions:
- `neutral/unknown`: party has not materially harmed property/guardian interests;
- `intruder`: party entered without clear authority but has not committed major theft/destruction;
- `rescuer`: party saved Merilla;
- `forgiven_guardian_damage`: source-backed state when Merilla survives and forgives guardians destroyed during her rescue;
- `theft_unresolved`: party stole treasury/research/personal property; this can coexist with `rescuer`;
- `hostile/reprisal`: party assaulted/looted without a rescue justification or continues wrongdoing after Merilla recovers.

## External assassins

Members:
- unnamed Level 7 assassin;
- unnamed Level 4 assassin.

Goals:
- murder Merilla while she is incapacitated.

Known resources:
- scaling tools used to enter through Level 5 windows;
- magical leather armour and protection rings.

Unknown/source-unspecified:
- employer;
- organisation;
- motive beyond killing Merilla;
- names/backstories.

DMOS must not convert these unknowns into hard canon. If a longer campaign later needs an employer, that becomes explicit DMOS continuation/homebrew, not source-derived truth.

## Investigating authority / private employer

The source offers hook categories rather than named factions:

- lawful local authority may send the party to gather information;
- another employer may hire the group to assault the tower;
- GM may create another hook.

DMOS stores the selected hook as campaign state because it changes what constitutes authorised entry, theft, contract fulfilment and later lawful reaction.
