# Merilla's Magic Tower - NPCs and Actors

Authoritative gameplay version: **Adventure Anthology One Release 21**.

## Merilla
Role: tower owner, retired lawful magic-user/adventuring hero, potential rescue target and patron.

History/state:
- retired after years of protecting realms with a successful adventuring group;
- built tower for spell research and protective magic-item creation;
- powerful item creation periodically leaves her weak;
- begins alive, exhausted and semi-comatose after creating Sword of Smiting +1;
- unable to defend herself initially unless earlier play wakes her.

If saved:
- eventually recovers;
- forgives guardians destroyed during her rescue;
- rewards rescuers 2,500 gp each plus a minor magical item.

Wanton looting/destruction can still bring Merilla/lawful-authority reprisals.

## Faerie Dragon
Role: Level 3 watchdog/companion.
- created by Merilla;
- mentally linked with her;
- tries to awaken her, 5% chance per round;
- source explicitly frames it as more watchdog than guard;
- uses 8th-level-Magic-User-equivalent spell capability.

DMOS must preserve its protective/waking goal. A successful wake changes Level 5 state.

## Level 7 Assassin
Role: external infiltrator attempting to murder Merilla.
- follows party to tower;
- uses party's guardian distraction to climb through Level 5 windows;
- source gives no name/employer/backstory;
- Release 21: HP 22, saves Thief 7, Leather Armour +1, Ring of Protection +1.

## Level 4 Assassin
Same campaign role as Level 7 assassin.
- source gives no name/employer/backstory;
- Release 21: HP 12, saves Thief 4, Leather Armour +1, Ring of Protection +1.

## Bronze Golem
Role: programmed ground-floor guardian.
- appears as muscular statue;
- attacks intruders within 30 feet of itself/worktable;
- contains Ring of Wonder;
- Release 21 is extremely formidable and damages attackers who strike it with molten-metal counter-effect.

## Carpet Trapper
Role: ground-floor magical construct/hazard.
- triggers when victim enters centre of carpet;
- knocks prone, wraps and squeezes;
- attacking it can partially harm trapped victim;
- dispel magic interacts with it under source mechanics.

## Rust Monsters (2)
Role: ground-floor equipment threats.
- current Release 21 versions use updated native stats in `release21-mechanics.md`.

## Morphganger
Role: Level 2 disguised guardian.
- begins as southeast chest;
- imitates nonliving objects;
- attacks only in humanoid form;
- strong surprise chance.

## Pipe Beasts (2)
Role: Release 21 Level 4 access guardians.
- arcane constructs created to guard a specific room/route;
- their explicit task is preventing anyone except Merilla from ascending to her private chambers;
- use claws/tail or acid cone;
- fire-immune;
- attack guarded intruders until threat or beasts are destroyed.

These **replace the three Blast Spores from the older standalone Release 3** in current campaign canon.

## Hook patrons / lawful authority
The source provides hook categories, not named patrons:
- local lawful authority may send party to investigate;
- another employer may hire party to assault tower;
- GM may create another suitable reason.

DMOS may generate a minor patron, but the selected hook's authority/motive is campaign state because it affects whether entry/theft/destruction is lawful or criminal.
