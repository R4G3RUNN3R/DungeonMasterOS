# Merilla's Magic Tower - NPCs and Actors

## Merilla

Role: tower owner, retired adventuring hero, lawful magic-user, potential rescue target/patron.

### Established history
- Adventured for years with a successful group that repeatedly protected realms and accumulated wealth.
- Retired to a peaceful valley/tower to research magic and create protective items.
- Receives requests from powerful nobles for magical items but refuses some requests because powerful people can become greedy.
- Has survived prior attempts by thieves/brigands to take her creations.

### Initial campaign state
- Alive.
- Exhausted and semi-comatose after creating the Sword of Smiting +1.
- Unable to defend herself initially.
- Does not know the assassins have entered her private chamber.

### Outcomes
- If saved, eventually regains magical ability.
- Forgives destruction of guardians committed in the course of rescuing her.
- Rewards rescuers with 2,500 gp each plus a minor magic item.
- Wanton looting/guardian killing without rescue/justification can still lead to Merilla or lawful-authority reprisals.

## Faerie Dragon

Role: Merilla's magical watchdog/companion on Level 3.

- Mentally linked to Merilla.
- Magical power equivalent to an 8th-level magic-user under source mechanics.
- Attempts to awaken Merilla when possible, with a 5% chance per round.
- Normally functions as a watchdog rather than a simple stationary guard.
- DMOS should treat this actor as potentially aligned with a genuine rescue effort even if initial intrusion creates hostility/suspicion; exact social adjudication must respect source behaviour and player actions.

## Assassin, Level 7

Role: external infiltrator attempting to murder Merilla.

- Followed the party to the tower.
- Used the party's distraction of the tower guardians to scale into Level 5 through the windows.
- Initially argues with the second assassin about who will kill Merilla.
- Source gives combat level/equipment but no name, employer or personal backstory. DMOS must not invent a hidden canonical employer as campaign truth.
- Wears leather armour +1 and ring of protection +1.

## Assassin, Level 4

Role: external infiltrator attempting to murder Merilla.

- Same infiltration/campaign function as the Level 7 assassin.
- Source does not give a name/employer/backstory.
- Wears leather armour +1 and ring of protection +1.

## Bronze Golem

Role: programmed guardian on the ground level.

- Initially appears as a muscular statue at parade rest.
- Attacks intruders who approach within 30 feet of itself/worktable.
- Ring of Wonder is located inside its head.
- This is a trigger-based guardian, not automatically hostile merely because characters exist elsewhere on the floor.

## Carpet Trapper

Role: magical construct/hazard on ground level.

- 25-foot-square carpet in southwest ground floor.
- Reacts when a victim stands in its centre.
- Knocks victim prone, rolls them up and squeezes.
- Damage against the construct can partly harm the trapped victim; dispel magic can damage the construct under source mechanics.

## Rust Monsters (2)

Role: roaming/equipment-threat creatures on ground level.

- Source places two here.
- DMOS should not treat them as loyal intelligent servants with invented dialogue or motives.

## Morphganger

Role: Level 2 ambush creature/disguised object.

- Imitates nonliving objects.
- Initially presents as the southeast chest.
- Can assume humanoid form to attack.
- Has strong surprise capability because of mimicry.

## Blast Spores (3)

Role: Level 4 environmental/combat threat.

- Hover on Level 4.
- Fragile but dangerous if attacked/struck.
- DMOS must use source stat behaviour under native rules rather than reinterpreting them as generic spore monsters from another ruleset.

## External lawful authority / patron

The source permits a local authority to send the party to gather information, or another party to hire an assault. These patrons are hook scaffolding rather than canonically named characters. DMOS may generate a minor patron appropriate to the campaign, but must preserve whether the job is lawful investigation, suspicious private assault, or another explicit hook because that affects later moral/legal consequences.
