## Historical source used during initial extraction

### Merilla's Magic Tower - standalone Release 3
- Indexed copy: https://www.scribd.com/document/52350608/AA1-2-Merillas-Magic-Tower-r3
- Copyright line: 2007-2009 Rob Pinnell
- Source explicitly designated its entire text and included maps/floorplans OGC under OGL v1.0a.

Release 3 is retained for provenance/version history but is **superseded for current gameplay extraction** by AA1 Release 21 where the texts differ.

## Material version differences

AA1 Release 21 changes source mechanics/content relevant to DMOS:

- no old Release-3 random magical percentages for ground-floor containers; current text says detect magic finds no magical aura on them;
- Bronze Golem current anthology stats/special behaviour differ;
- Level 2 adds Platemail of Life Protection +3 with 6 charges;
- Level 4 now uses two Pipe Beasts guarding access to Level 5;
- old standalone Release 3's three Blast Spores are superseded;
- Life Protection armour and Sword of Smiting mechanics were rewritten into linked energy-drain charging/protection mechanics;
- assassin stats were updated.
