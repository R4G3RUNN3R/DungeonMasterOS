- Copyright line: 2007-2009 Rob Pinnell; source OGL Copyright Notice separately records `Merilla's Magic Tower Copyright 2007 Rob Pinnell`.

The indexed copy is used to read the source. Rights are determined from the adventure's own OGL section and the anthology/core legal declarations, not from the hosting site's generic upload metadata.

## Exact OGC scope captured from source

The adventure's OGL section explicitly designates:

- **entire text of Merilla's Magic Tower** as Open Game Content;
- **included maps/floorplans** as Open Game Content;
- OGL licence text itself excluded from being OGC;
- other artwork remains owned by its copyright holder;
- Basic Fantasy product/product-line name, logos and related visual branding are Product Identity.

This means DMOS may transform source story/room/NPC/item/monster text under OGL v1.0a, provided OGL terms and source Copyright Notice obligations are satisfied.
