# Merilla's Magic Tower - Encounters and Triggers

Native ruleset: Basic Fantasy Role-Playing Game.
Recommended party: 3-6 characters, levels 4-7.

## Approach / Entry Doors

### Runic fireball
Trigger: a character explicitly attempts to read the runic inscription on the east door.
Native effect: fireball, 10d6 damage, save vs. Spells for half, normal fireball area.
Non-trigger actions: merely seeing the runes, approaching, touching or attempting another door action do not automatically satisfy the text's reading trigger.

Possible resolutions:
- avoid reading;
- identify danger by other means if the native rules/action supports it;
- endure/dispel/bypass according to rules;
- attempt an unconventional exterior route.

## Ground Level

### Bronze Golem
Trigger: an intruder approaches within 30 feet of the golem/work table.
Native profile: AC 20, Move 80', one attack plus special, 3d10 + 1d10 heat damage, saves as Fighter 10, Morale 12, 60 HP.
Important state: Ring of Wonder in head.

This is a programmed-area guardian. DMOS must not make it chase characters across the setting without source/player-state justification.

### Carpet Trapper
Trigger: character stands in centre of the 25-foot-square carpet.
Native profile: AC 12, HD 7, immobile, 1d8 squeeze damage per round, saves as Fighter 7, Morale 12, 34 HP.
Behaviour:
- instantly knocks the victim prone and rolls them up;
- damage dealt to the Carpet Trapper also harms the trapped victim at the source-defined ratio;
- dispel magic damages the magical construct under source rules, with save interaction.

### Rust Monsters (2)
Native profile each: AC 18, HD 5, Move 40', one rust/equipment attack, saves as Fighter 3, Morale 6; source HP 32 and 28.
Threat: primarily equipment destruction rather than direct HP damage.

### Research-library search
Not a combat encounter, but a time/resource encounter. Searching hundreds of scrolls/tomes/containers can reveal rare magic based on source percentages and should consume meaningful time. This can matter because the assassins use the party's intrusion/distraction to reach Level 5.

## Level 2

### North and southwest chests
Trigger: opening/triggering without successful bypass/dispel.
Effect: each has a 10d6 fireball trap, save vs. Spells for half.
Treasure: 15,000 gp in each.

### Southeast chest / Morphganger
State: creature disguised as nonliving chest.
Native profile: AC 16, HD 10, one attack for 1d12, Move 10', saves as Fighter 5, Morale 8, 60 HP.
Mimicry: can imitate nonliving objects and only attacks in humanoid form; source gives 1-5 on 1d6 chance to gain surprise, subject to normal modifiers.

## Level 3

### Faerie Dragon
Native profile: AC 26, HD 8, Move 30'/Fly 80', saves as Magic-User 8 with +5 vs magic, Morale 8, 32 HP.
Attack mode: spell use equivalent to an 8th-level magic-user.
Source spell list includes charm person, magic missile, sleep, detect invisibility, mirror image, web, hold person, invisibility 10-foot radius, confusion, polymorph other.

State/goal:
- mentally linked with Merilla;
- attempts to wake her when possible, 5% chance per round;
- source calls it more watchdog than guard.

DMOS adjudication:
- player intent matters. A rescue-minded party may still be intruders, but AI must not erase the dragon's core goal of protecting/waking Merilla.
- if it wakes Merilla before the assassination succeeds, update Merilla state rather than keeping her forcibly unconscious to preserve a scripted finale.

## Level 4

### Blast Spores (3)
Native source summary: AC 9, 1 HP, Move 30' (10'), one attack plus disease, explosion damage 6-36 if struck, saves as Fighter 1, Morale 9, Neutral.

Key behaviour: extremely fragile and dangerous to strike. DMOS should present tactical alternatives and avoid translating them into ordinary sacks of hit points.

## Level 5

### Two assassins
Default arrival state: both have followed the party, climbed through the only windows while the party distracted guardians, and are arguing over which one will assassinate Merilla.

Level 7 assassin:
- AC 16, HD 7d4, one attack, damage 1d8, Move 40', source saves Thief 7, Morale 9, 30 HP;
- leather armour +1;
- ring of protection +1.

Level 4 assassin:
- AC 16, HD 4d4, one attack, damage 1d8, Move 40', source line also gives Thief 7 saves, Morale 9, 12 HP;
- leather armour +1;
- ring of protection +1.

Objective: murder incapacitated Merilla.
They have no source-given names, employer or further plot. Do not manufacture a canonical conspiracy.

### Merilla rescue state
Merilla cannot defend herself initially. If the party prevents assassination, she survives and later rewards them. If the Faerie Dragon wakes her, use that updated state according to native GM adjudication instead of pretending she remains helpless.

## Campaign-level fail-forward states

- Party retreats before Level 5: assassins remain a danger; determine their progress from actual established infiltration state rather than automatic off-screen victory unless the party's delay reasonably gives them the opportunity.
- Party loots and leaves: Merilla may die; legal/reprisal state remains valid.
- Party saves Merilla after destroying guardians: source explicitly allows forgiveness for guardian losses incurred in rescue.
- Party saves Merilla but steals 30,000 gp/personal valuables: rescue success and theft state coexist. Reward does not erase theft.
- Party sides with assassins: valid free-text branch but produces Merilla-dead/hostile-law state, not the rescue reward ending.
