# Merilla's Magic Tower - Encounters and Triggers

Authoritative gameplay version: **Adventure Anthology One Release 21**.
Native ruleset: Basic Fantasy Role-Playing Game; 3-6 characters, levels 4-7.
Exact current stats are stored in `release21-mechanics.md`.

## Entry
**Runic fireball:** triggers only when a character attempts to read the east-door runes. Native damage 10d6, save vs. Spells for half.

## Ground Level
**Bronze Golem:** attacks intruders entering its 30-foot protected radius around itself/worktable. Current Release 21 version has strong heat/counter-damage mechanics and Ring of Wonder inside its head.

**Carpet Trapper:** triggers when a victim stands in its centre, knocks prone, wraps/squeezes, and can cause damage to the trapped victim when others attack it.

**Rust Monsters (2):** equipment-destruction threats on the ground floor.

**Research pressure:** Release 21 removes the older percentage-based magical scroll/tome/potion search. Important writings are code/language-locked and slow to decipher; component containers show no magical aura.

## Level 2
- North chest: 15,000 gp, 10d6 fireball trap.
- Southwest chest: 15,000 gp, 10d6 fireball trap.
- Southeast chest: Morphganger disguised as chest.
- Platemail of Life Protection +3 with 6 charges on armour rack.
- Dispel magic can disable the chest fireball traps.

## Level 3
**Faerie Dragon:** mentally linked with Merilla, uses powerful magic, and attempts to awaken her with a 5% chance per round. It is a watchdog, not merely a kill-on-sight monster. Any successful wake state persists into Level 5.

## Level 4
**Pipe Beasts (2):** current Release 21 guardians. They prevent anyone except Merilla from ascending to the private chambers, use claws/tail or powerful acid spray, are fire-immune, and fight until the guarded threat or they are destroyed.

This **supersedes the three Blast Spores in standalone Release 3**.

## Level 5
**Two external assassins:** Level 7 and Level 4 under native rules. They followed the party, climbed through the only windows while the party distracted guardians, and intend to murder Merilla. Both wear Leather Armour +1 and Ring of Protection +1. Source gives no names/employer/backstory.

## Outcome guards
- Rescue after guardian destruction: source allows Merilla to forgive rescue-related guardian losses.
- Rescue plus theft: both states remain true; rescue does not legalise theft.
- Wanton looting/killing without rescue justification: Merilla/lawful-authority reprisals remain valid.
- Merilla killed: no rescue reward.
- Exterior bypass: only if actual rules/geometry support reaching Level 5 windows.

## Community difficulty note
A 2026 Basic Fantasy reviewer considers the current Bronze Golem exceptionally deadly for the stated level band and describes the final assassins as much weaker than the tower's earlier defences. Store this as community balance intelligence, not as permission to silently rebalance native play.
