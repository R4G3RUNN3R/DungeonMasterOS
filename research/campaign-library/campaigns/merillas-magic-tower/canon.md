# Merilla's Magic Tower - Canon Locks

Authoritative gameplay version: **Adventure Anthology One Release 21**.

## Hard canon
- Merilla is a renowned retired lawful magic-user/adventuring hero, not the source villain.
- She built the tower for research/item creation; powerful creation can leave her weak.
- She begins alive and semi-comatose after creating Sword of Smiting +1.
- Tower guardians/traps are her protections.
- The source permits lawful investigation, hired assault, or another suitable GM hook.
- Wanton looting/guardian killing can bring Merilla/lawful-authority reprisals.
- East-door fireball triggers when someone attempts to **read the runes**.
- Ground Bronze Golem protects a 30-foot radius around itself/worktable and contains Ring of Wonder.
- Release 21 component containers show no magical aura; old percentage-based magical-search rules are superseded.
- Level 2 contains two 15,000-gp fireball-trapped chests, one Morphganger chest, and Platemail of Life Protection +3 with 6 charges.
- Level 3 Faerie Dragon is mentally linked to Merilla and attempts to wake her, acting more as watchdog than guard.
- Level 4 contains **two Pipe Beasts** whose purpose is preventing anyone except Merilla ascending to Level 5.
- Two external assassins followed the party and used its guardian distraction to climb through Level 5's only windows.
- Source gives the assassins no names, employer or larger conspiracy.
- Merilla cannot defend herself initially unless earlier valid play wakes her.
- If saved, Merilla forgives guardian destruction incurred during rescue and rewards rescuers 2,500 gp each plus a minor magic item.
- Rescue does not automatically erase unrelated theft/property consequences.
- Release 21 item mechanics in `release21-mechanics.md` are authoritative.

## Conditional canon
- Reading runes triggers entry fireball.
- Entering golem radius triggers golem.
- Level 2 traps trigger unless bypassed/disabled.
- Southeast chest remains Morphganger.
- Active Faerie Dragon attempts 5% per round to wake Merilla.
- Pipe Beasts attack guarded intruders other than Merilla until threat or beasts are destroyed.
- A successful wake persists through Level 5.
- Merilla death removes rescue reward and preserves lawful/property consequences.

## Superseded Release 3 material
Do not use with Release 21:
- Level 4 Blast Spores;
- Level 3 chainmail +2 / Stores Life Energy;
- old Sword of Smiting assassination/level-drain mechanic;
- Ring of Wonder changing on equip;
- old ground magical-search percentages;
- older stat lines.

## Forbidden AI deviations
- Do not make Merilla secretly evil to justify combat.
- Do not invent an assassin employer as canon.
- Do not trigger runes from merely seeing/touching them.
- Do not reset Merilla unconscious after a successful wake.
- Do not erase theft because a rescue occurred.
- Do not mix Release 3 and Release 21.
- Do not invent D&D 5e levels; source-native support is Basic Fantasy levels 4-7.
