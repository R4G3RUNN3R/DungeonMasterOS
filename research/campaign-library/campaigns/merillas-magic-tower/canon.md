# Merilla's Magic Tower - Canon Locks

## Hard canon

1. Merilla is a renowned retired lawful magic-user and former adventuring hero, not a villain occupying a stolen tower.
2. Merilla built the tower as a place for magical research and creation of protective items.
3. Creating powerful magic periodically leaves Merilla weak and vulnerable.
4. At the start of the adventure the tower is silent because Merilla has just created a Sword of Smiting +1 and is exhausted/semi-comatose in her private chamber.
5. The tower's strange creatures, constructs and traps are Merilla's established protections, not proof she is evil.
6. Local authorities or another patron may send/hire the party; the source explicitly allows multiple hooks.
7. Wanton looting or destruction of Merilla's guardians can create reprisals from Merilla and/or lawful authorities.
8. The east entry door carries a runic fireball trap that triggers when someone attempts to **read the runes**, not merely from touching/approaching the door.
9. The ground-floor Bronze Golem attacks an intruder who comes within 30 feet of itself/worktable; it contains a Ring of Wonder in its head.
10. Level 2 contains two trapped treasure chests and one Morphganger disguised as a chest.
11. Level 3's Faerie Dragon is mentally linked with Merilla and attempts to awaken her; it normally functions more as a watchdog than a simple kill-on-sight guard.
12. Level 4 contains three Blast Spores.
13. Two assassins followed the party to the tower and exploit the party's distraction of the tower guardians to scale the tower's only windows into Merilla's private level.
14. The assassins are present on Level 5 arguing over who will kill the incapacitated Merilla when the party reaches them under the default source sequence.
15. Merilla cannot defend herself in her initial state.
16. If the party saves Merilla from assassination, she forgives their destruction of guardians during the rescue and rewards them as described by the source.
17. Saving Merilla is therefore a materially different outcome from simply looting the tower.
18. Merilla's newly-created Sword of Smiting +1 is in her private chamber.

## Conditional canon

- If someone explicitly tries to read the east entry runes, the 10d6 fireball trap triggers (save for half under the native rules).
- If someone approaches within the Bronze Golem's protected radius, it attacks as programmed.
- If the party spends time searching hundreds of ground-floor books/scrolls/components, rare spell scrolls/potions may be found according to the source's stated percentages.
- If a character steps into the centre of the Carpet Trapper, it wraps/squeezes the victim according to its source mechanics.
- If the Level 2 trapped chests are opened without bypass/dispel, their fireball traps trigger.
- The southeast Level 2 chest is a Morphganger; its disguise is a creature state, not a normal treasure chest.
- On Level 3 the Faerie Dragon attempts to awaken Merilla with a 5% chance per round when it can do so.
- If the party kills/loots without ultimately rescuing Merilla or without lawful justification, later reprisals remain valid.
- If Merilla survives the assassins, she can recover over time and reward the party.
- If Merilla is killed, the reward/rescue ending is impossible and lawful/political consequences should reflect her death.

## Soft canon / permitted GM variation

The source explicitly says the scenario can be introduced in several ways and allows the GM to invent another sufficient hook. DMOS may create a lawful-investigation patron, a suspicious/greedy employer, or another source-appropriate reason to enter the tower so long as Merilla's true state and tower history remain unchanged.

The source leaves specific randomly-found spell scrolls/potions to the GM. DMOS may resolve these using the native ruleset tables or campaign compendium where appropriate.

## Forbidden AI deviations

- Do not retcon Merilla into an evil sorcerer to justify dungeon combat.
- Do not place the assassins as stationary tower employees/guards.
- Do not trigger the entry fireball just because a player saw/touched the runes; reading is the trigger.
- Do not force every guardian to attack on simple room entry if the source gives a more specific trigger.
- Do not let Merilla wake and defeat the assassins automatically unless the Faerie Dragon/source conditions or player intervention cause that state.
- Do not erase looting consequences simply because the players reached the top.
- Do not invent a D&D 5e level range; source-native levels are Basic Fantasy 4-7.
