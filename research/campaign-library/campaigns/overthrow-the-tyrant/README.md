# OVERTHROW THE TYRANT - An Homage to Blancaseda

Status: `researching`

## Executive summary

`OVERTHROW THE TYRANT` is an eight-page, system-agnostic OSR adventure by Rolets_CAT. It returns to Blancaseda, the setting of the creator's longest earlier campaign, and asks a new group to confront the consequences left behind by `The Heroes Who Departed`.

The creator page states that the zine includes regional rumours, wilderness travel events, dangerous people, key locations inside Blancaseda, city factions, and the Tyrant. The page also gives a concrete in-world lead: enter the `Dead Parrots' Tavern` and ask for the `Undead Kings`.

The creator explicitly licenses **the zine itself under CC BY 4.0**, stating that others may use it as they wish with attribution.

## Why this is a strong DMOS candidate

This is closer to a miniature campaign framework than a single keyed dungeon. It already promises:
- region context and rumours
- travel encounters
- city locations
- factions
- a named antagonist/tyrant
- consequences inherited from prior history

That combination is ideal for testing whether DungeonMasterOS can preserve a small living-world political situation while still allowing players to approach the tyrant problem freely.
