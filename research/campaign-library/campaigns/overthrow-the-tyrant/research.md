# OVERTHROW THE TYRANT - Source-derived research

Only facts visible on the primary creator page are treated as hard canon until the PDFs are fully extracted.

## Historical frame

Blancaseda was the first setting in which the creator's players began what became their longest campaign. The new adventure revisits that setting after the original group, referred to as `The Heroes Who Departed`, left consequences behind. The new player characters must confront those consequences.

The source page does not yet state what those heroes did, whether they are celebrated or blamed, or how their acts created the present tyranny. That entire historical causal chain is pending PDF extraction.

## Confirmed scope of the eight-page adventure

The creator explicitly says it contains:
- introduction and rumours concerning the Blancaseda region
- wilderness travel events
- encounters with perilous people
- key city locations
- overview of factions operating in Blancaseda's streets
- description of the Tyrant

The creator also gives a concrete lead: enter the `Dead Parrots' Tavern` and ask for the `Undead Kings`.

## Structural implications for DMOS

This adventure should not be reduced to `find tyrant -> fight tyrant`. The source promises rumours, wilderness events, urban locations and factions, which implies multiple information and relationship states before the final confrontation.

Once extracted, the DMOS graph should model:
- rumours separately from confirmed facts
- faction attitudes and resources
- travel events without making every encounter mandatory
- consequences inherited from The Heroes Who Departed
- multiple approaches to the Tyrant when supported by source
