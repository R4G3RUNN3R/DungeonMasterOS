# OVERTHROW THE TYRANT - Canon register

## Hard canon currently confirmed

- The adventure is set in the Blancaseda region/city setting.
- The current situation follows consequences left by a prior group known as `The Heroes Who Departed`.
- A Tyrant is a central adversarial/political figure.
- Blancaseda has multiple factions active in its streets.
- The adventure includes wilderness travel events and perilous people outside/in transit to the city.
- `Dead Parrots' Tavern` is a named location/lead.
- `Undead Kings` is a named source term tied to the instruction to ask for them at the tavern.
- The zine is system-agnostic/OSR and CC BY 4.0.
