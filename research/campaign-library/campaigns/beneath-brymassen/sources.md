# Beneath Brymassen - Sources and Rights

## Authoritative source hierarchy
1. **Adventure Anthology One, 1st Edition Release 21**, Basic Fantasy Project. This is the gameplay authority used by the DMOS template.
2. Current indexed Release 21 mirror used for line-by-line extraction because the publisher site was not directly readable by the research browser.
3. Older anthology releases are historical comparison only and do not override Release 21.
4. Community play reports are intelligence only, never canon.

## Ruleset and progression
- Native ruleset: Basic Fantasy Role-Playing Game.
- Adventure title block: **3 to 6 beginning player characters**.
- Release 21 contents: describes it as a beginning module.
- Independent RPG index: tags `Beneath Brymassen` as **1st level**.
- DMOS public guidance: **3-6 beginning / level-1 characters**.
- Do not copy the nearby `levels 2-5` text from `The Zombraire's Estate`; that range belongs to the preceding adventure in the anthology contents.

## Open-content disposition
Release 21's OGL section designates the entire text of `Adventure Anthology One` as Open Game Content, excluding the Open Game License itself as explained there. Artwork and designated Product Identity, including product branding/logos and visual material, are not Open Game Content.

### DMOS release requirements
Before public built-in distribution:
- include the required OGL v1.0a licence material and complete Copyright Notice package;
- clearly identify DMOS-derived Open Game Content as required by the licence;
- do not redistribute anthology artwork/maps unless separately permitted;
- do not use protected logos/trade dress;
- keep source attribution/provenance in the campaign page.

## Canon boundary
Only the Release 21 adventure text controls room contents, encounter relationships, treasure, traps, the two hooks and the two Room 14 variants. Community campaign additions such as renamed locations, expanded regional plots, later hobgoblin invasions or converted rules are not source canon.
