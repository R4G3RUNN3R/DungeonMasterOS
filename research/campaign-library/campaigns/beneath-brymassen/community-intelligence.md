# Beneath Brymassen - Community Intelligence

Community material is never source canon. It is used only to identify pacing, difficulty, conversion and player-behaviour risks.

## Felbrigg actual play (2022)
A two-character Basic Fantasy run used the dungeon-crawl hook and demonstrates several useful behaviours:
- the mayor's bounty hook is immediately understandable to players;
- the pit sequence can hurt beginning characters before major combat;
- players may leave the dungeon and re-enter through another route rather than treating the map as a single linear crawl;
- the Room 11 hobgoblin can be attacked even after trying to talk/bargain, so DMOS must support both diplomacy and sudden violence;
- players may focus heavily on extracting saleable treasure and proof-of-kill rather than clearing every room.

This report is not authority for room contents, encounter stats or treasure amounts.

## The 3 Toadstools campaign reports
The adventure was used as an introductory Basic Fantasy scenario and then connected into a larger home campaign. This supports:
- beginner suitability;
- easy attachment to a continuing sandbox;
- portability into broader OSR campaign play.

Later regional plots, renamed NPCs/locations and expanded enemy factions are home-campaign material and are excluded from source canon.

## Conversion evidence
A later Shadowdark/Wilderlands campaign log appears to adapt the rescue concept substantially, changing names/location/factions. This is evidence that the adventure premise ports well, not permission to import those alterations into the built-in template.

## DMOS implementation lessons
- Beginning characters can encounter meaningful traps early; do not silently reduce source danger.
- The information broker needs a truth-confidence model rather than a binary `knows/doesn't know` flag.
- The two Room 14 versions require a hard campaign-mode discriminator.
- Retreat/re-entry must preserve discovered traps, dead creatures, negotiated actors and opened routes.
- Room 7 is a deliberate anti-pattern test for AI hallucination: repeated investigation must be allowed to return `nothing further`.
