# Beneath Brymassen - Items, Treasure and Rewards

## Plot-relevant items
### Hidden-shrine key
Location: corpse in the southern pit, Room 3.
Purpose: opens the concealed shrine in Room 2.

### Golden healing chalice
Location: Room 2.
Behaviour: works only inside the shrine; holy water consumed from it provides limited healing, at most twice per day under source rules.
Guard: moving it elsewhere does not preserve its effect.

### Medallion of ESP
Holder: grizzled hobgoblin, Room 11.
Plot relevance: explains how he brokers information and manipulates visitors.

## Notable magic treasure
- Potion of Healing on the corpse in Room 3.
- Scepter of Healing as one possible catacomb-search result in Room 10.
- Invisibility spell scroll as another possible Room 10 result.
- Ring of Protection +1 in Room 13 sarcophagus.
- Ring of Fire Resistance in Room 13 sarcophagus.

## Other meaningful treasure
- Golden chalice and silver holy symbol in Room 2 have monetary value in addition to their context.
- Room 8 splinter kobolds possess two large coin loads plus personal coin.
- Room 10 search outcomes can also reveal a valuable garnet, alongside severe hazards.
- Room 11 furnishings are valuable but bulky; a small purse and mundane cookbook are also present.
- Room 14 coin exists in both variants, but holder/context changes with the selected mode.

## Surface rewards
### Crawl mode
Mayor Forsythe pays bounty by proof of slain raiders, with a larger bounty category for larger creatures.

### Rescue mode
The kidnapped children's parents offer their household savings for the children's return.

## DMOS reward guardrails
- Do not award both surface hooks' rewards unless the campaign fiction actually establishes both contracts.
- Do not convert Room 8's stolen treasure into the chieftain's full hoard; the source says it is only part of the tribal treasure.
- Random Room 10 rewards/hazards must be generated from the source-defined outcome logic, not cherry-picked to favour the party.
- Valuable bulky furnishings require plausible extraction; value does not imply effortless transport.
