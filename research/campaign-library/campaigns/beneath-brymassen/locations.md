# Beneath Brymassen - Locations

## Surface
### Brymassen
Small rural settlement containing the tavern and whichever surface property is used by the chosen hook.

### Entry property
- `crawl` mode: local mill, whose basement has been breached by humanoid raiders.
- `rescue` mode: leather-worker's shop and cellar, where clawed humanoid tracks lead into the breach.

## Dungeon key
### 1. Entrance Room
30-foot-square chamber reached from the crawl tunnel. East/west exits. A differently coloured floor stone near the tunnel mouth marks a ceiling spear trap.

### 2. Hidden Shrine
Secret room whose key is in the southern pit of Room 3. Long-dead cleric, silver holy symbol, illuminated golden chalice, and a permanent protective sanctuary effect. The chalice can provide limited healing from holy water but only while used in this room.

### 3. The Pits
Two 10-foot pits. Northern pit is open with narrow ledges. Southern pit is a concealed spiked trap that closes after triggering; a dead adventurer at its bottom carries the key to Room 2, a healing potion and mundane gear/coin.

### 4. Centipede Room
Knee-deep sludge and debris. Simply crossing is safe. Probing/searching risks disturbing a large immature-centipede nest.

### 5. Octagonal Room
Transit hub with north/west/east doors. East route narrows into a natural passage that eventually reaches a concealed outdoor cavern exit.

### 6. Battle Axe Trap / Bedroom
Opening the door triggers a large swinging axe unless bypassed carefully. Bedroom-like furnishings, abandoned card game, minor coin/value.

### 7. Empty / False Clue Room
Broken floor, graffiti and deliberately misleading markings. The apparent door outline is scratched into the wall and is not a real secret door. Yellow 'button' does nothing.

### 8. Kobolds on the Run
Six kobolds around a fire. They are hiding from their chieftain after stealing part of the tribal treasure and are arguing over what to do next. Historical busts/tapestries and carried coin bags are present.

### 9. Storeroom
Mostly looted room with broken storage debris. Green Slime occupies the central ceiling and attacks creatures/objects reaching the middle below it.

### 10. Catacomb
Bone-lined chamber dedicated to an old death/fertility cult. Repeated searching uses a source random-outcome table that mixes rewards and serious hazards, including disease, poison and a request-compulsion curse.

### 11. Hobgoblin's Room
Comfortable green-stone chamber full of carpets, tapestries and pillows. Elderly grizzled hobgoblin information broker uses a Medallion of ESP, trades information for coin and is not hostile unless attacked. He mixes actual dungeon knowledge with opportunistic lies.

### 12. Room of Glowing Skulls
Octagonal room with permanently luminous skulls fixed into wall niches. Five orcs are trying to remove one. Their attention state depends on how noisy the approaching party is.

### 13. Sarcophagus Room
Secret chamber with a central sarcophagus and four corner skeleton guardians. Guardians remain motionless until attacked or the sarcophagus is touched. Sarcophagus holds two magic rings on the interred adventurer.

### 14A. Jail Cell (`rescue` mode only)
Wooden cage suspended from ceiling with four kidnapped children. Five kobolds and a chieftain guard them. The group is debating ransom versus selling the captives to the orc slavers from Room 12. Cage must be lowered from its tied-off rope before the children can be freed safely.

### 14B. Bird Cage (`crawl` mode only)
Fallen iron cage, dead goblin and seven escaped stirges. The goblin carries coin. This version replaces 14A completely when the adventure is run as a conventional dungeon crawl.

## Topology rules
- Map geometry from the Release 21 dungeon map is authoritative.
- Room 5's outside exit can create a legitimate alternate ingress/egress route once discovered.
- Secret access to Rooms 2 and 13 must remain secret-door dependent; DMOS must not invent extra doors.
- Room 7 has no secret door despite visual bait.
