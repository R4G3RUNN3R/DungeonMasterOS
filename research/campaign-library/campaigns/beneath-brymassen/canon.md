# Beneath Brymassen - Canon Locks

## Hard canon
- The adventure has two valid modes: `crawl` and `rescue`.
- The dungeon map/topology is fixed by the source. Room 5 has a natural eastern route to a concealed outside exit.
- Room 2 is a hidden shrine protected as a sanctuary; its healing chalice works only while it remains in that room.
- Room 3 contains one open pit and one concealed spiked pit; the hidden-shrine key is on the corpse in the southern pit.
- Room 4 is safe if simply crossed; disturbing the sludge may trigger a mass immature-centipede encounter.
- Room 6 has a door-triggered swinging battle-axe trap.
- Room 7 contains deliberate false clues and **no secret door**.
- Room 8 contains six kobolds hiding from their own tribe after stealing part of the tribal treasure.
- Room 9 contains Green Slime on the ceiling.
- Room 10 is a catacomb whose prolonged searching can produce treasure, poison/disease hazards or a magical compulsion curse.
- Room 11's elderly hobgoblin information broker is non-hostile unless attacked; he trades information, often mixing lies with useful hints.
- Room 12 contains five orcs attempting to remove permanently glowing skulls.
- Room 13 contains a sarcophagus guarded by four skeletons that remain inert until attacked or the sarcophagus is touched.
- In `rescue` mode, Room 14 contains four kidnapped children, five kobolds and a kobold chieftain. The kobolds are considering ransom or sale to the orcs/slavers from Room 12.
- In `crawl` mode, Room 14 instead contains the fallen bird cage, a crushed goblin and seven stirges. No kidnapped children exist in this mode.

## Conditional canon
- If the players approach Room 8 quietly and understand Kobold, they can learn the splinter group's situation before deciding how to act.
- If the rescue-mode party listens to Room 14 in Kobold, they can learn that the children may be ransomed or sold and that the chieftain is angry about stolen tribal treasure.
- If the Room 13 sarcophagus is touched/opened, the four corner skeletons activate.
- If the Room 10 compulsion curse is triggered, the victim cannot refuse understandable requests until the curse is removed; ordinary orders are not equivalent to requests.
- If the party repeatedly searches the fake Room 7 clue, nothing new appears. The AI must not convert persistence into a secret passage.

## Soft canon
- Ordinary details of Brymassen, the tavern, mayoral aides, the leather shop and other incidental townspeople may be improvised when they do not alter either hook.
- The GM may place ordinary support services in town. Such additions are support detail, not adventure truth.

## Free-improvisation boundary
The AI may improvise dialogue, ordinary travel description, tactical positioning and reactions consistent with morale/intelligence. It may not invent additional keyed rooms, move source treasure between rooms, merge the two Room 14 versions, convert Room 11 into a mandatory fight, or create a secret route in Room 7.
