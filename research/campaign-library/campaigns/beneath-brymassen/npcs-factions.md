# Beneath Brymassen - NPCs and Factions

## Surface actors
### Forsythe, Mayor of Brymassen
Mode: `crawl`.
Goal: stop humanoid raids beneath the local mill.
Offer: bounty for proof of slain raiders, with higher payment for larger creatures.
Canon boundary: he is a patron, not a hidden villain or dungeon controller.

### Kidnapped children's father
Mode: `rescue`.
Goal: recover his four children alive.
Resources: poor household; offers all available savings as reward.
Canon boundary: DMOS may improvise ordinary personality/dialogue but must preserve desperation, poverty and the rescue objective.

### Kidnapped children's mother
Mode: `rescue`.
Goal: same as father; begs for the children to be recovered.

## Dungeon factions
### Kobold tribe / chieftain group
Primary base: Room 14 in rescue mode.
Goal: retain control of captives/treasure and decide whether to ransom or sell the children.
State: angry because Room 8 splinter kobolds stole part of the tribal treasure.
Potential outcomes: combat, intimidation, bargain, ransom negotiation, stealth rescue, or other free-text tactics. The source does not force combat.

### Room 8 kobold splinter group
Six kobolds hiding from their own chieftain after stealing part of the tribal treasure.
Goal: avoid the boss and decide where to go next.
Knowledge: their theft, their own faction trouble and local dungeon context.
DMOS guard: do not automatically make them loyal to the chieftain or the player characters.

### Orc slavers / Room 12 orcs
Five orcs attempting to remove a glowing skull.
Relationship: in rescue mode they are the buyers/slavers the Room 14 kobolds expect may arrive for the kidnapped children.
DMOS guard: this relationship is campaign truth even if the party encounters Room 12 before Room 14.

### Grizzled hobgoblin information broker
Room: 11.
Disposition: neutral unless attacked.
Capabilities: uses a Medallion of ESP; barters information for gold.
Knowledge behaviour: genuinely knows some things about the dungeon but often embellishes/lies enough to keep buyers interested.
DMOS guard: do not turn every statement into reliable exposition. Treat information as potentially mixed truth/falsehood unless the source or discovered state verifies it.

### Room 13 skeleton guardians
Four inert guardians.
Trigger: attacked or sarcophagus touched.
No independent agenda beyond source trigger.

### Environmental threats
Centipedes, Green Slime, stirges and traps are hazards/creatures, not factions. Do not grant them strategic coordination unless normal creature behaviour requires it.
