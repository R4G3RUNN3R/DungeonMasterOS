# The Equinoctial Threshold - Location register

## Forest hill / ancient tree entrance

- Threshold entrance is hidden in a large grassy hill in a forest.
- Stone door lies among roots of an ancient tree and may require keen observation to find.
- Boggart magic controls the door's nightly opening window.
- Exact exterior map/encounters: pending.

## The Equinoctial Threshold

- One of eight portals to the Fae.
- Former garrison/holdfast of Laidre the Duskkiller.
- Visitors can be sealed inside during daylight.
- Complete interior map and keyed rooms: pending v1.3 extraction.
