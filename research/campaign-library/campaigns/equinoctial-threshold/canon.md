# The Equinoctial Threshold - Canon register

## Hard canon currently confirmed

- The Equinoctial Threshold is one of eight portals to the world of the Fae.
- It is hidden inside/beneath a large grassy hill in a forest, with a stone door among the roots of an ancient tree.
- Boggart magic makes the entrance open only for a brief period each night; it seals mortal and Fae visitors inside during the day.
- Laidre the Duskkiller was a vicious warlord who used the Threshold as a garrison with his Satyr Bullies.
- Ste. Brumaire, Saint of Boundaries, confronted Laidre; the battle killed her and left him cursed so he can never leave the Threshold.
- Laidre is now diminished enough that the Satyr Bullies work with/around the goblin merchants of the Night Market.
- The Night Market does not use ordinary coin and can demand currencies such as luck, faces, time and talents.
- The Gloomblade is a legendary artefact rumoured to be in Laidre's vault.
- The Boundary Stone is a source-defined object whose destruction can send away the Threshold and its Night Market.
- Source-supported player motives include commerce, theft, vengeance, planar travel and destruction of the Threshold.

## Rumour / uncertainty locks

- `The Gloomblade is in Laidre's vault` is presented as a rumour in the public pitch. It may not be treated as verified treasure location until the source confirms what is actually in the vault.
- The Night Market's claim to carry effectively anything anyone could want is market legend/presentation, not permission for the AI to conjure arbitrary plot-breaking items without the source's trade rules.
