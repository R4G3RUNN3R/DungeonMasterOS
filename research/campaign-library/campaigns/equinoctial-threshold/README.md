# The Equinoctial Threshold

Status: `researching`

## Executive summary

`The Equinoctial Threshold` is a Cairn 2E-compatible puzzle-dungeon/adventure by Marlowe Rehac / Godbotherers. The Threshold is one of eight portals to the Fae, hidden beneath a grassy hill in a forest. Boggart enchantments allow the stone entrance to open only briefly each night, trapping mortal and Fae visitors inside during the day.

The adventure's public source already establishes a dense nonlinear situation rather than a single quest: the ruined holdfast of cursed warlord **Laidre the Duskkiller**, his diminished **Satyr Bullies**, the goblin merchants of the **Night Market**, the dead boundary-saint **Ste. Brumaire**, the legendary **Gloomblade**, and the **Boundary Stone**. Player motives may include shopping for impossible goods, robbing Laidre's vault, avenging Ste. Brumaire, finding passage to the Fae, or destroying the Boundary Stone and removing the Threshold/Night Market.

The work is explicitly **CC BY-SA 4.0**, AI-free, and currently available as v1.3.

## Why this is one of the best DMOS graph candidates

It naturally supports multiple simultaneous objectives with no single mandatory heroic path:
- commerce at the Night Market
- theft/heist against Laidre
- vengeance/religious mission
- planar passage
- destruction of the site itself

DMOS should therefore model **goal state + faction state + strange-currency state + day/night access state**, not a chapter railroad.
