# The Equinoctial Threshold - Sources and provenance

Accessed: 2026-08-17

## Primary creator page
URL: https://godbotherers.itch.io/equinoctialthreshold

Supports:
- Threshold is one of eight portals to the Fae
- hidden in grassy hill/forest beneath roots of ancient tree
- stone door opens briefly each night because of Boggart magic and seals visitors inside during the day
- Laidre the Duskkiller, vicious former warlord, garrisoned the Threshold with Satyr Bullies
- Ste. Brumaire, Saint of Boundaries, opposed him; she died and he was cursed never to leave
- Satyr Bullies later found work with goblin Night Market merchants
- Night Market offers essentially anything but does not trade in coin; prices can include luck, faces, time and talents
- source-supported adventure goals include: buy a peculiar item, steal the Gloomblade from Laidre's vault, avenge Ste. Brumaire, seek passage to the Fae, destroy the Boundary Stone / send away Threshold and market
- author Marlowe Rehac; editor Dylan Garofalo
- Cairn 2E compatible
- Dungeon Scrawl maps
- work explicitly CC BY-SA 4.0 and AI-free
- current v1.3 plus original jam v1.0 downloads

Authority: primary creator publication.
