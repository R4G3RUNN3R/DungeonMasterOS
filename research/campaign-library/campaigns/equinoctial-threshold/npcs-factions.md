# The Equinoctial Threshold - NPC and faction register

## Laidre the Duskkiller

- Role: former warlord / major current inhabitant
- Historical behaviour: vicious and rapacious
- Former force: Satyr Bullies
- Historical conflict: drew the intervention of Ste. Brumaire
- Current curse: cannot leave the Threshold
- Current state: public pitch describes him as little more than a husk who still rants/weeps in the heart of his former holdfast
- Current exact motives, combat/social options, redemption possibilities and stats: pending v1.3 PDF

## Ste. Brumaire

- Title: Saint of Boundaries
- Role: historical opponent of Laidre; potential focus of vengeance/religious objective
- Fate: slain in the historical battle that left Laidre cursed
- Church: source pitch confirms adventurers may act on behalf of `the Church`, but exact institution, doctrine and representatives are pending
- Current supernatural presence/relics: pending

## Satyr Bullies

- Role: Laidre's former herd/garrison followers
- Current situation: with Laidre diminished, they found some form of work with/among other Threshold denizens, especially Night Market goblins
- Exact number, hierarchy, attitudes and loyalties: pending

## Night Market goblin merchants

- Role: merchants of impossible/unusual wares
- Currency: not normal coin; source examples include luck, faces, time and talents
- Relationship to Satyr Bullies: employ/use them in some capacity according to public pitch
- Individual merchants, inventories, bargains and transaction consequences: pending

## Boggarts

- Confirmed source role: Boggart magic enchants the stone entrance and controls the nightly opening/daytime sealing cycle
- Whether Boggarts are physically present, organised or involved in the current Threshold economy: pending

## The Church

- Confirmed source concept: a party may come to avenge Ste. Brumaire on behalf of the Church
- Name, structure, doctrine, representative NPC and reward: pending

## Faction-state requirement for DMOS

After PDF extraction, track independently:
- Laidre disposition and power
- Satyr Bullies loyalties/jobs
- Night Market merchant attitudes and debts
- Church/Ste. Brumaire mission status
- Boggart/access conditions

A deal with one group must not automatically rewrite the motives of the others.
