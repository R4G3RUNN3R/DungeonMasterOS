# Castle Inspection

Status: `researching`

## Executive summary

`Castle Inspection` is a system-neutral, comedic fantasy one-page dungeon by Internal Rock (Martin and Alex). The player characters are undead heroes working for the lich Radagast. An Imperial insurance inspector has arrived without warning while Radagast's castle is already suffering multiple simultaneous disasters. The party's job is to keep the inspector alive, impressed, and unaware of violations while fixing or concealing escalating crises throughout the castle.

The adventure placed second in the 2025 One Page Dungeon Contest. The official 2025 submission rules require every accepted submission to be original and released under CC BY-SA 4.0, making this a strong candidate for a DungeonMasterOS adaptation as long as attribution and ShareAlike obligations are preserved.

## Research state

The creator's itch.io page, public Patreon post, development logs, official contest rules, and several creator/community Reddit threads have been captured. Those sources expose the premise, named NPCs, broad crisis types, several section headings, version history, and community feedback.

The complete one-page PDF itself has not yet been fully extracted in this research environment. Files in this folder therefore distinguish confirmed source facts from pending room-by-room extraction. No missing room details, solutions, checks, monsters, timings, or outcomes are invented.

## DMOS suitability

High. The scenario naturally maps to a stateful AI-DM graph because each crisis can progress independently while the inspector moves through the castle. Free-text actions can be adjudicated against crisis state, inspector knowledge, deception success, time pressure, and damage to the castle without changing the adventure's core objective.
