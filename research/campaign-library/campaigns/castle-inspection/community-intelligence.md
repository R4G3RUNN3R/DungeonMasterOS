# Castle Inspection - Community intelligence

Community observations are not source canon. They are recorded because they can identify usability problems and useful DMOS adaptation strategies.

## Positive reception

The creator's r/osr thread received strong engagement. Commenters particularly praised:
- the central insurance-inspection premise
- the name/concept of Ignatius the Intransigent
- artwork and layout
- the one-page format being genuinely runnable rather than merely compressed
- the comedic crisis-management tone

This matters for DMOS curation because the appeal appears to be the scenario structure itself, not dependence on a specific rules system.

## Participation risk: constant party splitting

A commenter on the r/DnD creator thread raised a concrete table-play concern: if the adventure requires the group to remain split throughout, individual players may spend too much time outside the currently active scene.

Suggested community alternative:
- allow the party to address some problems together before the inspector reaches them
- later have the group accompany/deceive the inspector, splitting only when necessary

Creator response:
- the party-splitting pressure was intentional because of the contest theme
- the problems could instead be made non-escalating so players can solve them pre-emptively before the inspector arrives

DMOS implication:
- preserve the original simultaneous-crisis mode as the canonical/default presentation
- consider an optional `cohesive-party` pacing mode as a labelled DMOS adaptation, not a rewrite of source canon

## Participation risk: escort-PC sidelining

A commenter in the r/onednd creator thread suggested that an NPC, rather than a player character, could occupy Ignatius and announce where he is headed. That leaves every PC free to intervene.

The creator responded positively to the idea of a slow zombie worker escorting the inspector around the castle.

DMOS implication:
- an optional `slow-escort` mode could make turn allocation easier in asynchronous/AI play
- if implemented, it should be explicitly marked as an adaptation based on creator-acknowledged community feedback

## Proofreading / version history

The r/osr thread contains a detailed proofreading pass by `u/lukehawksbee`. The creator incorporated the corrections and published v1.2. The v1.2 devlog thanks the user directly.

DMOS implication:

- do not preserve known typos from v1.0/v1.1 in the template

## Evidence limitations
