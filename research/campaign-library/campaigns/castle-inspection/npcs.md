# Castle Inspection - NPC register

Only characters supported by current primary sources are listed as canon.

## Radagast

- Type: lich
- Role: castle owner/master; quest giver/employer of the undead heroes
- Current problem: an unannounced insurance inspection during a castle-wide crisis
- Motivation: prevent Ignatius from discovering violations and increasing the insurance cost
- Immediate state: overwhelmed by magically accumulating paperwork in his study
- Known history: the previous inspection ended badly and increased his insurance burden
- Relationship to PCs: summons/employs them to solve the crisis
- Knowledge: knows the inspection is dangerous to his finances and that the castle has multiple active problems
- Full stat/mechanical profile: pending; source is system neutral

- Behaviour: strict, rules-focused, actively checks the castle for infringements
- Relationship to PCs: obstacle rather than necessarily a combatant; the party must keep him alive, impressed, and unaware of problems
