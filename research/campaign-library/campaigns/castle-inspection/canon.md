# Castle Inspection - Canon register

This file separates confirmed campaign truth from later DungeonMasterOS adaptation decisions.

## Hard canon currently confirmed

- The adventure is `Castle Inspection` by Internal Rock.
- The player characters are undead heroes working for/summoned by the lich Radagast.
- Radagast's castle receives an unannounced Imperial insurance inspection.
- The inspector is named Ignatius the Intransigent.
- Ignatius is strict about rules and searches the castle for violations that can increase Radagast's insurance cost.
- Radagast is occupied with a severe paperwork burden during the crisis.
- Radagast assigns or identifies Butler Bones as someone who can help the heroes.
- A previous inspection went badly; Radagast reports that the skeleton he assigned suffered a breakdown and quit, while the insurance policy cost increased.
- At the start of the present scenario multiple serious castle problems are already active.
- Confirmed problems include fire, loose monsters, unraveling rituals, a haunted library, crying monster babies in the cellar, and free-roaming goats.
- The source encourages the party to split up and fix problems quickly.
- The source is system neutral and intended to be playable in one evening.
- A flame elemental can become a phoenix that roams the castle.
- The current known published version is v1.2.

## Conditional canon currently confirmed

The available public sources establish that Ignatius discovers/looks for violations and that unresolved crises threaten the inspection, but they do not yet expose the complete conditional rules. Exact triggers and consequences are therefore pending full-PDF extraction.

## Soft canon / presentation facts

- Tone is intentionally comedic and chaotic.
- Bureaucracy and insurance are central jokes and pressure mechanisms.
- The scenario's design intentionally uses the 2025 `Never Split the Party` theme ironically by encouraging separation.

## Not canon yet

Do **not** treat any of the following as source truth until the full PDF confirms it:
- an exact inspector route
- a numeric violation score
- a fixed time limit in rounds/minutes
- exact room order
- exact crisis escalation timers
- exact outcomes for success/failure
- exact D&D 5e challenge ratings or recommended level
- the slow-zombie escort variant
- pre-emptive non-escalating crisis mode

The last two are creator-acknowledged community/adaptation ideas, not established original adventure rules in the currently extracted source set.
