# Heron Watch - Canon register

Target source variant: Weeping Stag CC BY-SA 4.0 blog-text edition, 2026-01-09.

## Hard historical canon

- Heron Guard were magically bonded knights/riders partnered with Great Herons.
- Heron Watch was their headquarters/order site and was considered lost for a very long time.
- Great Herons became mythological/legendary to later people.
- Marchguard traditions are based on stories about the ancient Heron Guard.
- Merithon the Petro-Lich was an enemy of the Heron Guard.
- Merithon created a magical prion wasting disease that killed Great Herons.
- Bonded Heron Guard died from grief several days after their partners died.
- Disease is extinct in the present source.
- Pilot Dominic was the last Heron Guard and died beside his Great Heron Lenore.
- Three Great Heron eggs survived through magical preservation.
- One living Great Heron remains in the present.

## Hard present-day canon

- Humans rediscovered Heron Watch roughly an hour before the source's current situation.
- Four living noble claimants hold a moot over ownership and right to refound the order.
- Sir Carl, who called the moot, is dead.
- Carl died because Area 2's speaking/disorientation trap caused him to fall; Galahad did not murder him.
- Giovanni is a murderer and plans further claimant killings.
- Ibn Rashid found the site and regrets bringing the nobles.
- Ibn deliberately hides the vow poster from nobles.
- White Ape Cultists discovered Heron Watch approximately one month ago.
- White Ape Cultists worship Merithon, kidnap victims and corrupt the Power Gem through blood sacrifice.
- Merithon remains magically sealed in his tomb but can communicate through holographic stone.
- Heron Guard ghosts haunt the compound.
- The Power Gem supplies heat/power and can catastrophically overload.

## Heron Vow hard rules

- Vow is a discoverable source ritual, not an automatic player ability.
- Full ritual requires correct words and ceremonial method.
- Initiate kneels before Great Heron (or Guard when no Great Heron available) and lays down/offers Heron Lance.
- Valid bond links rider/heron health/wellbeing, grants human-level intelligence to heron and allows psychic communication over any distance.
- Dominic's ghost does not accept murderers and will not entrust eggs to them.

## Hard item/facility rules

- Heron Lance functions as magical weapon and multiple Heron Guard lock/ritual credential.
- Flightsuit protects from extreme temperatures and counts as Heron Guard gear.
- Area 16 immolation trap has three known source bypasses: lance kata, Flightsuit, or Area 7 control.
- Area 22 Hidden Library requires three Heron Lances to open.
- Area 7 controls facility heat/power/doors/trap but labels are unreliable until lever chart recovered.
- Power Gem threshold at 7 sacrifices causes major shaking and reveals tunnel; 14 sacrifices causes approximately 10-mile catastrophic blast.
- Holographic stone remains trackable by Merithon.

## Truth/rumour locks

### Noble hereditary claims

Claimants say they descend from Heron Guard. Source deliberately says it is unclear whether claims are true **or whether bloodline matters**. AI must not validate genealogy as objective truth unless later player evidence establishes it.

### Galahad accusation

Nobles suspect Galahad killed Carl. This is false source rumour. AI must preserve discoverable evidence rather than randomly changing murderer.

### Radio distress calls

Signals from Doomsday Shelter and spaceship may sound active if radios powered. Source explicitly leaves whether those people remain alive ambiguous. They are sequel hooks, not proof of survivors.

## Player-dependent conditional canon

- Great Heron disposition can improve through Flight Song and valid vow.
- Dominic can entrust eggs only to acceptable caretakers/vow-takers and never murderers.
- Glass-feather trap stops if stolen Guard gear is returned.
- Facility lighting/temperature/doors/trap can change through Area 7 controls.
- Hidden Library becomes accessible when opening method and three lances are obtained.
- Cult sacrifice progression changes based on rescue/interference.
- No-intervention failure chain is default only while dependencies remain valid.

## Persistent soft canon / tone

- Heron Watch mixes bleak pre-Collapse magical-military archaeology with deliberate absurdist satire.
- Ancient order has aviation/pilot aesthetic expressed through hangar, radio, flight gear, lances, propaganda and Great Heron riders.
- Nobles are petty, selfish and frequently ridiculous rather than a dignified knightly council.
- White Ape Cultists are dangerous but capable of speech/negotiation/deception; they are not mindless combat tokens.

## AI forbidden deviations

The AI DM must not:
- make Galahad Carl's murderer;
- make disease still contagious unless player action/source sequel causes new condition;
- let Merithon freely appear in person inside Heron Watch while he remains sealed;
- let Laidre/other unrelated campaign lore leak into Heron Watch;
- allow vow bond without its source conditions;
- let murderer pass Dominic's vow judgement merely because of high Charisma roll;
- invent extra Great Heron eggs;
- remove catastrophe clock without player action;
- treat White Apes as incapable of negotiation;
- erase current noble agendas when party changes rooms;
- alter fixed room topology to help a desired scene happen;
- reveal hidden-library location from Dominic's diary, which only gives opening method;
- turn ambiguous sequel radio callers into confirmed survivors prematurely;
- rewrite no-intervention timeline as mandatory if PCs invalidate its dependencies.
