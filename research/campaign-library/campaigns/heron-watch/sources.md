## System dependencies

Heron Watch is written for Cairn 2E. Several creature stat blocks are based on or reproduced/adapted from the Cairn 2E Warden's Guide. The final DMOS package must preserve all required Cairn attribution/licence notices for those source mechanics in addition to Heron Watch attribution.

## Author/design context

The creator says the dungeon was written as practice using Cairn 2E dungeon-generation tools and as a more serious thematic module than their usual comedy work, though deliberate jokes remain. The magical-knight concept was inspired by early pilots, with the headquarters imagined as a bleak concrete bunker.

These authorial comments inform tone but do not override keyed campaign facts.
