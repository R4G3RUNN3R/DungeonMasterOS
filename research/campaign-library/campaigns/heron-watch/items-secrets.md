# Heron Watch - Items, rituals, clues and secrets

## Heron Lance

Source mechanical identity:
- magical machined-aluminum lance with feather engraving
- Cairn damage: d10
- bypasses supernatural-creature immunity in source

Campaign functions:
- valid offering/key object for Heron Vow initiation
- opens multiple Heron Guard locks, including Area 7, Area 15, Area 16 and Area 11-style lance-key mechanisms where specified
- three lances are required to open Area 22 Hidden Library
- several exist in the dungeon: Area 8 crate, Area 9 locker, Area 12 cultist, Area 22 stock, Giovanni carries one

AI rule: a Heron Lance is both weapon and credential/key. Do not reduce it to generic magic spear behaviour.

## Flightsuit

Source mechanical identity:
- woven from Great Heron feathers
- Cairn armor 1
- protects against extreme temperature
- genuine suits are valuable to collectors

Campaign functions:
- counts as Heron Guard gear and may provoke magical/security reactions when worn/stolen by non-Guard
- Area 9 glass-feather security reacts to unauthorised removal
- one method of bypassing Area 16's heat trap
- White Ape Cultists use stolen suits as lures in surrounding mountains
- source locations include Area 9, nine suits in Area 18, four pristine suits in Area 22; Quixote wears one

## Heron Vow and bond ritual

The actual vow text is source copyrighted but CC licensed; DMOS should store its exact text only in the licensed template data where attribution/ShareAlike is satisfied. Research summary:
- oath commits initiate to reverence for life, sky-freedom and humble service
- must be taken kneeling before a Great Heron, or a Heron Guard if no heron is available
- Heron Lance is laid down/offered during ceremony
- valid Great-Heron initiation forms instant magical bond, links wellbeing, grants human-level intelligence to heron and long-distance psychic communication
- murderers are rejected by Dominic's ghost and cannot receive eggs from him

### Distributed ritual clues

**Vow wording can be learned from:**
- Area 3 record/Flight Song
- Ibn Rashid's stolen propaganda poster in Area 13
- Area 22 Hidden Library
- burial inscriptions in Area 15 also preserve the vow wording

**Ceremonial procedure can be learned from:**
- Area 10 imagery around fireplace
- Dominic's diary in Area 12
- Area 15 Crypt
- Area 22 Hidden Library

This must be modelled as a clue-combination system. Knowing words alone is not necessarily equivalent to knowing full ritual procedure.

## Heron Guard Flight Song

- ethnomusicological recording in Area 3 if power is available
- uses vow wording as lyrics/text
- calms last Great Heron
- can be a path to friendly contact without immediate combat

## Power Gem

Area 20.
- massive magical power source for Heron Watch
- controls heat/power functions
- source says extraordinarily valuable to wizards/magic-users
- nobles become violent if they learn its value
- cultist blood sacrifice corrupts/overloads it
- 7-sacrifice threshold causes major structural tremor and reveals tunnel
- 14-sacrifice threshold causes roughly 10-mile catastrophic blast
- output controlled via Area 7

AI rule: cannot be pocketed like ordinary gem. It is facility-scale infrastructure and campaign timer.

## Holographic stone

Area 21.
- paired magical remote-image communication device
- permits bearer to project holographic image through paired stone
- Merithon always knows this stone's location
- creates risk if PCs steal/use it; must maintain tracking consequence

## Great Heron eggs

Area 17.
- three magically time-preserved eggs
- guarded by Dominic's ghost
- may be entrusted to suitable caretakers or valid vow-takers
- never entrusted to murderers
- represent restoration of Heron Guard/Great Heron legacy, not generic treasure

## Great Heron feathers

- Source says feathers are magical and master craftsperson can make Flightsuit.
- Area 23 stores Heron feathers; live heron also has feathers.
- Do not allow trivial mass harvesting without source/adjudication consequences.

## Propaganda posters

Area 3 initially has five remaining posters; one vow poster is held by Ibn Rashid.
- written in pre-Collapse language
- require scholarly translation time
- individual posters have collector value
- reveal Merithon conflict, Heron Guard culture, old war propaganda and vow text

## Lever chart and Power Gem schematics

Area 22.
- complete accurate lever chart solves Area 7 ambiguity
- Power Gem schematics provide deep technical/magical knowledge

Potential DMOS consequence: after discovery, lever interactions can become deterministic rather than blind/unknown.

## Radio distress signals

Not portable item, but persistent clue system in Area 3 after power restoration:
- repeated aid calls from nearby Doomsday Shelter
- calls from spaceship in orbit
- source truth intentionally ambiguous about survivors
- classic-rock station also exists

AI rule: distress calls create optional future hooks; do not confirm living survivors without later campaign content.

## Area 23 map

Reveals three sequel locations:
- Merithon's underground tomb/castle
- Doctor Dastardly's decaying volcanic-island lair with Quantum Shark haunting
- theorised underground observatory beneath ancient dam with telescope into `The Roots`

These should be written to campaign-world memory as **known map leads**, not spawned immediately as mandatory quests.

## Murder/identity secrets

- Galahad did **not** murder Carl.
- Carl died due to Area 2 disorientation trap causing fall.
- Carl himself was a murderer, making him visible in Revealing Pool if applicable and relevant to later wight state.
- Giovanni is a murderer and intends more murders.
- Quixote is not counted as murderer by pool because source treats him as not responsible due to insanity.
- Cherubino is secretly a woman.
- Ibn Rashid intentionally hides vow poster from nobles.

## Historical secrets

- Merithon caused the Great Heron wasting disease.
- Heron Guard deaths followed partners' deaths through grief/bond.
- Disease is extinct in present source.
- White Apes discovered Heron Watch only one month ago.
- present day marks their first claimed/sacrificial victim and the beginning of critical escalation.
