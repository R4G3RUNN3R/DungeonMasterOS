# Heron Watch - Source adventure hooks

The open source provides six distinct entry hooks. DungeonMasterOS should preserve them as selectable campaign starts rather than collapsing them into one canonical reason for entering Heron Watch.

## Hook A - Retrieve Sir Galahad

- Employer: Galahad's unnamed noble father.
- Goal: find/retrieve Galahad before his obsession with Great Herons gets him killed.
- Source reward: 1000 gold.
- Starting relationship: party is not necessarily tied to any claimant faction.
- Natural choice pressure: protect Galahad, investigate his murder accusation, decide whether to help his claim.

## Hook B - Alleged Heron Guard heir

- One PC is allegedly a noble heir to the Heron Guard legacy and has been invited to the moot.
- Other PCs travel as entourage.
- Party arrives late after a holiday-related delay/hangover.
- Source deliberately does not prove that the hereditary claim is true or that ancestry grants legitimate right.
- Natural choice pressure: enter noble succession dispute while potentially discovering that merit/vow matters more than blood.

## Hook C - Flightsuit collector commission

- Employer: unnamed collector of oddities.
- Goal: obtain authentic Heron Guard Flightsuit or credible reproduction/pattern.
- Source rewards: 5000 gold for a suit; 15000 for manufacturing pattern.
- Natural choice pressure: theft/recovery of Guard artefacts can trigger security, ghost hostility and moral conflict with legacy restoration.

## Hook D - Criminal absolution through Ancient Laws

- PCs are fugitives for a crime the source explicitly says they actually committed.
- Ancient Laws supposedly forgive past crimes of Heron Guard members.
- Party seeks Heron Vow as route to legal/personal salvation.
- Four Marchguard pursue them, roughly one day behind.
- Important moral rule: Dominic's ghost refuses murderers, so not every criminal PC can necessarily qualify if their crime was murder.
- Natural choice pressure: find ritual, prove worth, possibly confront conflict between legal loophole and actual ethical vow.

## Hook E - Mountain sighting investigation

- Village elders hear rumours/reports of Heron Guard sightings in mountains.
- PCs are sent to investigate.
- This can connect naturally to White Apes wearing stolen Flightsuits to lure victims.
- Natural choice pressure: determine whether sightings are true Great Heron/Guard, cultist deception, or both.

## Hook F - Follow the bird

- PCs see an interesting/remarkable bird and follow it to Heron Watch.
- It flies over the compound toward Area 15 Crypt.
- Minimal-prep exploration hook with no employer/reward obligation.

## DMOS hook-state requirements

Selected hook must persist throughout campaign and influence suggested choices:
- Galahad retrieval should keep his survival/objective visible.
- heir hook should track claim/evidence and noble politics.
- collector hook should track item/pattern acquisition and employer payout.
- criminal hook should track Marchguard pursuit and eligibility for vow.
- village hook should track factual resolution of sightings.
- bird hook permits pure exploration with no imposed employer.

Free-text play may create new motives after entry, but original contract/debt/pursuit does not disappear merely because the party finds a more interesting room.
