# Heron Watch

Status: `extracted` (blog-text edition), DMOS conversion/QA pending

## Executive summary

`Heron Watch` is a Cairn 2E dungeon module by Clark / Weeping Stag. The author publishes the complete text of a web/blog edition under **CC BY-SA 4.0**, while noting that the illustrated DriveThruRPG/itch editions differ slightly after layout changes. DungeonMasterOS can therefore treat the blog-text edition as a fully available source variant and keep its provenance distinct from the commercial-layout variant.

Heron Watch is an ancient concrete-and-aluminum mountain bunker once used by the **Heron Guard**, magical knights psychically bonded to enormous Great Herons. The order vanished before a civilisation-ending Collapse. Their lost headquarters has just been rediscovered by humans.

The site is now simultaneously occupied by:
- four competing noble claimants trying to prove hereditary ownership of the Heron Guard legacy;
- historian/mountaineer **Ibn Rashid**, who wants to understand the order's fall and does not trust the nobles;
- **White Ape Cultists**, who are secretly kidnapping victims and feeding blood to a magical Power Gem for the imprisoned Petro-Lich **Merithon**;
- ghosts of the dead Heron Guard;
- the last surviving Great Heron;
- three magically preserved Great Heron eggs.

The central historical truth is that Merithon created a wasting prion curse/disease that killed the Great Herons. Their bonded riders then died from grief soon afterward. The White Ape Cultists now seek to overload/corrupt the Power Gem; after enough sacrifices it destroys the site and helps Merithon break the seal on his tomb.

The dungeon contains **23 numbered areas plus sub-area 1A**, several secret Ape Tunnel connections, multiple interacting traps, a dynamic event table, faction conflicts, a murder mystery around Sir Carl, a recoverable initiation ritual (the Heron Vow), and explicit source-defined consequences if the PCs fail to intervene.

## Why this is a flagship DMOS template candidate

Heron Watch provides nearly every state type the campaign engine needs:
- fixed dungeon topology
- multiple source hooks
- competing NPC goals
- truth-vs-rumour/murder evidence
- discoverable ritual knowledge distributed across rooms
- timed captives and sacrifices
- global facility power/temperature states
- roaming encounters and environmental events
- source-defined NPC deaths when players do nothing
- multiple ways to disable traps
- optional magical bonding/legacy restoration
- artefacts that react differently depending on character status
- catastrophic failure with downstream campaign hooks

## Player-facing level policy

The source is Cairn 2E and does not publish a D&D 5e level. A DMOS 5e level recommendation must be produced from an explicit conversion pass rather than guessed from the words `dungeon` or `knight`. Until then, the template is source-extracted but not player-catalogue-ready for 5e.
