# Heron Watch - Complete keyed location extraction (CC blog-text edition)

This file paraphrases the complete keyed web text. Where the prose does not state an exact map connection, adjacency remains `map-pending` rather than inferred.

## Global construction

- **Heron Watch:** ancient mountain compound/bunker of poured concrete and thick aluminum fittings.
- **Normal lighting:** dark unless facility electricity is restored via Area 7; non-Ape areas have fluorescent strips in floor/ceiling.
- **Ape Tunnels:** originally carved by early Heron Guard, now occupied by White Ape Cultists.
- **Power Gem:** Area 20 powers heat/electric systems and can catastrophically overload through sacrifice.

## Area 1 - Revealing Pool

- 10-foot-deep poured-concrete pool, unnaturally bright blue and warm from Power Gem heat.
- Magical property: reflects **murderers only**.
- Explicit views/connections: wide stairs to 1A; small stairs to Area 5; Area 4 balcony visible; Area 9 hangar visible.
- Social clue: nobles in Area 4 argue about whether Galahad killed Carl.
- Dynamic event: pool can boil and fill nearby space with obscuring mist.
- Dynamic event: if Carl's corpse becomes a wight/possessed body, the pool can turn red and bloody tracks lead here.

## Area 1A - Heron Guard Mural

- Landing with ancient pre-Collapse mural and two Great Heron statues.
- One statue remains intact and is secretly polished daily by a Heron Guard ghost; the other was crushed by a fallen pillar.
- Mural depicts heroic Heron Guard deeds and the order's silence/service identity.
- A secret-door-shaped opening leads to Area 2.

## Area 2 - Hall of Reverence

- Stone-tiled, Heron-decorated hall.
- Polished aluminum door leads to Area 3.
- **Disorientation trap:** speaking triggers magical confusion/panic. The victim may run into danger and trigger dungeon events, or safely wander outside depending on the source die result.
- Historical clue: Sir Carl triggered this trap, became disoriented and fell from Area 4, causing his death.

## Area 3 - Radio Room

- Dusty room with pre-Collapse propaganda posters, ham radio gear and record player.
- Secret floor tile opens into Ape Tunnels.
- Five propaganda posters originally existed; Ibn Rashid removed the vow poster and holds it in Area 13.
- Posters can be translated by scholarly characters and reveal Heron Guard/Merithon/pre-Collapse propaganda context.
- If facility power is restored, radios carry repeated distress calls from a nearby Doomsday Shelter and a spaceship in orbit; whether those callers remain alive is explicitly ambiguous. Another station endlessly broadcasts classic rock.
- Powered record player contains an ethnomusicological recording of the Heron Guard Flight Song set to the words of the Heron Vow.
- **Ritual clue:** provides the vow text through the recording.

## Area 4 - Heron Court

- Open-air amphitheatre formerly used for trials/debates of Heron Guard.
- Great Heron perches remain above.
- Four wanna-be-knights and their noble entourages hold a hostile moot over ownership/right to refound the order.
- All claim Heron Guard ancestry; source says truth/relevance is unclear.
- Each offers extravagant reward for help validating their claim; only Galahad is actually able to pay. Giovanni plans to murder PCs after receiving help.
- Major NPCs here: Almaviva, Quixote, Giovanni, Galahad; Carl is dead at 5A.
- Fifteen courtiers present, historically three per claimant including Carl's group. Carl's entourage intends to leave within an hour; remaining group later goes to dinner at Area 10.

## Area 5 - Ancient Garden

- Overgrown garden with banana tree, palm/coconut tree, iris boxes and tall grass.
- 25 well-bred horses graze here; valuable and stealable.
- One ancient stubborn donkey roams here.
- Coconut tree has three remaining coconuts and damage from Quixote striking it.
- Banana tree has been stripped by White Ape Cultists.
- Area 5A contains Carl's corpse.

## Area 5A - Sir Carl's corpse

- Carl died after Area 2's disorientation trap caused him to fall from Area 4.
- A Heron Guard ghost may possess/reanimate his body as a wight seeking vengeance on nobles who disrespect the dead.
- This is a key murder-mystery correction: Galahad did not kill Carl.

## Area 6 - Infirmary

- Medical facility for Great Herons and riders.
- Contains old medical records and probably spoiled medical supplies.
- Records establish that each Heron Guard died several days after their bonded Great Heron died.
- Historical truth: Great Herons suffered a magical prion wasting disease created by Merithon; disease is now extinct.

## Area 7 - Room With Too Many Levers

- Locked control room, opened by Heron Lance.
- Dozens of poorly/incorrectly labelled controls; the useful original chart was lost.
- **Power Gem control:** raises/lower heat/output; pushing beyond warning threshold can drive White Ape Cultists out of tunnels toward Area 10.
- **Hangar control:** operates Area 9 doors.
- **Electricity control:** turns facility fluorescent lighting on/off.
- **Crypt control:** operates crypt ceiling/iris system, but labelled misleadingly.
- **Immolation trap control:** controls Area 16 lethal heat trap and begins in active/on state.
- Random environment events can cause nobles to manipulate controls or trigger facility alarms/power effects.
- Complete accurate lever chart is recoverable in Area 22.

## Area 8 - Staging / Supply Room

- Heron Guard and noble-entourage supply crates.
- Western wall blocked by collapse; Area 20 cannot be seen from this side.
- Secret passage hidden behind heavy Heron Guard crates.
- One Heron Guard crate hides a Heron Lance.
- Noble crates contain mundane clothing/food plus hidden valuable jewellery.

## Area 9 - Hangar

- Arched aluminum hangar open to outside through large bay.
- Magical glass feathers hover near ceiling and produce musical tones in wind.
- Ten flightlockers; one contains Dominic's aged/bloodied Flightsuit and Heron Lance.
- **Glass-feather security:** non-Heron-Guard theft of Flightsuit/Lance triggers pursuing cutting feathers; returning items stops attack.
- Hangar bay is controllable from Area 7.
- North wall blocked by collapse; Area 20 beyond is not directly visible.

## Area 10 - Mess Hall

- Dining room lit by Power-Gem-fed fluorescents and a fireplace.
- Nobles' staff prepare a large feast/paella.
- Named staff: Susanna, Figaro, Cherubino, Leporello, Sancho Panza.
- Susanna and Figaro are engaged and seek a way to humiliate/stop Almaviva's predatory plan.
- Cherubino is secretly a woman and rapidly becomes infatuated with people.
- Leporello serves Giovanni.
- Sancho Panza serves Quixote and wants out of the relationship but fears his erratic behaviour.
- Trapdoor/secret opening leads to basement Ape Tunnels.
- Heron Vow ceremonial details are depicted around the fireplace (ritual-clue location).
- Pushing Power Gem heat from Area 7 can force White Ape Cultists from tunnels into this room.

## Area 11 - Treasury

- Locked aluminum secure room; large circular keyhole.
- Can be opened with Heron Lance or by lockpicking with source fatigue cost.
- Five large heron-pattern porcelain vases, one broken.
- Vases/contents contain 10,000 pre-Collapse aluminum coins total.
- White Ape Cultists have repurposed some coins into crude aluminum weapons.
- Vases themselves are valuable.

## Area 12 - Bunk

- Circular sleeping hall with central heating grate, old Great Heron nests and loft of decayed mattresses, open to sky.
- White Ape Cultists (2d4 source roll) prepare an ambush in mattresses; one carries a Heron Lance.
- Cultists prefer to capture at least one victim alive and pull prisoner through heating grate into Ape Tunnels.
- Listening at grate can reveal nearby ape activity from Area 21.
- **Pilot Dominic's Diary** hidden in bedding: records Heron Guard rituals/daily life, wasting disease, how Area 22's hidden door opens (but not its location), and lever-labelling frustration.
- **Ritual clue:** diary contains ceremonial details of Heron Vow.

## Area 13 - Landing Pad

- Exposed second-floor landing with Great Heron mosaic and broad mountain-region view.
- Ibn Rashid is here.
- He holds the Heron Vow propaganda poster removed from Area 3 to keep the nobles from discovering the ritual.
- He located Heron Watch and was funded by the nobles, but now regrets bringing them because of their behaviour and violence.

## Area 14 - Training Hall

- Covered balcony extending over steep cliff with regional views and Great Heron perches.
- Walls show deteriorated instructions for a complex Heron Lance kata.
- Performing the kata is one source-defined method of bypassing Area 16's immolation trap.
- Entrance to Area 15 is through locked polished aluminum doors.

## Area 15 - Crypt

- Locked using Heron Lance key mechanism.
- Holds coffins/urns of generations of Heron Guard and their steeds.
- Every burial inscription records the Heron Vow.
- Pilot Dominic's corpse rests with skeleton of his Great Heron partner Lenore; Dominic died of grief after Lenore's wasting death.
- **Last living Great Heron:** probabilistically present/away. Flight Song calms her; valid Heron Vow can bond with her, giving intelligence/speech/psychic rider bond according to ritual.
- Open-sky iris ceiling can be closed from Area 7.
- Ritual-clue location for both vow text and ceremonial practice.

## Area 16 - Incubator Entrance

- Locked aluminum disc/door decorated with Heron imagery and opened with Heron Lance.
- Leads to Area 17.
- **Immolation trap:** lethal heat activates when approaching wall; source methods to bypass include correctly performing lance kata, wearing Flightsuit, or deactivating via Area 7.
- Ape Tunnel entrance exists beneath balcony ledge and can be spotted from cliff approach.

## Area 17 - Magic Incubator / Eggs

- Heated by magical lamp powered by Area 20.
- Contains **three magically preserved Great Heron eggs**.
- Freshly dead White Ape shows Pilot Dominic's ghost actively defends the eggs.
- Dominic's ghost attacks people using Heron gear without taking/accepting the vow.
- He may entrust eggs to characters capable of rearing them or valid vow-takers.
- He never entrusts eggs to murderers and will not accept a murderer into the Heron Vow.
- Generations of Guard initiations occurred here.

## Area 18 - Flightsuit pile

- Nine Flightsuits in varied condition, including one bloodied from White Ape theft.
- Cultists have been wearing stolen Flightsuits around the mountains to lure potential sacrifices into Heron Watch.

## Area 19 - Emptied Storage Cavern

- Cultists discard bodies and belongings of sacrificed victims here.
- Functions as evidence of ongoing sacrifice operation.

## Area 20 - Power Gem

- Massive floating/rotating/humming white gem in cavern.
- Powers parts of Heron Watch and currently supplies substantial heat.
- Output controlled from Area 7.
- Southern wall collapsed; source explicitly identifies tunnel connections toward Areas 16 and 8.
- Extremely valuable to magic-users; nobles may turn violent if they learn its value.
- **Sacrifice track:** White Ape Cultists pour victims' blood on gem.
  - At 7 sacrifices: structure trembles/shakes and reveals Ape Tunnel passage near Area 16.
  - At 14 sacrifices: gem goes critical, destroying everything within roughly ten miles.
- This is the campaign's primary catastrophic timer.

## Area 21 - Ape Camp

- White Ape Cultist base with bananas, crude aluminum weapons and Heron Guard records.
- Records were abused as mundane waste material but collectively reveal locations of other Heron Guard strongholds.
- **Holographic stone:** paired magical communication device; Merithon appears through it and always knows this stone's location.
- Merithon is magically sealed in his tomb. Successful cult sacrifices here/elsewhere can ultimately free him to rebuild an army.
- Explicit spatial relationship: directly beneath Area 1.

## Area 22 - Hidden Library

- Hidden behind ordinary brickwork.
- Opening method requires **three Heron Lances**; Dominic's diary explains how but not where library is.
- Contains complete Heron Guard enrolment/tradition/procedure records.
- Contains Power Gem schematics and the long-lost accurate lever chart.
- Contains four pristine Flightsuits and four Heron Lances.
- Ritual-clue location for complete vow and ceremony.

## Area 23 - Storage

- Bird feed, Great Heron feathers, long-lasting packaged food and traces of old private drinking/party use by the Guard.
- Wall map reveals three larger campaign hooks:
  - Merithon's castle/tomb, now fallen underground;
  - volcanic-island lair of Doctor Dastardly, decaying and haunted by a Quantum Shark;
  - theorised underground observatory beneath an ancient dam, with a telescope that can see into `The Roots`.
- These are valid sequel/world-extension hooks, not areas inside the present dungeon.
