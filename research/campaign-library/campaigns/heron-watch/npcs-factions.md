# Heron Watch - NPC and faction extraction

## Historical faction: Heron Guard

- Ancient magical knight order bonded to Great Herons.
- Defended humanity in ancient times and vanished decades before the Collapse.
- Heron Watch was their lost headquarters.
- Marchguard culture was later modelled on stories about them.
- Core vow emphasises reverence for life, freedom of the sky and humble service.
- Valid initiation requires kneeling before a Great Heron (or a Heron Guard if no heron is available) and offering a Heron Lance while taking the vow.
- A valid bond links rider/heron wellbeing, grants human-level intelligence to the Great Heron and enables long-distance psychic speech.
- Historical downfall: Merithon created a magical prion wasting disease that killed the Great Herons; bonded Guard died from grief soon after.
- Ghosts of the order still haunt the compound.

### Pilot Dominic

- Last member of the historical order in the source timeline.
- Famous pre-Collapse pilot/ace in propaganda.
- Bonded Great Heron: **Lenore**.
- Died of grief beside Lenore after she succumbed to Merithon's disease.
- Body and Lenore's skeleton lie in Area 15.
- Diary in Area 12 records rituals, daily life, the disease, hidden-library opening method and lever frustrations.
- Ghost in Area 17 guards the three surviving eggs.
- Rejects murderers from the vow and will not entrust eggs to them.
- Attacks unauthorised people using Heron Guard gear unless they submit to valid initiation.

### Lenore

- Great Heron bonded to Pilot Dominic.
- Died from Merithon's wasting disease.
- Skeleton lies with Dominic in Area 15.

### Last living Great Heron

- Final surviving Great Heron in current source.
- Nests in Area 15 but may be temporarily away.
- Calmed by the Heron Guard Flight Song.
- Can form a valid Heron Vow bond; once bonded, gains human-level intelligence/speech/psychic connection.
- Name not provided in blog-text source. DMOS must not invent a permanent source-canon name.

### Three Great Heron eggs

Not NPCs yet, but future living legacy. Magically preserved in Area 17 and defended by Dominic's ghost.

## Antagonistic faction: White Ape Cultists

- Discovered Heron Watch about one month before present events.
- Occupy Ape Tunnels and Area 21.
- Worship **Merithon the Petro-Lich**.
- Kidnap unwitting victims and sacrifice them by pouring blood onto the Power Gem.
- First victim is claimed on the current day; source events can accelerate further kidnappings/sacrifices.
- Can speak and negotiate; source explicitly says they will lie to gain PCs' trust.
- Use crude weapons made from scavenged aluminum coins and stolen Heron Lances.
- Have stolen Flightsuits and worn them in surrounding mountains to lure sacrifices into the compound.
- Use Heron Guard records as disposable material, accidentally retaining valuable historical location data.

### Merithon, the Petro-Lich

- Eschatonic Petro-Lich and enemy of ancient Heron Guard.
- Created the magical prion wasting disease that destroyed Great Herons and indirectly killed their bonded riders.
- Recently awakened from slumber but remains magically sealed in his tomb.
- Communicates with cultists through a paired holographic stone in Area 21.
- Always knows location of the stone.
- If cultists succeed at Heron Watch, or other minions sacrifice powerful artefacts in his name, he can break his seal and rebuild an army of darkness.
- Area 23 map gives location of his underground/fallen castle/tomb as sequel hook.

## Noble claimant faction: wanna-be-knights

Four living nobles currently participate in a moot to claim Heron Watch and the right to refound the Heron Guard. All claim ancestral connection, but the source deliberately leaves truth/relevance uncertain. They disrespect the dead and argue over ownership/history.

### Sir Galahad

- Attractive, confident, naive/himbo presentation.
- Genuine lifelong desire to become a knight; source says he would make a good one.
- Accused of Carl's murder but **not a murderer**.
- His father believes he will get himself killed and wants him retrieved (one possible hook pays 1000 gold).
- Unlike other claimants, can actually pay the extravagant reward he promises for help with his claim.
- Source no-intervention timeline: murdered by Giovanni.

### Sir Giovanni

- Don-Juan-style claimant.
- Murderer; plans to kill other knights and claim Heron Watch for his family.
- Carries Heron Lance.
- Will betray/murder PCs after they help establish his claim.
- Source events can show him after another claimant murder.
- Source no-intervention timeline: kills Galahad and Ibn Rashid; later is killed by Carl's wight in the Crypt immediately before completing/securing the vow/claim.
- Note: final source line spells `Sir Giovanna`; dossier treats this as a likely typo referring to Giovanni unless later edition proves otherwise.

### Sir Almaviva

- Married into nobility.
- Wants to refound Heron Guard partly to resurrect Ancient Laws and exploit them for sexual access to Susanna.
- Employer of Susanna, Figaro and (currently) Cherubino.
- Source no-intervention timeline: killed by White Ape Cultists.

### Sir Quixote

- Mad/unstable older claimant.
- Revealing Pool does not count him as murderer because source treats him as not of sound mind.
- Wears a Flightsuit.
- Has been striking the coconut tree in Area 5.
- Frequently fights inanimate objects in random-sign/event material.
- Squire: Sancho Panza.
- Source no-intervention timeline: killed by Area 16 immolation trap after lending Flightsuit to Sancho.

### Sir Carl

- Young claimant who called the moot.
- Descended from Heron Guard according to source.
- Was a murderer.
- Dead at start/current discovery: he spoke in Area 2, became magically disoriented and fell from Area 4.
- His death is wrongly blamed on Galahad by the nobles.
- A Heron Guard ghost may possess/reanimate his body as a wight to punish disrespectful claimants.

### Noble entourage

- Fifteen nameless courtiers associated with claimants in source setup.
- Produce weak/contingent genealogical evidence for their patrons.
- Carl's group intends to leave within an hour; others later move to dinner.
- Source no-intervention timeline: killed by White Apes.

## Historian faction / independent actor

### Ibn Rashid

- Historian, archaeologist and mountaineer.
- Correctly theorised location of Heron Watch and achieved a major discovery by finding it.
- Expedition financed by claimant nobles.
- Regrets leading them there because of their disrespect, bickering and violence.
- Removes Heron Vow poster from Area 3 and keeps it at Area 13 specifically to prevent nobles discovering ritual text.
- Investigates true cause of Heron Guard decline.
- Source no-intervention timeline: murdered by Giovanni.

## Noble staff / social subplots

### Susanna

- Betrothed to Figaro.
- Employed by Almaviva.
- Target of Almaviva's attempt to restore an abusive Ancient Law.
- Works with Figaro to humiliate/stop him.
- Can be kidnapped by White Apes in event progression and sacrificed if not rescued.
- Source no-intervention timeline: killed by White Ape Cultists.

### Figaro

- Betrothed to Susanna.
- Employed by Almaviva.
- Collaborates with Susanna against Almaviva.
- Can be kidnapped/sacrificed with Susanna.
- Source no-intervention timeline: killed by White Ape Cultists.

### Cherubino

- Former page of Carl; now works for Almaviva.
- Secretly a woman.
- Source comedic trait: falls in love very easily; a source roll can make a PC immediate target of affection.
- Source no-intervention timeline: survives by stealing the donkey with Leporello after dinner and eloping.

### Leporello

- Retainer/administrator of Giovanni.
- Loyal, administrative and foolish.
- Source no-intervention timeline: survives by eloping with Cherubino on stolen donkey.

### Sancho Panza

- Quixote's squire.
- Has endured years of Quixote's chaotic behaviour, wants to leave but fears him.
- Skilled paella cook.
- Source no-intervention timeline: killed by White Ape Cultists.

## External / hook-only actors

These exist only if their corresponding hook is chosen; source does not give names unless listed.

- Galahad's noble father: offers 1000 gold to retrieve his son.
- Collector of oddities: offers 5000 gold for authentic/good Heron Guard Flightsuit and 15000 for a manufacturing pattern.
- Four Marchguard: pursue criminal PCs in one hook, one day behind.
- Village elders: ask PCs to investigate mountain reports of Heron Guard sightings in one hook.
- Cutpurses: can steal/run off with horses during environmental events.

## Faction-state engine requirements

DungeonMasterOS should track independently:
- each noble claimant's alive/dead status, suspicion, claim evidence and betrayal intentions;
- Ibn Rashid trust and knowledge withholding;
- White Ape sacrifice progress and prisoner list;
- Merithon influence/seal state;
- Ghost/Heron Guard hostility and respect state;
- last Great Heron disposition/bond state;
- Susanna/Figaro/Almaviva social conflict;
- Cherubino/Leporello/Sancho personal states;
- external hook obligations (father, collector, Marchguard, elders).

No faction state should be globally flattened into `friendly/hostile`; several actors can cooperate tactically while holding incompatible long-term goals.
