# Midnight in Bonetown - Community Intelligence

## Verified public reactions

### A Town, A Forest, a Dungeon jam submission feed
The public itch.io jam feed contains positive reader ratings/comments. Two useful observations:
- one reader specifically liked the intelligent-skeleton premise and noted that similar ideas had confused their own players in the past
- another described the adventure as a classic adventure with a strong setting, NPCs and factions

DMOS implication:
- opening narration must establish very clearly that Bonetown skeletons are sapient community members, not conventional hostile undead
- source faction distinctions should be surfaced early so players understand that `skeleton`, `corrupted skeleton`, `Rixi`, and `fungal creature` are different social/ecological categories

## DMOS QA intelligence derived from source structure, not community consensus
Potential table-friction points to test:
1. Players may initially assume all skeletons are enemies.
2. Gebert's 21-day clock can be forgotten in a sandbox unless persisted explicitly.
3. The Rixi/skeleton conflict can be accidentally oversimplified into good-vs-evil.
4. The giant Resurrection Stone stockpile can radically affect campaign economics and worldbuilding if removed from the cavern.
5. Freeing the Red Dragon is a valid solution with potentially enormous collateral consequences.
6. Forest hallucinations must be labelled internally as perceptions so the AI does not accidentally persist them as real world-state.
7. Visual pointcrawl edges should eventually be ingested from the source map; off-path travel currently guarantees source-faithful fallback navigation.

These seven points are QA/adaptation observations, not claims attributed to external reviewers.
