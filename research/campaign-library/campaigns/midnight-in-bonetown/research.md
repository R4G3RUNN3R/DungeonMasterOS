# Midnight in Bonetown - Source-Derived Research

## Historical timeline

### 15 years ago
Gebert and Balstrava discover the cavern and begin exploring it.

### 12 years ago
Balstrava becomes increasingly erratic after fungal/spore infection and retreats into the forest, where her magic and behaviour become dangerous.

### 10 years ago
Gebert discovers Resurrection Stones, uses them to raise skeletons from local ossuaries and begins constructing Bonetown.

### 9 years ago
The Rixi discover Bonetown. Initial trade develops, especially around their eel-powered electrical technology.

### 7 years ago
Rumours reach the city above. Living visitors begin entering Bonetown; the town develops a business strip catering to them.

### 5 years ago
The first resurrected skeletons become fully corrupted by the Resurrection Stones/fungal influence and are expelled into the Fungal Forest. Their attacks on the Rixi destroy Rixi trust in skeletons, and the Rixi begin treating skeletons as enemies.

### 1 week before campaign
Gebert enters the Dim Warrens on another expedition for Resurrection Stones and fails to return. He has been swallowed by a Dim and is being slowly digested.

### 3 weeks after campaign start
If nobody rescues Gebert, he becomes fully digested and can no longer be recovered alive.

## Access to the cavern
The entrance from the overworld appears at midnight in a dank back alley as a magical arched passage opening into a steep, dark descent. Visitors emerge immediately outside Bonetown and see its welcoming neon sign.

The AI must not treat Bonetown as a normal permanently open subterranean gate. Midnight access is a source-defined feature.

## Bonetown
Population is roughly 100 resurrected skeletons. They mostly want to live simple lives and grow their community. They tolerate living visitors partly because visitors create commerce and perform dangerous tasks.

The town's immediate crisis is Gebert's disappearance and the dwindling supply of Resurrection Stones.

### Reward structure
Bonetown offers large rewards for:
- Gebert returned alive
- credible information about the source of the Resurrection Stones if Gebert cannot be recovered
- individual Resurrection Stones

### Internal tension
Resurrection Stones gradually corrupt the skeletons. Early corruption manifests as fungal growth that skeletons may remove or hide. Fully corrupted skeletons become violent and are expelled into the forest. Albert, the armory proprietor, is secretly becoming corrupted and hoarding weapons for a possible skeleton uprising.

This tension is not a guaranteed scripted coup. It is an active faction pressure the AI can advance when time, NPC state or player choices justify it.

## The Fungal Forest
The forest is a large fungal ecosystem threaded by a river full of electric eels and connected by a pointcrawl.

### Travel
- Directly connected point to point: approximately 1 hour.
- Roll Forest Die when traveling between connected points, arriving at a new point, or lingering more than 30 minutes outside protected Rixi villages.
- Off-path travel is explicitly allowed: approximately triple travel time and three Forest Die rolls.

This off-path rule lets DMOS adjudicate unconventional navigation without inventing invisible walls.

### Mycelicardia alert
The Mycelicardia is a massive malicious fungal organism beneath the cavern. Its mycelial network links much of the forest and attempts to expand toward the overworld. Harming a spore-infected creature alerts the network and makes the next forest event more dangerous.

### Spores and hallucination
Giant fungi release parasitic spores. Exposure can damage WIL and cause temporary hallucinations. Hallucination details are random/source-table driven and must not be treated as actual objects or creatures unless separately present.

### Fungus hunting
Characters can deliberately hunt fungi for alchemy/trade. Search outcome depends on a source roll, with optional risk-taking to target specific magical types. Fungi carry type/value information used by Duli and the Rixi economy.

## Factions/ecology

### Bonetown skeletons
Want community growth, Resurrection Stones and Gebert's return. They are vulnerable to slow corruption.

### Corrupted skeletons
Former Bonetown residents changed by Resurrection Stone/fungal corruption into aggressive forest threats. They repeatedly reform if their Resurrection Stones are left in their sockets.

### Rixi
Native cavern people adapted for fungus hunting/mining. They harness electric eels to power tools, lights, fences, transportation and weapons. They once traded with Bonetown but now hate/distrust skeletons because corrupted skeletons attacked them.

PCs are not automatically enemies. Rixi villages admit visitors who swear to help hunt corrupted skeletons and can offer lodging, gadgets, guides and mercenaries.

### Mycelicardia
The deeper fungal network seeks to infect/control increasing numbers of cavern inhabitants and eventually expand into the overworld. Its exact intelligence is alien/ecological rather than a conventional speaking villain.

### Shroom Trolls
Regenerating fungal humanoids. Individual trolls may have their own agendas, including the ferry operator who is magically bound to service and wants another creature to take her place.

### Balstrava
Former associate of Gebert, now heavily spore-affected and erratic. Appears through forest events with changing spellbooks/actions. She trapped both the Water Elemental and the Red Dragon in different ways/locations.

### Red Dragon
A pure-fire creature trapped beneath the Steamy Pool by Balstrava. It has learned about the Mycelicardia and wants freedom so it can burn the fungal forest/network. It may trade its hoard for help escaping and can command nearby Dim as a higher fire creature.

### Dim
Enormous fire-breathing tunnel worms. They excavate the Dim Warrens and excrete Resurrection Stones. They are oblivious to most non-worm life but lethally swallow whatever lies in their path. Gebert is trapped inside one.

## Primary campaign problem: Gebert
The source of Resurrection Stones is no longer a mystery once characters understand the Dim Warrens: the Dim excrete them. Gebert's disappearance is tied directly to his research expedition into the Warrens.

The Warrens encounter procedure repeatedly gives a chance that a moving Dim contains Gebert, whose muffled cries can be heard. The exact Dim is therefore procedurally discovered rather than assigned to one fixed room.

Rescue can be approached through:
- exploring warrens and surviving encounters
- using Rixi knowledge/guides
- repairing/using the Delver
- following clues from skeleton scouts
- interacting with the Red Dragon, which can influence Dim
- creative free-text plans consistent with worm anatomy/behaviour

## Dim Warrens
A shifting network of tunnels and chambers excavated by the worms. Source procedure includes:
- encounters in current or adjacent locations
- loss/resource complications
- exhaustion
- environmental blockage
- quiet intervals

Entrances include:
- cave-in entrance with broken Delver and Rixi hunter, repairable by Gaspar
- flooded entrance
- open entrance
- fiery/coal-smoke entrance

Key chambers include:
- accidental lake connected to surface reservoir leakage
- empty turn chamber
- Dim nesting chamber with eggs and many Resurrection Stones
- dragon-skeleton excavation chamber
- black-pudding pit
- green-slime chamber
- lava chamber
- tomb chamber with several magic items
- communal Dim waste chamber containing thousands of Resurrection Stones
- vampire-bat chamber avoided even by Dim

## Side objectives and major bargains

### Rixi Village North attack
The north village begins under assault by corrupted skeletons riding Sporespiders. Helping creates strong positive Rixi relations and free gadget/guide support.

### Rixi ferry troll
The Waterfall crossing is operated by a Shroom Troll magically bound to pull a raft. She wants to trick someone into replacing her. Players can expose the trick, accept it, negotiate, kill/bypass her or use another crossing.

### Old Well
Contains a Water Elemental trapped by Balstrava, old adventurer remains/equipment and an account of attempts to bottle the dangerous water. The elemental wants freedom/revenge.

### Standing Stones
A blood-stained ritual site produces a vision of a wizard using Resurrection Stones on a corpse, followed by immediate violence from the resurrected subject. This foreshadows the corrupting nature/history of resurrection.

### Red Dragon
Trapped at the Steamy Pool, guarded/protected by Sporemanders. It knows the Mycelicardia is a major threat. It is willing to bargain for freedom and believes a Dim could widen its escape route. Freeing it can produce a destructive anti-fungal force rather than a simple treasure encounter.

### Bonetown economy
PCs can gamble, shop, trade, gather fungi, commission warped alchemical concoctions, recruit guides and sell/collect unusual materials. The campaign can function as a recurring adventure site even after Gebert's crisis resolves.

## End-state space

### Gebert rescued
Bonetown gains back its wizard/creator. Reward follows. The deeper corruption problem remains unless separately addressed.

### Gebert lost but source learned
After 21 days he is unrecoverable. PCs can still return with proof/information that the Dim produce Resurrection Stones and claim the corresponding reward.

### Resurrection Stone recovery
Any number of stones can be recovered and sold/used, but their corruption remains hard canon.

### Rixi alliance
PCs can become trusted outsiders, obtain gadgets/guides and potentially improve relations around specific threats. This does not automatically reconcile Rixi and Bonetown skeletons.

### Dragon freed
The dragon's anti-fungal agenda can dramatically alter the forest and Mycelicardia state. Freedom is not equivalent to a safe or gentle outcome.

### Mycelicardia challenged
The source identifies it as the deep ecological antagonist/influence but does not require a single scripted final battle. DMOS should allow campaigns to continue into future actions against its network rather than inventing a mandatory boss chamber not present in the extracted text.

### PCs leave
The Gebert deadline and local faction/ecology state continue. The site remains available when the midnight entrance can be accessed again.
