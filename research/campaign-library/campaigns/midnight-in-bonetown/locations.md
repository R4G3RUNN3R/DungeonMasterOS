# Midnight in Bonetown - Location Register

## Overworld Midnight Alley
- Type: magical access point
- Access: appears only at midnight as an arched passage in a dank alley
- Connection: steep dark stairs descend to landing outside Bonetown

## 1. Bonetown
- Type: subterranean skeleton town / hub
- Population: roughly 100 skeletons
- Entrance landmark: red neon welcome sign
- Main street businesses cater to visitors from above
- Important sites:
  - Tavern run by Tiana; Scapula Sam Quintet performs; Knucklebones gambling
  - Armory run by Albert
  - Flea Market run by Gran
  - Duli's Alchemist Shop
  - skeletal-animal racetrack frequented by Young Jenkins
- Dynamic state: increasing individual skeleton corruption is visible through fungal growth
- Primary quest: Gebert recovery / Resurrection Stone source

## 2. Rixi Railroad
- Type: forest transportation route
- Narrow-gauge railroad through fungal terrain
- A train may pass when PCs arrive
- Jumping aboard is possible but risky
- Fast travel along the rail path avoids normal Forest Die travel rolls
- Stowaways discovered at Rixi villages can be arrested/fined
- Old/dilapidated platform reflects earlier skeleton-Rixi contact

## 3. River Ford
- Type: river crossing
- Narrower river section crossed by large rocks
- Water is filled with electric eels and visibly crackles
- Falling during crossing exposes travelers to eel swarm attack
- Cave Ducks swim safely among the eels

## 4. Rixi Village South
- Type: fortified Rixi settlement
- Carved dome dwellings; electric lighting; mining/fungus work
- Railroad platform connects toward north village
- Electrified perimeter fence and guarded gate
- Visitors can be admitted if they agree to help hunt corrupted skeletons
- Offers beds/food, Rixi gadgets, guides/mercenaries
- Knows Gaspar lives in north village
- Bridge over river available to trusted Rixi allies

## 5. Waterfall / Ferry
- Type: river crossing / hazard
- Electrified water drops roughly 50 feet into eel-filled pool
- Cave Ducks present and can provide useful Dim information if communicated with
- Shroom Troll ferryman pulls raft along chained route
- Troll is magically bound to service and wants to transfer the obligation to another creature
- Alternate/creative crossings remain possible

## 6. Rixi Village North
- Type: fortified Rixi settlement
- Begins under active assault from approximately 10 corrupted skeletons mounted on Sporespiders
- Electric fence and guarded gate
- Railroad service to south village
- Gaspar the mechanic lives here
- Rixi built the Delver and Gaspar can repair it
- Helping defend village earns strong gratitude, gadgets and guide support

## 7. Old Well
- Type: isolated forest feature / encounter
- Deep stone well with water at bottom and signs of a failed adventurer experiment
- Water Elemental trapped at bottom by Balstrava
- Elemental calls weakly for help and wants revenge
- Bottled portions of its water remain dangerously aggressive
- If elemental is removed/defeated, water drains and exposes old adventurer remains/equipment

## 8. Standing Stones
- Type: ancient ritual site
- Three giant square arches form a triangle around blood-stained altar stone
- Close inspection triggers a disturbing vision involving Resurrection Stones reviving a corpse that immediately attacks its wizard
- Vision carries WIL/Fatigue consequences under Cairn
- Function: foreshadows the danger/corruption inherent in resurrection

## 9. Steamy Pool
- Type: hot pool / dragon prison
- Unnaturally lush vegetation and Sporemanders around steaming water
- Red Dragon trapped in a submerged cave
- Dragon sits on large treasure hoard
- Dragon understands the Mycelicardia as a threat and wants to escape to burn the fungal network
- It believes a Dim can widen the exit enough for escape
- Dragon can command nearby Dim as a higher fire creature

## 10. Dim Warrens
- Type: procedural dungeon network
- Massive Dim tunnels with side chambers
- Dim movement forces travelers to evade, dig aside or risk being swallowed
- Resurrection Stones are scattered and produced by Dim
- Gebert is inside a Dim and can be detected during appropriate encounter results

### Warrens entrance 1: Recent Cave-In / Delver
- Blocked by fallen earth/rocks
- Broken Delver outside with frustrated Rixi hunter
- Gaspar can repair Delver

### Warrens entrance 2: Flooded Entrance
- Muddy/difficult route receiving water from Accidental Lake chamber

### Warrens entrance 3: Open Entrance
- Clear/direct entrance

### Warrens entrance 4: Fiery Entrance
- Coal deposit ignited by Dim
- Fire/smoke affects nearby tunnels

### Warrens room 5: Accidental Lake
- Shallow lake caused when a Dim breached the floor of an overworld/town reservoir
- Water drains partially into adjacent tunnels

### Warrens room 6: Empty Chamber
- Turnaround space created by Dim

### Warrens room 7: Nesting Chamber
- Multiple Dim on nests/eggs
- Many Resurrection Stones on floor
- Dim are protective but not automatically hunting intruders if eggs are not threatened

### Warrens room 8: Dragon Chamber
- Excavated ancient dragon skeleton
- Scales/bones may be harvested with time

### Warrens room 9: Black Pit
- Narrow path around black-liquid pit
- Black Pudding hides within

### Warrens room 10: Green Chamber
- Dead Dim decomposing under green slime infestation
- Multiple Green Slimes on ceiling

### Warrens room 11: Lava Chamber
- Deep chamber, steeply descending approaches
- Bubbling lava pool; heat noticeable from nearby tunnels

### Warrens room 12: Tomb Chamber
- Warrens broke into older tomb from above
- Open/tumbled sarcophagi and skeletons
- Key magic items include Hedgehog Ring, Bonesaw Blade and Wayward Friend

### Warrens room 13: Communal Waste Chamber
- Dim waste area
- Several thousand Resurrection Stones accumulated here
- Extremely important economic/ethical discovery

### Warrens room 14: Vampire Bat Chamber
- Large swarm of vampire bats
- Even Dim avoid entering

## Forest geography implementation note
The printed adventure includes a visual pointcrawl showing which forest locations are directly connected. The structured extraction environment recovered every numbered location and the source's direct/off-path travel procedures but not every visual edge from that map.

DMOS runtime rule until the map asset is ingested:
- if a direct source-map edge is known by future map data, travel = ~1 hour + normal Forest Die
- otherwise the player may still travel off-path between any forest points at ~3x time + 3 Forest Die rolls, exactly as the source permits
- never invent that an off-path destination is unreachable

This preserves playability and source rules without hallucinating direct edges.
