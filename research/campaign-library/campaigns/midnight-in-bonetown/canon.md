# Midnight in Bonetown - Canon Locks

## Hard canon
1. Bonetown is a subterranean town of roughly 100 resurrected skeletons founded by the wizard Gebert.
2. The overworld entrance to the cavern appears magically at midnight in a dank alley.
3. Gebert discovered Resurrection Stones and used them to animate skeletons.
4. Resurrection Stones gradually corrupt resurrected skeletons; fungal growth is an early sign and fully corrupted skeletons become violent.
5. Corrupted skeletons were expelled from Bonetown and their attacks caused the Rixi to sever relations with skeletons.
6. The Rixi are native to the cavern, not invaders created for the current crisis.
7. The Rixi harness electric eels for electricity, technology, tools and weapons.
8. Gebert has been missing for one week at campaign start because a Dim swallowed him during an expedition for Resurrection Stones.
9. Gebert remains rescuable for three more weeks; after that he is fully digested and cannot be recovered alive.
10. Dim are enormous fire-breathing worms that excavate the Warrens and excrete Resurrection Stones.
11. The source of Resurrection Stones is therefore tied to the Dim, not to Gebert manufacturing them.
12. The Mycelicardia is a massive malicious fungal organism/network beneath the cavern floor seeking to spread its influence toward the overworld.
13. Spores/fungal infection influence wildlife and skeleton corruption; hallucinations caused by spores are not automatically real events.
14. Balstrava is Gebert's former associate and became dangerously erratic after spore infection.
15. Balstrava trapped the Water Elemental in the Old Well and the Red Dragon beneath the Steamy Pool.
16. The Red Dragon is a pure-fire creature, knows about the Mycelicardia and wants to escape partly so it can burn the fungal forest/network.
17. The two Rixi villages may welcome non-skeleton outsiders who credibly agree to help against corrupted skeletons.
18. Rixi hostility toward skeletons does not make every Rixi automatically hostile to every living PC.
19. The north Rixi village begins under attack by corrupted skeletons riding Sporespiders.
20. The Waterfall ferry is pulled by a Shroom Troll magically bound to the task; she wants another creature to take her place.
21. The Old Well contains a trapped Water Elemental seeking release/revenge against Balstrava.
22. The Standing Stones can produce a violent resurrection vision demonstrating that Resurrection Stone use has dangerous implications.
23. The Dim Warrens are a dynamic tunnel network; Gebert can be encountered procedurally inside a Dim rather than being fixed in one named chamber.
24. Removing Resurrection Stones from corrupted skeletons prevents their normal reformation. Leaving the stones in place allows them to reform after death according to source mechanics.
25. Bonetown's Gebert/reward crisis can resolve without defeating the Mycelicardia, Balstrava, the dragon, the Water Elemental or every corrupted skeleton.
26. The adventure is an open site. Side quests, trade, faction choices and repeated visits are valid.

## Conditional canon

### Time passes
For every meaningful day spent/elapsed in-world, reduce Gebert's rescue window. At 21 days after campaign start, `gebertState=unrecoverable` unless already rescued.

### Harm infected creature in forest
Set/increase `mycelicardiaAlert`; the next Forest Die danger is worsened as the source procedure specifies.

### Rixi North defended
If PCs materially help repel the opening attack, Rixi gratitude provides free gadgets/guide services and improves relations.

### Ferry replacement
If a character voluntarily or through deception takes the Shroom Troll's place pulling the bound ferry, the magical binding transfers according to source fiction. Do not make it a harmless joke.

### Water Elemental freed
If released from the Old Well without being destroyed, it wants revenge on Balstrava.

### Dragon freed
If PCs create a viable escape route, the Red Dragon becomes free and acts on its anti-Mycelicardia/fire nature. Its future destruction can be extensive; it is not domesticated by the act of rescue.

### Gebert rescued
Bonetown's primary Gebert reward resolves positively; Stone scarcity/corruption and forest politics continue.

### Gebert unrecoverable
Bonetown can still reward credible information about the Stone source and recovered Stones.

## Soft canon
The AI may improvise:
- unnamed skeleton residents using the source generator pattern
- ordinary dialogue, meals/drinks and commerce within Bonetown
- fungal colours/smells outside keyed mechanical fungus results
- minor trail detail and weather-like cavern ambience
- Rixi household detail consistent with their village technology

## Prohibited improvisation
The AI may not:
- make Gebert secretly safe in Bonetown
- remove the 21-day rescue deadline
- make the Rixi uniformly evil simply because they attack corrupted skeletons
- make Bonetown skeletons uniformly murderous at start
- turn hallucination-table results into real monsters unless separately generated
- make Resurrection Stones consequence-free resurrection
- decide the Red Dragon must be killed rather than bargained with/freed
- convert the Mycelicardia into a talking humanoid villain without source support
- create a mandatory final boss fight against the Mycelicardia
- prevent off-path travel between forest points; the source explicitly permits it at extra time/risk
- label the adventure D&D 5e without an actual conversion adapter
