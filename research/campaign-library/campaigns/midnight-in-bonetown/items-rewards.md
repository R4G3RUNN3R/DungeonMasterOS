# Midnight in Bonetown - Important Items and Rewards

## Resurrection Stone
- Appearance: red egg-shaped stone with green flecks
- Function: reanimates a dead creature when placed in an eye socket; nearby separated body parts can reconnect during resurrection
- Cost/consequence: resurrected creatures progressively develop fungal corruption
- Recharge: source requires extended soaking in willingly given blood
- Plot role:
  - created Bonetown's skeleton population
  - Bonetown wants more Stones
  - corrupted skeletons carry bright/redder Stones
  - removing a corrupted skeleton's Stone prevents normal reformation
  - Dim produce/excrete Stones
  - Warrens Waste Chamber contains an enormous stockpile
- AI rule: never present these as consequence-free resurrection tokens

## Bonetown rewards
- Return Gebert alive: major gold reward from skeletons
- If Gebert cannot be found: major reward for credible information about the Stone source
- Individual Stones: substantial reward per recovered Stone
- Exact source amounts are preserved in structured mechanics but should not be dumped into spoiler-free campaign selection text.

## Gran's Flea Market relics
### Starscryer
Projects current celestial positions when charged; recharge tied to full-moon reflection in natural water.

### Endless Thread
Magical spool producing persistent adhesive thread until user chooses otherwise.

### Instant Boat
Small boat figure that expands into a usable rowboat; recharge involves communal drunken sea-shanty ritual.

### Duckspeaker
Allows communication with ducks for a limited period; recharge requires placing it in a duck's mouth.

### Field Guide to Subterranean Mushrooms
Improves fungus-hunting effectiveness.

### Flamefender
Protective golden sheet/cloak that blocks fire for those beneath it but impairs sight/mobility.

## Rixi gadgets
The Rixi trade gadgets for fungus value (`MV`).

### Shock Spear
Eel-powered spear that stuns/damages; rechargeable by vigorous mechanical action/source procedure.

### Shock Arrow
Single-use electrical arrow.

### Headlamp
Eel-powered directional light.

### Lantern
Eel-powered area light.

### Static Mask
Electrically charged mesh mask that repels dangerous spores until discharged.

### Jackhammer
Eel-powered digging tool.

### Power Cart
Powered cargo cart using live-eel battery system.

### Eel Net
Tool for harvesting/catching electric eels safely.

### Eel Container
Insulated storage for captured eels.

## Delver
- Location: broken at recent cave-in Warrens entrance
- Creator/operators: Rixi; Gaspar can repair
- Form: enclosed tracked digging machine with forward cutting/spike wheel
- Function: rapidly excavates Dim-sized tunnels and offers some operator protection
- Power: eel-powered charge system
- Plot use: alternate engineering solution for Warrens access/rescue

## Old Well loot
After Water Elemental leaves/is defeated and water drains, old adventurer remains/equipment become accessible. Source includes climbing/rope/container evidence connected to prior failed attempts to exploit the elemental.

## Red Dragon hoard
- Value: extremely large compared with ordinary Bonetown commerce
- Dragon bargaining position: willing to trade hoard for a viable escape plan
- AI rule: hoard is leverage in negotiation, not automatically available because PCs found the pool

## Dim Warrens Tomb Chamber items
### Hedgehog Ring
Protective/offensive magical ring associated with spines/thorns under source rules.

### Bonesaw Blade
Magic blade recovered from older tomb.

### Wayward Friend
Magic item from tomb chamber with source-defined unusual behaviour.

Exact Cairn mechanics for these three items should be kept in the rules/item-definition layer from the licensed source; campaign state only needs identity/location/ownership until used.

## Quackolocation
- Source: Cave Ducks can provide written spell text/information to characters able to communicate with them
- Effect concept: locate/navigate around nearby objects through quacking-based echolocation
- AI rule: this is a learnable source spell/reward, not a generic duck ability granted to everyone

## Duli's concoctions
Duli's recipes combine three fungal magical types and produce deliberately warped spell-like results.

Campaign data should store:
- recipe/type combination
- cost with shop-supplied ingredients
- reduced cost if party brings ingredients
- exact source effect

The AI must not rename them into standard D&D potions or silently swap mechanics across rulesets.
