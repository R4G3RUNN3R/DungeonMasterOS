## Player-facing pitch
At midnight, a hidden alley opens into Bonetown, a subterranean refuge of resurrected skeletons. Their wizard Gebert has vanished while searching for the stones that bring the dead back, the fungal forest around them is becoming increasingly hostile, and old tensions with the native Rixi have turned violent. Find Gebert, get rich, brew dangerous mushroom concoctions, make questionable alliances, or discover why the cavern's ecology is going very wrong.

## Core campaign state
- `gebertState`: trapped-in-Dim | rescued | dead/unrecoverable
- `gebertDeadlineDays`: 21 days from campaign start until fully digested and no longer rescuable
- `bonetownCorruption`: ongoing fungal/resurrection-stone corruption among skeletons
- `rixiRelations`: hostile-to-skeletons, independently adjustable toward PCs
- `mycelicardiaAlert`: increases when spore-infected creatures are harmed in the forest
- `balstravaState`: wandering/infected/chaotic; may appear through forest encounters
- `dragonState`: trapped | freed | allied | hostile | dead
- `waterElementalState`: trapped | freed | defeated
- `dimWarrensState`: active tunnel network
- `resurrectionStonesRecovered`
- `factionRelations`: Bonetown, Rixi, corrupted skeletons, fungal ecosystem

## Primary objective
Bonetown offers rewards for:
- Gebert returned alive
- reliable information about the source of Resurrection Stones if Gebert cannot be found
- recovered Resurrection Stones

This is an open objective, not a linear quest. PCs may instead pursue trade, alchemy, treasure, Rixi alliances, the trapped dragon, the water elemental, Balstrava, or the deeper fungal threat.

## DMOS guard principle
The cavern is an ecosystem, not a boss corridor. The AI must track who wants what, preserve the Gebert clock, allow faction diplomacy and lateral solutions, and never require the party to kill the Rixi, skeletons, dragon, troll or fungal creatures simply because stat blocks exist. The players may rescue Gebert, ignore him, free the dragon, bargain with the Rixi, hunt mushrooms, rob Bonetown, or leave. Consequences must follow source state rather than a hidden preferred path.
