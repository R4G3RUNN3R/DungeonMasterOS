# Midnight in Bonetown - Factions

## Bonetown Skeletons
- Population: roughly 100
- Founder: Gebert
- Goals:
  - recover Gebert
  - acquire more Resurrection Stones
  - grow/maintain their unusual community
  - profit from/tolerate overworld visitors
- Internal problem: slow corruption caused by Resurrection Stones/fungal influence
- Resources: town economy, businesses, scouts, money/rewards, information
- Enemies: corrupted skeletons are expelled threats; relationship with Rixi is badly broken
- AI rule: individual skeletons vary; not all are secretly planning violence

## Corrupted Skeletons
- Origin: formerly ordinary Bonetown skeletons
- Cause: progressive corruption associated with Resurrection Stones/fungus
- Goals/behaviour: violent, roaming forest threats
- Resources: regeneration/reformation while Resurrection Stone remains active
- Enemies: Rixi, living creatures, Bonetown order
- Strategic effect: their attacks caused the collapse of Rixi-Bonetown relations

## Rixi Villages
- Type: native cavern society
- Settlements: North and South villages
- Goals:
  - defend villages
  - collect/farm fungi
  - mine materials
  - advance eel-powered technology
  - eliminate corrupted skeleton threats
- Resources:
  - electrified fences
  - railroad
  - hunters
  - eel-powered gadgets/weapons
  - Gaspar/Delver engineering
  - knowledge of forest
- Relationship with PCs: potentially cooperative
- Relationship with skeletons: hostile after years of corrupted-skeleton attacks
- AI rule: diplomacy is possible without forcing reconciliation with Bonetown

## Mycelicardia Network
- Type: distributed fungal intelligence/ecosystem
- Goal: expand fungal influence throughout cavern and into overworld
- Resources:
  - mycelial network
  - spores
  - infected/corrupted organisms
  - awareness reaction when infected creatures are harmed
- Relationship: influences ecology rather than negotiating as conventional faction
- AI rule: do not invent humanoid command hierarchy

## Balstrava
- Type: single-person volatile faction actor
- Goal: source does not give a clean strategic master-plan after corruption
- Resources: magic, spellbooks, erratic forest activity
- Relationships:
  - former Gebert associate
  - enemy/captor of Water Elemental
  - captor of Red Dragon
- AI rule: use encounter/event behaviour and established past actions; don't fabricate a coherent conspiracy she does not have

## Red Dragon
- Type: trapped high-power independent actor
- Goal: escape and burn the fungal forest/Mycelicardia
- Resources: immense fire power, hoard, influence/control over nearby Dim
- Potential relationship: ally-of-convenience or catastrophic released force
- Enemies: fungal network

## Water Elemental
- Type: trapped independent actor
- Goal: freedom and revenge on Balstrava
- Resources: water-form combat/containment abilities
- AI rule: objective overlaps may permit alliance, but it retains autonomy

## Shroom Trolls
- Type: fungal humanoid population/encounter group
- Behaviour: regenerating forest inhabitants; individuals may have specific roles such as ferry troll
- Relationship to Mycelicardia: fungal ecosystem connection but not automatically mindless soldiers

## Dim
- Type: giant fire-breathing worm ecology
- Goal: essentially worm-like survival/tunnelling rather than political conquest
- Resources: massive bodies, flame, tunnels, eggs, Resurrection Stone production
- Relationship:
  - source of Stones
  - dangerous to nearly everything in path
  - Red Dragon can command nearby Dim
  - Cave Ducks are enemies
- AI rule: Dim are hazards/creatures, not evil masterminds
