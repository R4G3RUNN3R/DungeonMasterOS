# Midnight in Bonetown - Encounters, Procedures and Clocks

## Clock: Gebert Digestion
- Start: Gebert already missing for 1 week
- Remaining rescue window: 21 days
- Advance: each elapsed in-world day
- Terminal state: `gebertState=unrecoverable`
- Important: this deadline continues if PCs leave the cavern and return later

## Clock/state: Mycelicardia Alert
- Start: baseline forest danger
- Trigger: harming a spore-infected creature alerts the mycelial network
- Consequence: next Forest Die event is biased/worsened according to source procedure
- Reset/decay: follow source procedure; do not permanently max difficulty from one fight

## Forest travel procedure
### Connected-point travel
- approximately 1 hour
- roll Forest Die while moving/arriving or lingering >30 minutes outside Rixi villages

### Off-path travel
- allowed between locations
- approximately triple travel time
- requires three Forest Die rolls

### Forest Die result families
- immediate encounter
- distant/telegraphed encounter
- spore release
- fungal trap
- exhaustion/rest pressure
- quiet/safe interval

## Forest encounter families
Source table includes interactions among:
- Shroom Trolls
- Electric Eels displaced from water
- Shroom Troll witch
- Corrupted Skeletons
- Balstrava performing erratic activities
- Sporemanders
- Sporespiders
- Rixi Hunters
- treasure-digging corrupted skeleton
- faction-vs-faction fights
- burning/infected creatures

AI rule: encounter composition can create negotiation/avoidance opportunities. Not every rolled creature attacks automatically unless source behaviour says it does.

## Spore events
Giant fungi audibly signal releases shortly before dangerous clouds spread.
- players may evade/take precautions
- failed Cairn WIL resistance can cause WIL harm and hallucinations
- hallucination content remains subjective
- masks and other source equipment can mitigate infection

## Fungal trap families
- adhesive fungal webbing that pulls victims toward digestive fungus
- loop/fiber trap that inflates/tightens and drags targets
- harpoon-like spore cloud attacks
- sedating liquid drops leading to fungal overgrowth

The AI must telegraph traps according to source visibility rather than making them unavoidable gotchas.

## Fungus-hunting procedure
Characters may spend time seeking fungi.
- random/simple search provides ingredients
- riskier targeted search can seek a desired magical type
- fungus magical type determines Duli concoction recipes
- fungus value is also Rixi trade currency (`MV`)

## Rixi Village North opening crisis
- approximately 10 corrupted skeletons on Sporespiders attack village defenses
- PCs can help, ignore, exploit chaos, side elsewhere or leave
- successful assistance increases Rixi relations and can unlock free gadgets/guide services
- failed/ignored defense must alter village condition rather than simply resetting next visit

## Waterfall/Ferry encounter
- troll operates chained ferry
- core social hazard is transfer of magical obligation
- possible outcomes: pay/use ferry, expose trick, free troll, take her place, bypass crossing, fight, use alternate route
- troll regeneration/fire vulnerability handled by Cairn adapter

## Old Well encounter
- Water Elemental trapped below
- faint plea for help + hostile grasping water creates ambiguous approach
- PCs can free, bargain, fight, bottle water, inspect remains or leave
- if elemental gone, well drains and old adventurer equipment becomes available

## Standing Stones encounter
- close inspection triggers resurrection vision
- observer can be physically pulled away or resist effect
- consequence includes mental/WIL damage/fatigue in Cairn
- vision is historical/symbolic evidence, not a command to perform ritual

## Steamy Pool / Red Dragon encounter
- Sporemanders occupy/protect surrounding vegetation
- Red Dragon trapped under pool
- dragon has treasure and offers bargain for freedom
- known desired solution: use a Dim to enlarge escape
- dragon may command nearby Dim
- freeing it introduces major forest/Mycelicardia consequences
- killing it is possible but not required

## Dim Warrens exploration die
Roll as source directs when entering branches/chambers or lingering.
Result families:
1. immediate encounter
2. adjacent/distant telegraphed encounter
3. loss/resource disruption
4. exhaustion/rest requirement
5. environmental blockage/change
6. quiet

If a Dim is generated, there is a source chance it contains Gebert; his muffled screams can reveal that fact.

## Dim Warrens encounter patterns
Includes:
- damaged Skeleton Scout still searching for Gebert
- moving/sleeping/excreting Dim
- Dim swallowing material/creatures as they travel
- Rixi search parties
- cave-ins
- burning skeletons/Rixi/Dim conflicts
- Resurrection Stone deposits behind Dim
- dead Dim and casualties

## Warrens room encounters
### Nesting Chamber
Dim protect eggs; avoiding aggression is possible if eggs remain unthreatened.

### Black Pit
Black Pudding hazard in narrow passage.

### Green Chamber
Green Slimes cling to ceiling around dead Dim.

### Lava Chamber
Environmental extreme heat/lava traversal.

### Tomb Chamber
Treasure and older burial intrusion, not mandatory combat.

### Waste Chamber
Thousands of Resurrection Stones create a huge economic/ethical/transport problem rather than merely `loot +9999`.

### Vampire Bat Chamber
Large bat swarm. Dim avoidance is itself warning information.

## Bonetown social/economic encounters
### Tavern
- drinks with bizarre effects/ingredients
- music
- Knucklebones gambling
- Tiana/Scapula Sam relationship subplot

### Armory
- Albert reluctant to sell and secretly hoarding weapons because he is becoming corrupted
- investigation/social pressure can expose his condition

### Flea Market
- Gran offers mostly junk with chances for valuable relics
- friendly interaction can produce better treasure guidance

### Duli's Alchemy
- PCs can purchase concoctions or gather their own ingredients for reduced cost
- recipes use three fungal magical types
- outputs intentionally twist familiar magical effects

### Racetrack
- betting on skeletal animals
- Young Jenkins pushes gambling

## Fail-forward rules
- Missing Gebert does not end play; source-information and Stone rewards remain.
- Losing Rixi trust does not make the Warrens inaccessible; forest/off-path routes still exist.
- Destroying the Delver does not eliminate Warrens access; other entrances exist.
- Killing the ferryman does not erase river geography; other crossing methods remain.
- Refusing the dragon bargain does not block Gebert rescue.
- Ignoring the Mycelicardia is a valid partial conclusion; its threat persists.
