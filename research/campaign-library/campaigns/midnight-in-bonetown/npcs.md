# Midnight in Bonetown - NPC Register

## Gebert
- Role: wizard, founder/creator of Bonetown, missing quest target
- History: discovered Resurrection Stones and used them to reanimate local skeletons
- Current state: swallowed alive inside a Dim in the Warrens
- Time limit: rescue window expires 21 days after campaign start
- Bonetown value: community founder and source of the Stone expeditions
- AI rule: Gebert is not waiting in a fixed room; Warrens procedures can reveal the Dim containing him

## Balstrava
- Role: witch, former associate of Gebert, roaming chaotic threat
- History: explored cavern with Gebert approximately 15 years ago
- State: badly affected by spores; erratic and dangerous
- Forest behaviour: appears through random events doing irrational/volatile things; carries changing spellbooks
- Known past actions: trapped Water Elemental; trapped Red Dragon
- AI rule: her behaviour is unstable, but do not turn every forest event into her deliberate master plan

## Tiana
- Role: Bonetown tavern keeper
- Description: skeleton in formal/ball-like dress; cheerful, melodic voice
- Relationship: secretly attracted to Scapula Sam but misreads his performative coolness
- Function: town social hub, rumours, gambling/drinks

## Scapula Sam
- Role: leader of Scapula Sam Quintet
- Presentation: skeleton musician in dark glasses/bowler hat with cultivated cool persona
- Relationship: secretly in love with Tiana, frustrated that she does not seem to reciprocate
- Function: tavern atmosphere/social subplot

## Scapula Sam Quintet
- Five skeleton musicians with vocals/drums/saxophone/trombone/keyboard roles
- Function: entertainment/social scene, not combat faction

## Albert
- Role: Bonetown armory proprietor
- Public behaviour: reluctant seller despite unusually large weapon/armor stock
- Secret: becoming corrupted and hoarding weapons in expectation/preparation for skeleton violence/uprising
- AI rule: this is a live tension, not proof that the entire town starts in rebellion

## Gran
- Role: flea-market proprietor
- Presentation: elderly/grandmotherly skeleton persona
- Goal: enjoy commerce, direct patient visitors toward better treasure
- Function: treasure market, information, mask sales

## Duli
- Role: Bonetown alchemist; only major non-skeleton resident identified in town source material
- Presentation: cloaked/mysterious, long bluish-green nose, quiet voice
- Goal: experiment with the fungal forest's magical ingredients and make new discoveries
- Function: crafts strange concoctions from fungal types; sends/encourages ingredient expeditions
- AI rule: potion outcomes follow source recipe/types or rules adapter, not improvised generic D&D potions

## Young Jenkins
- Role: child-sized skeleton racetrack gambler/promoter
- Presentation: top hat/monocle, fast-talking accent/personality
- Goal: gamble and persuade others to bet

## Gaspar
- Role: Rixi mechanic in Village North
- Presentation: squat, gruff, overloaded tool belt, grease-covered
- Expertise: built/understands the Delver; can repair it
- Campaign value: mechanical route into Warrens and Rixi technological contact

## Waterfall Shroom Troll
- Role: magically bound ferryman
- State: compelled to pull river raft by chain
- Goal: transfer binding/job to another creature and escape
- Behaviour: mutters/plans ways to trick a traveler into taking her place
- AI rule: do not make her automatically attack. Deception, negotiation, rescue, replacement and combat are all valid

## Water Elemental
- Role: trapped entity at Old Well
- Captor: Balstrava
- Goal: escape and revenge on Balstrava
- Behaviour: dangerous around water/nearby creatures; calls faintly for help
- AI rule: freeing it creates an autonomous entity with its own revenge goal, not a permanent PC summon

## Red Dragon
- Role: trapped powerful faction/creature at Steamy Pool
- Captor: Balstrava
- Nature: pure-fire dragon, immune to heat/fire effects described by source
- Knowledge: understands Mycelicardia as a deep threat
- Goal: escape pool/cavern restriction and burn down fungal forest/network
- Bargain: may trade substantial treasure for credible help escaping
- Proposed solution: believes a Dim can widen the route
- Special relation: can command nearby Dim as higher fire entity
- AI rule: is neither compulsory boss nor harmless ally. Its goals overlap with PCs only conditionally

## Rixi Hunters
- Role: native cavern hunters/guides/defenders
- Goals: gather fungi, defend villages, kill corrupted skeleton threats
- Technology: shock weapons/eel-powered gadgets
- Relationship: hostile to skeletons due attacks; can cooperate with living PCs
- Hiring: can serve as paid guides/mercenaries

## Corrupted Skeletons
- Role: former Bonetown skeletons turned violent through Stone/fungal corruption
- Goal/behaviour: aggressive violence in forest
- Key rule: Resurrection Stones in sockets allow reformation; removing stones stops that cycle
- Relation: enemies of Rixi; rejected by Bonetown

## Skeleton Scouts
- Role: Bonetown explorers/searchers
- Goal: find Gebert / Stones
- Warrens state: several can be encountered trapped, damaged or searching
- Key clue: some point toward Gebert's presence inside Dim

## Cave Ducks
- Role: blind/electricity-resistant forest wildlife
- Behaviour: navigate by constant quacking/echolocation; can group defensively
- Relationship: enemies of Dim
- Information: if communicated with, can share information about Dim
- Special knowledge/reward: source includes Quackolocation spell concept

## The Mycelicardia
- Role: non-humanoid deep antagonist/ecological intelligence
- Form: huge malicious fungal heart/network under cavern floor
- Goal: spread fungal influence through cavern and ultimately into overworld
- Method: spores, connected mycelium, influence/corruption
- AI rule: treat as distributed ecological intelligence, not a chatty NPC with invented monologues

## Generated Bonetown skeletons
The source provides a d20 skeleton NPC/rumour generator with named individuals and traits. DMOS may instantiate these as needed while keeping their source rumours tagged as `rumour`, not facts.

Examples include residents who gossip about:
- missing scouting parties
- expelled corrupted skeletons
- Rixi hostility
- Standing Stones visions
- monster/treasure at Old Well
- dragon in forest
- electric-eel river
- Dim Warrens as source of Stones
- spore madness

Generated NPCs should retain source-defined name/personality/rumour pair when selected; do not fuse multiple table rows into one omniscient resident.
