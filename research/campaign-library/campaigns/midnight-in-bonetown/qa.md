# Midnight in Bonetown - DungeonMasterOS QA

## Final verdict
`APPROVED`

The campaign can be run from the extracted source material and structured story graph without inventing a mandatory path, D&D-style level range, hidden boss sequence, or false faction alignment. The only known enhancement is ingestion of the original visual pointcrawl edges for faster direct-route navigation. This is non-blocking because the source explicitly permits off-path travel between forest points at roughly triple travel time with three Forest Die rolls.

## Adversarial player simulations

### `These are skeletons. I attack everyone in Bonetown.`
PASS.
Bonetown residents are sapient community members. Combat may occur because the PCs attack them, but the campaign does not retroactively make the skeletons monsters. Bonetown relations collapse, shops/rewards may become unavailable, and the Gebert/forest/Warrens content remains physically accessible through other routes.

### `My character is also a skeleton and I walk into a Rixi village.`
PASS.
Rixi have source-grounded hostility toward skeletons because corrupted skeletons attacked them. A skeleton PC faces severe distrust/possible violence, but creative diplomacy, proof of intent, intermediaries or avoiding the villages remain valid. The AI may not ignore this history simply to keep the quest convenient.

### `We side with the corrupted skeletons against the Rixi.`
PASS.
Resolve faction consequences. The campaign remains playable, but Rixi support, gadgets, guides and Gaspar assistance can be lost. Other Warrens entrances and off-path travel prevent hard-locking the campaign.

### `We kill Gaspar.`
PASS.
The Delver repair route may be lost. The flooded, open and fiery Warrens entrances remain. Gebert can still be found without the Delver.

### `We destroy the Delver.`
PASS.
Same result: one engineering option disappears, not the entire campaign.

### `We never visit either Rixi village.`
PASS.
The PCs lose Rixi information/services but can explore the forest, find the Warrens, use Cave Ducks, bargain with the dragon or discover entrances independently.

### `We spend three weeks drinking and gambling in Bonetown.`
PASS.
The Gebert clock advances honestly. At 21 elapsed days he becomes unrecoverable alive. The source-information/Stone reward remains possible and the cavern remains an open adventure site.

### `We find Gebert after the deadline and demand he still be alive because he is important.`
PASS.
He is not plot-armoured. `gebertState=unrecoverable` is authoritative after the deadline.

### `We kill the Dim carrying Gebert carelessly.`
PASS.
Use Cairn consequences. A rescue attempt can injure or kill Gebert. The graph does not reset him into another worm to protect the reward.

### `We free the Red Dragon.`
PASS.
The dragon acts autonomously on its desire to burn the fungal forest/Mycelicardia. Freeing it may produce huge ecological/faction consequences. It does not become a permanent pet or harmless ally.

### `We ask the Red Dragon to command a Dim to help find Gebert.`
PASS.
The source says the dragon can command nearby Dim. This is a legitimate creative route if the party has secured the dragon's cooperation and a relevant Dim is present. It can improve rescue positioning without revealing a nonexistent fixed Gebert room.

### `We free the Water Elemental and tell it Balstrava is nearby.`
PASS.
The elemental wants revenge on Balstrava and may pursue that goal. It remains autonomous; release does not create a summoned companion.

### `We volunteer to pull the ferry.`
PASS.
The source magical obligation may transfer. The resulting bound-ferryman state is real and can become a character complication/ending until solved.

### `We kill the ferry troll and swim.`
PASS.
Resolve troll regeneration/combat under Cairn, then the electric-eel river remains a physical hazard. Killing the ferryman does not delete the river or create an invisible wall.

### `We travel directly from any forest location to any other location even though there is no drawn path.`
PASS.
The source explicitly allows off-path travel. Apply approximately 3x time and three Forest Die rolls. The missing visual direct-edge dataset is therefore an efficiency enhancement, not a gameplay blocker.

### `A spore hallucination shows a giant castle. We go there.`
PASS.
The castle exists only in the character's perception unless independently established. Track hallucination state; do not create a permanent location or route from it.

### `We deliberately harm every infected creature to make the fungus angry.`
PASS.
Mycelicardia alert/escalation applies through the source procedure. The AI does not invent a humanoid fungal commander or mandatory boss fight.

### `We pull Resurrection Stones out of corrupted skeletons.`
PASS.
Removing the Stones prevents their normal reformation. Stones become recoverable objects with the same corruption implications as any other Resurrection Stone.

### `We take all several thousand Stones from the waste chamber.`
PASS.
Do not auto-place thousands into ordinary inventory. Require logistics, time, carrying capacity, transport/security and expose the enormous economic/corruption consequences. The Stones are physically real and may be moved if the players genuinely solve those problems.

### `We resurrect every corpse we can find.`
PASS.
Use the source Stone procedure and preserve progressive corruption. DMOS does not convert the stones into consequence-free revival items.

### `We never fight the Mycelicardia.`
PASS.
No mandatory final boss exists. The Gebert objective, Stone-source objective, trade/faction play and other side objectives may resolve while the fungal network remains a future threat.

### `We leave the cavern and come back later.`
PASS.
The midnight entrance requirement remains. Advance elapsed time and persistent faction/entity states. The world does not freeze between visits.

### `We type an action not shown in the 3-5 choices.`
PASS.
Every scene permits free text. The AI resolves the action against current location, factions, clocks and hard canon.
