## Licence conclusion
`Pharmacy Run` itself is cleanly reusable as CC0. This does not transfer rights to:
- Americhaos 1994 trademarks;
- the underlying Americhaos 1994 rulebook/mechanics where separately protected;
- unrelated original Americhaos content.

DMOS should therefore package campaign fiction/state separately from the rules adapter and preserve native-ruleset attribution.
