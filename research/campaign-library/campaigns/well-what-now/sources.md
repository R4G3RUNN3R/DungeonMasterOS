## Community / actual-play evidence

### itch.io comments on primary adventure page
URL: https://pork-mort.itch.io/well-what-now

An actual player/GM commenter reports:
- they enjoyed drawing enemies, especially one called `Maria`
- their players were amused by a naked pig-man wearing only an apron

Creator replies positively and says that reaction is how it should be.

Another commenter praises the frogs, and the creator replies that the frogs' charm is their secret weapon.

These comments strongly suggest those entities appear in the adventure, but they are not promoted to hard canon until the source PDF itself is extracted.

## Licensing conclusion

The one-page version was an accepted 2025 contest entry and ultimately the overall winner. The official contest rules require accepted entries to be original and licensed CC BY-SA 4.0. Adaptation is therefore viable in principle with attribution and ShareAlike handling.

Do not confuse that licence with the broader Pork Mort commercial book/game. The main Pork Mort product is separately based on the MÖRK BORG third-party licence and remains distinct from the contest submission's CC BY-SA status.

This research note is not legal advice.

## Access gap

The public creator download route for the English PDF is known, but the complete one-page document has not yet been text-extracted in this environment. Do not invent cave rooms, enemy identities, treasure, traps, endings or mechanics from the synopsis alone.
