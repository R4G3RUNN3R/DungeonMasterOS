# Well, what now? - Source-derived research

Only currently supported facts are recorded as source canon. The full one-page dungeon remains pending extraction.

## Opening situation

The party is travelling while escorting a merchant and his goods. During the night, the merchant disappears after being dragged into a mysterious well. The party descends into the well together, entering a twisting maze of caves populated by strange creatures. The stated objective is to rescue the merchant.

## Structural implications supported by source

- The well is the transition from travel/surface play into the dungeon.
- The adventure environment is a cave maze rather than a single linear chamber.
- The scenario is explicitly system neutral.
- The creator describes the one-page document as a condensed but playable version of a larger adventure under development for Pork Mort.
- A secondary compendium announcement describes deadly choices as part of the descent, consistent with the 2025 contest's choice/party-splitting theme.

## Community-observed entities pending source verification

An actual-play commenter on the primary itch.io page reports drawing enemies and specifically names `Maria`. The same report mentions a naked pig-man wearing only an apron. Separate creator/comment discussion mentions frogs and the creator jokes that the frogs' charm is their secret weapon.

These are useful extraction targets. Until verified directly in the PDF, record them as `community-observed`, not hard canon.

## Broader setting context, not automatically adventure canon

Pork Mort's main game is set on `The Island`, a folklore-inspired setting with witches, giants, demons and other mythological beings. It is a MÖRK BORG-compatible game inspired by Balearic folklore. The one-page adventure is intended for the Pork Mort book, but the DMOS adaptation must not import unrelated setting lore into the adventure merely because it exists elsewhere on the creator site.

## DMOS design direction after complete extraction

The likely implementation should use a location graph beginning with the escort/well incident, followed by cave nodes with explicit connections. Suggested choices should expose 3-5 immediate navigational or interaction options while preserving free-text actions. The AI must never invent the merchant's captor, cave destination or rescue outcome before source extraction establishes them.
