Community observations are recorded separately from source canon.

## Actual-play report on itch.io

A commenter named Klondika reports having played the adventure with a group. They say:
- they had fun drawing the enemies
- an enemy/entity named `Maria` was especially enjoyable to draw
- players were amused by a naked pig-man wearing only an apron

Value for DMOS:

- flags `Maria` and the apron-wearing pig-man as entities to verify during PDF extraction
