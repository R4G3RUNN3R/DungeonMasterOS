# Well, what now? - Canon register

## Hard canon currently confirmed

- The adventure is `Well, what now?` by the Pork Mort team.
- The party is escorting a merchant and the merchant's goods during a journey.
- At night the merchant is dragged into a mysterious well and disappears.
- The party descends into the well in an attempt to rescue the merchant.
- The dungeon is a twisting maze of caves.
- Strange creatures inhabit the caves.
- The one-page version is system neutral.
- The one-page version is a condensed playable form of a larger adventure being developed for Pork Mort.
- The submitted one-page dungeon won Overall Winner in the 2025 One Page Dungeon Contest.

## Conditional canon currently confirmed

No exact source-defined trigger/outcome rules have yet been extracted from the PDF. Do not create hard conditional rules about cave routes, creature reactions, merchant fate or endings until the source page is read.
