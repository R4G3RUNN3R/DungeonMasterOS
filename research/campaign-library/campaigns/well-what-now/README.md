# Well, what now?

Status: `researching`

## Executive summary

`Well, what now?` is a system-neutral one-page dungeon by the Pork Mort team / Cap de Pork Games. The party is escorting a merchant and his goods when the merchant is dragged into a mysterious well during the night. The group descends together into a twisting cave maze filled with strange creatures to rescue him.

The one-page version won the **Overall Winner** award in the 2025 One Page Dungeon Contest. Because accepted 2025 entries were required by the official contest rules to be original and CC BY-SA 4.0, the submitted one-page dungeon has a strong legal path for DungeonMasterOS adaptation with attribution and ShareAlike compliance.
