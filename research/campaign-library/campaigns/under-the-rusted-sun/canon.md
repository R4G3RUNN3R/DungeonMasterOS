# Under the Rusted Sun - Canon register

## Hard canon currently confirmed
- The Temple of Karsh has been abandoned for a long time.
- Metal weapons and armour have gone missing from a nearby village.
- Villagers report chanting from the old temple at night.
- Karsh is addressed in a source prayer asking that iron be turned to rust.
- The same prayer asks protection from those who keep weapons shining and calls for them to be slain with stone.
- The prayer is attributed to `Old Veemor`.
- The adventure is a short OSR/system-agnostic scenario and is explicitly CC BY 4.0.

## Hard unknowns
- identity/nature of Old Veemor
- whether Karsh is a god, spirit, saint, demon or other entity
- who is chanting
- who stole the village's metal equipment
- what happened to the missing equipment
- cult/faction identity and motivation
- temple map, rooms and hazards
- any rust mechanics
- ending and reward states

## AI guard
The AI DM may present villagers' suspicions and rumours, but cannot turn them into truth before source extraction. It must not decide that worshippers/cultists stole the weapons, that Karsh physically exists, or that a rust curse is active merely because those explanations are thematically plausible.
