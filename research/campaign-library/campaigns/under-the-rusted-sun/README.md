# Under the Rusted Sun

Status: `researching`

## Executive summary

`Under the Rusted Sun` is a short OSR adventure by Ruby and Moss. A nearby village has been losing metal weapons and armour, and villagers hear chanting from the long-abandoned Temple of Karsh at night. The creator's opening prayer establishes a religious/ideological hostility to iron and polished weapons: Karsh is invoked to turn iron to rust and protect worshippers from those who keep weapons shining.

The creator explicitly states that **Under the Rusted Sun © 2024 by Ruby and Moss is licensed under CC BY 4.0**. The PDF is free/pay-what-you-want and only 157 KB, making this a strong short-form candidate once the actual document is extracted.
