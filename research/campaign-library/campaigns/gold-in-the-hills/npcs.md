# Gold in the Hills - NPCs / Actors

## Xikek 'the Loud'
Role: Black Fang chief; hobgoblin.

Goals:
- protect the tribe/lair;
- exploit the exposed gold while it lasts;
- use gold to acquire better weapons.

Knowledge:
- knows the tribe found gold and has been digging the exposed crevasse;
- does **not** understand the larger history of the dwarven mine or that the accessible vein is essentially exhausted.

Initial location:
- Area 6 if the mine is not already alerted;
- Area 4 with the main force if the alert state mobilised the tribe.

Native Release 21 profile:
- AC 15, HD 2, halberd 1d10, Move 30', Save Fighter 2, Morale 10, HP 14;
- Ring of Protection +1.

## Snerk
Role: Xikek's hobgoblin bodyguard.
Initial location follows Xikek alert state.
Native profile: AC 14, HD 1, mace 1d8, Move 30', Save Fighter 1, Morale 10, HP 9.

## Yuliak
Role: Black Fang shaman / magic-user support.
Initial location follows Xikek alert state.
Native profile: AC 11, HD 1, dagger or spell, Move 40', Save Magic-User 1, Morale 7, HP 4.
Prepared: magic missile.
Spellbook: magic missile, sleep, read magic.

## Entrance guards (2 goblins)
Role: Area 1 lookouts.
- if they detect the party, fire one arrow volley then retreat to Area 4;
- one arms the Area 2 pit during retreat;
- their warning changes the mine's global alert state.

## Area 4 goblin workforce
15 goblins mining the crevasse under pressure from hobgoblins. Their weapons are new, reflecting Xikek's gold-funded rearmament.

## Area 4 hobgoblin overseers
Three hobgoblins bullying/directing the mining goblins. They are part of the main defence.

## Area 5 goblins (5)
Resting in the tribal lair. Important source behaviour: they **do not help Area 4** because they are on their day off and do not want to go to the mine.

DMOS must preserve this behaviour even if it seems tactically irrational.

## Tentacle Worm
Area 3 shaft predator. The goblins effectively use the shaft as garbage disposal; the creature now treats it as home. Attacks when it notices characters.

## Dwarven Mining Automaton
Not a normal NPC/follower. Ancient damaged machine in Area 4.

Capabilities/constraints:
- two drill arms; left drill badly damaged;
- understands simple two-word Dwarven commands such as go/dig, move/forward, attack/enemies;
- understands single-word stop/wait;
- only recognises traditional dwarf enemies for an enemy-target command;
- if commanded to attack with no valid traditional enemy, attacks nearest object/creature;
- 20% catastrophic failure chance while in motion; failure destroys automaton and damages everyone within 20 feet;
- can be repaired enough to walk back to civilisation and sold for several thousand gp.

DMOS guard: never give it fluent conversation, modern command parsing, loyalty/friendship or independent strategic intelligence.
