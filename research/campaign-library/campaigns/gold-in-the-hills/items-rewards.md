# Gold in the Hills - Treasure / Salvage

- Black Fang goblins commonly carry 1d4 raw gold nuggets worth about 1 gp each; their conspicuously new weapons are a clue to Xikek's recent gold-funded rearmament.
- The earthquake-exposed gold vein is already almost exhausted. It is not an endless income source.
- Area 4 contains a damaged dwarven mining automaton. If repaired enough to extract, the source says it can walk back to civilisation and be sold for several thousand gp. Its movement carries a 20% catastrophic-failure risk until properly dealt with.
- Behind Area 4's secret door is an incomplete second automaton frame and toolbox, not another functioning construct.
- Xikek wears a Ring of Protection +1.
- Yuliak's spellbook contains magic missile, sleep and read magic.
- Area 6 chief chest is dart-trapped for 1d4 damage and contains 400 gp value in gold nuggets, 326 cp, 168 sp, 48 gp and 2 Potions of Healing.

Standard weapons/potions/ring should link to the appropriate native-system item definitions. The mining automaton remains a campaign construct/salvage object rather than an ordinary backpack item.
