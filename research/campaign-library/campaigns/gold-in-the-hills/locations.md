# Gold in the Hills - Locations and Topology

## Highlands / approach
The mine lies about half a day's walk from Barrow's Edge or a full day from Kingsholm. Wilderness encounters use the native campaign rules. A Dwarvish-speaking character is especially useful because of the ancient mine technology.

## Mine exterior
Unremarkable 10-15 foot portal on the mountain's south side. Dirty seepage water trickles outward. Careful characters can approach without alerting the entrance guards; exterior searching shows substantial goblin activity.

## Area 1 - Guards
Small guard room near entrance.
- Unalerted: two goblins can be caught resting/asleep.
- Alerted: guards fire a volley, retreat north to Area 4 and shout warnings; one pauses to arm Area 2's pit.

Connections: exterior -> Area 1 -> Area 2 / deeper corridor.

## Area 2 - Pit of Protection
Old dwarven defensive pit:
- 35 feet long;
- 10 feet deep;
- east-wall alcove/lever north of pit disables mechanism;
- one-foot-wide west-wall ledge can bypass it;
- alert state determines whether fleeing guards armed it.

At corridor T: stone double doors east toward mining complex; dark chamber west toward elevator shaft.

## Area 3 - Elevator to the Underdark
Large shaft with block/tackle.
- elevator carriage fell and is wedged about 20 feet down;
- ropes worn/frayed;
- Tentacle Worm occupies shaft as goblin garbage-disposal predator;
- if repaired, elevator can reach deeper levels the source leaves to GM creation;
- each use has 5% rope-break chance;
- shaft depth 100 feet, source falling damage applies.

This is an explicit **source boundary/continuation portal**. Deeper levels do not exist as written canon.

## Area 4 - The Mine
Large, well-lit active mining chamber.
- gold crevasse to east with goblins digging;
- hobgoblins bully/control goblin labour;
- main force: 15 goblins and 3 hobgoblins before alert-state leadership changes;
- alert state can add Xikek/Snerk/Yuliak here;
- tribe fights fiercely because this is the lair;
- old mining equipment/slag along north wall hides damaged dwarven mining automaton;
- secret door leads to incomplete second automaton frame/toolbox.

## Area 5 - Tribal lair / day-off room
Living/rest area with pallets, fire/pot and five goblins.

Critical behaviour: these five **do not reinforce Area 4** because it is their day off and they do not care to see the mine that day.

## Area 6 - Chief's chamber
Better-appointed living chamber:
- bearskin rug, bed, oak chair/table;
- treasure chest near bed;
- default occupants: Chief Xikek 'the Loud', bodyguard Snerk, shaman Yuliak;
- on seeing party, Xikek/Snerk attack and Yuliak casts;
- if Area 4 battle lasts more than six rounds while they remain here, they investigate.

Treasure chest is dart-trapped and contains gold nuggets/coins plus healing potions.

## Default topology
`Exterior -> Area 1 -> Area 2 -> branches toward Area 3 / Areas 4-6`

Area 4 is the active mine hub; Areas 5 and 6 are deeper lair/leadership spaces. Exact visual map remains the authority for corridor geometry; DMOS may not create shortcut tunnels. Area 3's deeper shaft is the only source-authorised expansion boundary.
