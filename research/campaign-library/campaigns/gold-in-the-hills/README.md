# Gold in the Hills

Status: `researching`

`Gold in the Hills` is a short Basic Fantasy adventure by Ray Allen from `Adventure Anthology One`, current research edition **1st Edition Release 21**. The source is for **2-4 characters of levels 1-3**.

The Black Fang goblin tribe has begun spending raw gold nuggets and buying new weapons. Their lair is an abandoned dwarven mine. A minor earthquake exposed a limited gold vein; Chief Xikek used the gold to rearm the tribe, but the newly exposed vein is already nearly exhausted.

The six-area mine is stateful. If entrance guards detect the party, they fire once, retreat, warn the mine and arm an old dwarven pit. In an alerted state the leadership can join the main mining force. If the party stays covert, leadership remains deeper in the lair and later reacts to prolonged battle noise.

The source also includes a damaged dwarven mining automaton that can be controlled only with very simple Dwarven commands and can catastrophically fail while moving. A damaged mine elevator can be repaired and explicitly opens GM-created deeper levels beyond the written adventure.

## DMOS guardrails
- Alert/no-alert state changes trap and encounter composition.
- Area 4 battle noise can pull leadership from Area 6 after six rounds if not already mobilised.
- Area 5 goblins explicitly do not help Area 4.
- The automaton's commands/failure state are preserved rather than treated as a normal follower.
- The exposed gold vein is nearly exhausted; the AI may not invent endless gold.
- Deeper mine levels are **GM-expansion territory**, not source canon.
