# Gold in the Hills - Community Intelligence

## Living 4 Crits actual play (2015)
Source: https://ilive4crits.blogspot.com/2015/07/basic-fantasy-rpg-gold-in-hills-session.html

A family group ran the adventure with four randomly generated **level-1 Basic Fantasy characters**. They used maximum starting HP and still described the session as dangerous. The GM improvised `Steel Fisted Goblins` after play.

DMOS use:
- confirms actual play at the bottom of the source's stated level band;
- supports warning that level-1 parties can find the mine dangerous;
- `Steel Fisted Goblins` are GM homebrew from that session and are **not source canon**.

## Scarlet Heroes solo conversion (2022)
Source: https://felbrigg.blogspot.com/2022/08/

A solo player used `Gold in the Hills` to learn Scarlet Heroes with a level-1 halfling fighter. The play report follows the source entrance guards and Pit of Protection route.

DMOS use:
- evidence that the story structure ports to a solo OSR ruleset;
- not enough evidence to advertise Scarlet Heroes as approved support without a dedicated adapter.

## Club/convention use (2016)
Source: https://ilive4crits.blogspot.com/2016/03/?m=0

A game club used the adventure with three players and DCC-style 0-level pregenerated characters due to OSR mechanical similarity.

DMOS use:
- further portability evidence;
- not native balance evidence and not an approved DCC conversion.

## Conclusion
Community reports reinforce the need to separate **source-native Basic Fantasy balance** from optional rules adapters. No community report found in this pass overrides alert logic, room contents or source canon.
