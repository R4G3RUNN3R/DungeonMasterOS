# Gold in the Hills - Canon Locks

Authoritative gameplay version: `Adventure Anthology One`, Release 21.

## Hard canon
- The Black Fang tribe lairs in an abandoned dwarven mine once associated with Gryk Thalak.
- A minor earthquake exposed a limited gold vein.
- Chief Xikek has been using gold nuggets to buy new weapons for the tribe.
- Xikek does not understand the mine's history and the exposed vein is almost played out.
- The party can approach the mine without alerting the entrance guards if sufficiently careful.
- If Area 1 guards notice intruders, they fire one volley, retreat to Area 4, warn the tribe, and one arms the old dwarven pit trap in Area 2.
- Area 2's pit is an old dwarven defensive mechanism with a disabling lever and a narrow ledge bypass.
- Area 3's damaged elevator is wedged 20 feet down and a Tentacle Worm lives in the shaft.
- Repairing the elevator opens access to deeper mine levels that the source explicitly leaves for the GM to develop.
- Area 4 is the active mining chamber containing the main goblin/hobgoblin workforce and the damaged dwarven mining automaton.
- If the tribe is already alerted, Xikek, Snerk and Yuliak join the Area 4 defence.
- If they remain in Area 6 and battle in Area 4 lasts more than six rounds, Xikek/Snerk/Yuliak investigate.
- Area 5's five goblins are on their day off and explicitly do not help Area 4.
- The damaged mining automaton understands only very short Dwarven commands and may catastrophically fail while moving.
- Area 6 contains Chief Xikek, Snerk and shaman Yuliak in the unalerted/default state.
- The mine does not contain an inexhaustible source of gold.

## Conditional canon
- `mineAlerted=true` moves the entrance guards to Area 4, arms the pit, and mobilises leadership into the main mine defence.
- `mineAlerted=false` leaves the entrance guards in Area 1, the leadership in Area 6, and allows separate room resolution.
- Area 4 combat over six rounds while leadership remains in Area 6 causes leadership to investigate.
- The mining automaton has a 20% catastrophic-failure chance whenever it is in motion because of its damaged drill; failure destroys it and harms nearby creatures.
- If the automaton receives an unsupported/ambiguous attack command and no traditional dwarf enemy is valid, it attacks the nearest object or creature as the source states.
- If repaired enough for extraction, the automaton can be walked back to civilisation and sold for several thousand gold pieces.
- Each elevator use after repair has a 5% source rope-failure chance; the shaft is 100 feet deep.

## Soft canon / source-authorised expansion
The source explicitly leaves deeper elevator-accessed mine levels for the GM to create. DMOS may offer a continuation/homebrew expansion **only after clearly marking the written adventure boundary**. Generated lower levels are not Ray Allen/AA1 canon.

## Forbidden AI deviations
- Do not invent an endless mother lode.
- Do not make Area 5 automatically reinforce Area 4; they explicitly refuse.
- Do not teleport Xikek/Yuliak/Snerk between Areas 4 and 6 without alert/noise state.
- Do not make the automaton fluent, intelligent or safely controllable with natural-language orders.
- Do not turn GM-developed lower levels into source-derived canon.
- Do not invent D&D 5e levels; native support is Basic Fantasy levels 1-3.
