# Born Into Black Nights - Important Items and Rewards

## Plot-critical / clue-critical

### Bertram's ring
- Initial owner in play: Kyran, who stole it from Bertram's remains.
- Recognisable by: Osian.
- Importance: strong evidence connecting Kyran to the disturbed tomb; restoring it to Bertram strengthens the proper-rest resolution.
- Guard: the source says restoration of the ring is desirable "if possible", so DMOS must not make a lost ring an impossible hard-lock on campaign completion.

### Bertram's journal
- Location: Bertram's chamber.
- Importance: historical account of the settlement's early days.
- Value: potentially important to the burgomaster/townspeople beyond simple sale price.

## Tomb treasure / useful resources
- Room 2: serviceable knife blade needing a handle; rusty crowbar.
- Room 3: sealed holy water; tarnished silver incense burner.
- Room 4: valuable historical tapestry of early Bertram's Grove.
- Room 5: Rod of the Fisher Folk, a three-charge magical fishing rod with source-defined recharge condition; spellbook of plant control.
- Room 7: hidden cache of small precious stones.
- Room 8: random Cairn spellbook; three single-use magical arrows dealing enhanced damage.
- Room 9: surviving standard shield.

## External rewards
If the nightmare crisis is demonstrably ended, Oskar can reward the heroes through coin, favour or local services. His preferred substantial reward is land/homestead near Bertram's Grove, encouraging the settlement to become a campaign base.

Successful resolution can also support a shared human/gnome celebration. Treat that as an aftermath opportunity, not an automatic erasure of every existing social tension.

## DMOS integration notes
Where an item already exists in the DungeonMasterOS master item compendium, use a canonical definition key and preserve campaign-specific provenance separately. Adventure-unique items such as Bertram's ring/journal and Rod of the Fisher Folk need campaign-scoped item definitions or aliases with explicit source attribution.
