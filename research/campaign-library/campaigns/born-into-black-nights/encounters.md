# Born Into Black Nights - Encounter Model

## Town / investigation encounters

### Oskar's request
Trigger: party engages the burgomaster/family or learns of Zahra's condition.
Objective: establish the nightmare crisis and potential employment/reward.
Alternatives: party may investigate without accepting payment.

### Zahra sleepwalking
Trigger: Zahra is not sufficiently watched during an episode.
Objective: protect/follow her; creates a direct physical clue toward the ruins.
Fail-forward: following rather than stopping her can reveal direction without resolving the underlying danger.

### Kyran confrontation
Trigger: party identifies Kyran as a likely grave robber, sees the ring, or gains enough rumours.
Approaches: bribery, intimidation, deception, negotiation, theft, surveillance or violence.
Canonical payload: Kyran disturbed the tomb and possesses Bertram's ring.
Guard: combat is not required and killing Kyran does not resolve the haunting.

## Woodland encounters (d6 or Warden choice)
1. Wounded/hungry bear, fearful and already injured.
2. 1d6+2 brigands who exploit weakness but do not fight fanatically to the death.
3. 1d6 wandering zombies from the tomb area.
4. 2d6 isolationist woodland gnomes wary of the town settlement; may become aggressive if pressed.
5. 1d6+3 aggressive wild dogs.
6. 1d3 boars; their meat has local value.

## Tomb encounter principles

- Wandering threats can include vampire bat, zombie, cave locust or pit viper.
- Room hazards are location-specific and should not be randomly duplicated elsewhere.
- Door state matters. Locked, stuck, reinforced and collapsed passages are distinct state constraints.

### Room 2 - Giant weasel return
Triggered by extended lingering/camping. The animal is attracted by previous food scraps rather than supernatural purpose.

### Room 3 - Pit viper
Triggered by disturbing/searching its current home among the ruin debris.

### Room 4 - Ceiling slimes
Three slimes use the ceiling/drop tactic. Mundane attacks are poor against them except fire according to the source profile.

### Room 5 - Grave awakening
Disturbing the floor/graves awakens twelve skeletons. This is a consequence of interaction, not an automatic entrance encounter.

### Room 6 - Flooded fish
1d6 cave fish/giant-rockfish-type creatures in stagnant deep water.

### Room 7 - Trapped zombies
2d6 zombies behind reinforced/locked/stuck access. Opening access changes the encounter state.

### Room 8 - Flies and plant-overgrown zombies
Biting flies create repeated WIL/action pressure; 1d6 zombies are present. Dense vegetation slows movement.

### Room 9 - Fire haunting
Psychological/supernatural hazard. Failed WIL resistance causes Fatigue and repeated haunting pressure until resisted. Do not convert this automatically into combat.

### Room 10 - Bertram and skeletons
Bertram (Draugr) plus 1d6 skeletons. Defeating them is only an intermediate state. The encounter's real objective is discovering and executing proper rest. Without it, the dead begin to return.

## Failure / escalation handling

- Retreat from the tomb leaves the nightmare crisis active.
- Losing/abandoning Bertram's ring does not make completion impossible: the source says the ring should be restored "if possible"; peaceful restoration of Bertram remains the core requirement.
- Destroying Bertram completely is a negative long-term state, not victory: future ghost return and possession/nightmares become valid continuation material.
