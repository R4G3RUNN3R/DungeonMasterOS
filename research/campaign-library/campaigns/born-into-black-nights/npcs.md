# Born Into Black Nights - NPCs and Knowledge

## Oskar
Human burgomaster of Bertram's Grove. Father of Zahra, husband of Fleur. Public goal: stop his daughter's haunting/nightmares. He believes something is haunting Zahra and will hire capable help. He previously dismissed Osian's explanation.

## Zahra
Human daughter of Oskar and Fleur. The most sensitive victim of the haunting because of her family connection to Bertram. Suffers severe nightmares and sleepwalking. If not stopped, her sleepwalking leads toward the ruins. She is a clue-bearing victim, not an antagonist.

## Fleur
Human wife of Oskar and mother of Zahra. Bertram's great-granddaughter, making Zahra Bertram's great-great-granddaughter. Concerned that Zahra is cursed and has become emotionally distant while hoping for a solution. Her ancestry is not automatically public knowledge.

## Tiana
Human bodyguard serving Oskar's family for years. Capable fighter with other practical skills. Exhausted because she is preventing Zahra from wandering away during sleepwalking episodes. Dedicated and unwilling to abandon the family.

## Anet
Gnome potion crafter. Wants anise plants for sleep-related potion work and can offer payment or trade. Can provide guidance or a rough route toward where the herb can be found, creating a non-haunting-specific route toward the ruins.

## Pyke
Introverted gnome woodsman. Has seen groups of undead roaming the woods. Avoided them but may guide heroes if doing so benefits the town. Provides an undead/wilderness clue path.

## Osian
Gnome mystic, respected among gnomes and often dismissed by human residents. Has lived locally for a very long time and personally knew Bertram. Knows Fleur/Zahra's descent from Bertram. Suspects disturbance of the old tomb caused the nightmares. Can recognise Bertram's stolen ring if seen.

## Kyran
Dwarf grave robber/treasure seeker staying at the tavern and preparing to leave. Scarred face; highly coin-motivated. Kyran disturbed the tomb and stole Bertram's ring, causing the supernatural unrest. Reluctant to admit this but can potentially be bribed, coerced, frightened or otherwise persuaded. Has boarded/sealed some tomb doors behind him. Not a necromancer and does not control the undead.

## Bertram
Deceased founder of the settlement. Currently manifests as a Draugr after Kyran's disturbance. His true goal is to be laid properly to rest. Bertram is not coherently conversational in the Draugr state and produces confused/moaning speech. Correct handling of his remains and peaceful rites are more important than combat victory.

## Optional generated townspeople
The source provides spare human and gnome names and explicitly authorises the Warden to add residents. DMOS may generate minor NPCs for services, rumours and local colour, but generated characters cannot replace source NPCs as mandatory clue holders or contradict the established ancestry/haunting truth.
