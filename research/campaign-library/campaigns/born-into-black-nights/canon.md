# Born Into Black Nights - Canon Locks

## Hard canon
- Bertram's Grove is a mixed human/gnome settlement suffering undead/ghost nightmares.
- Zahra, daughter of Oskar and Fleur, is the worst affected and sleepwalks toward the ruins if not stopped.
- Zahra descends from Bertram through Fleur's family line.
- Kyran, a dwarf grave robber, disturbed Bertram's tomb and stole Bertram's ring.
- Kyran's disturbance caused the dead to become restless and triggered the haunting.
- Osian knew Bertram personally, knows the family connection and can recognise Bertram's ring.
- Bertram was one of the settlement's founders and rested peacefully before the tomb was disturbed.
- Bertram's supernatural objective is rest, not conquest or revenge.
- Bertram manifests as a Draugr in the tomb.
- Defeating Bertram/undead without the proper rest rite does not permanently solve the haunting.
- Proper rest means returning Bertram to the sarcophagus, preferably restoring his ring, replacing the lid, and giving a prayer/wish/peaceful rite for the dead.
- Successful rest causes the tomb's undead to return to their graves and ends the nightmares.
- If Bertram is completely destroyed rather than laid to rest, he can return about a month later as a ghost, resume the nightmares and potentially possess townspeople or heroes.

## Conditional canon
- Zahra left to sleepwalk beyond town travels toward the tomb.
- Identifying Kyran's ring or obtaining his confession strongly connects the crisis to the disturbed tomb.
- Disturbing room 5 awakens the buried skeletons.
- Opening room 7 releases/activates its trapped zombie threat.
- Failed resistance in room 9 continues the fire-haunting/fatigue pressure until resisted.
- Bertram defeated without the rest rite leaves the supernatural crisis unresolved.
- Completing the rest rite resolves the nightmare crisis regardless of which initial hook brought the party to the tomb.

## Soft canon / Warden-authorised expansion
The source explicitly allows the Warden to add townspeople and expand Bertram's Grove. DMOS may improvise minor residents, buildings and ordinary services if they do not contradict the named NPCs, clue graph or human/gnome social context.

## Forbidden AI deviations
- Do not make Bertram secretly evil merely to manufacture a boss fight.
- Do not count killing every undead as permanent victory unless the rest condition is also satisfied.
- Do not reveal the family connection before a valid clue supports it.
- Do not give Kyran supernatural control over the undead.
- Do not invent a D&D-style level requirement for this Cairn adventure.
