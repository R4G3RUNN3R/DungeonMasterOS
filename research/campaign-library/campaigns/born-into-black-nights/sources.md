# Born Into Black Nights - Sources and Rights

## Primary source

- Creator page: https://junkyardtornado.itch.io/born-into-black-nights
- Creator/author: Jason Renslow / JunkyardTornado
- Native ruleset: Cairn
- Primary page states that the adventure text is available under **CC BY-SA 4.0**.
- The page credits Cairn separately under CC BY-SA 4.0 and identifies the artwork as public domain.
- The page provides the adventure PDF plus separate Bertram's Grove and Bertram's Tomb map images.
- The page explicitly says the adventure can be adapted to another fantasy TTRPG with minimal adjustment.

## Full-text research source

A publicly indexed copy of the 13-page adventure was used to recover the detailed text structure for research and transformation:

- Indexed document: https://www.scribd.com/document/805545676/Born-Into-Black-Nights-A5

This mirror is **not** treated as licence authority. Its generic uploader copyright label conflicts with the original creator's explicit CC BY-SA grant. Rights and attribution are determined from the creator's primary itch.io page and the adventure's own credited licence statement, both of which identify the text as CC BY-SA 4.0.

## Source facts captured

The indexed text exposes:

- premise and Warden guidance;
- five adventure hooks;
- named town NPCs and their knowledge;
- six-entry woodland encounter table;
- tomb history and wandering-monster table;
- all ten tomb locations and their door states/major contents;
- Bertram's actual rest condition;
- consequences of killing without proper rest;
- consequences of total destruction;
- conclusion/reward guidance;
- repeated CC BY-SA credit statement.

## DungeonMasterOS rights disposition

- Commercial reuse: permitted by CC BY-SA 4.0, subject to licence terms.
- Adaptation: permitted.
- Attribution: required.
- ShareAlike: required for adapted text covered by the licence.
- Built-in template: permitted if DMOS packaging preserves attribution/licence obligations.
- Art/maps: do not automatically redistribute based solely on the text licence; track their separate source/rights metadata where used.
