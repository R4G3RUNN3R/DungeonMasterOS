# Born Into Black Nights - Locations

## Bertram's Grove
Mixed human/gnome settlement. The source supplies named residents and hooks but explicitly leaves room for the Warden to add ordinary NPCs and details. Nightmares affect many residents; Zahra is the strongest visible case.

## Surrounding woods
Travel zone between town and the forgotten tomb. Random encounters: wounded/hungry bear, brigands, tomb-wandering zombies, isolationist woodland gnomes, aggressive wild dogs, and boars. Roads are safer partly because wild dogs make the woods dangerous.

## Bertram's Tomb
Overgrown, partially sunken and easy to overlook. Once used for many early townspeople before being sealed and forgotten. Kyran disturbed it and triggered the haunting.

### Room 1 - Entrance chamber
Broken former sealed entrance; stairs descend. East door toward room 6 is stuck; west toward room 2 is open/propped.

### Room 2 - Old camp
Evidence of a small camp/cook fire. Useful mundane salvage includes a blade needing a new handle and a rusty crowbar. A giant weasel may return if the party lingers.

### Room 3 - Former funerary temple
Ruined prayer space for peaceful transition of the deceased. East door toward room 7 locked; west toward room 4 stuck. Searchable remains include holy water and a silver incense burner; a pit viper may react to disturbance.

### Room 4 - Tapestry room
Preserved tapestry depicting early Bertram's Grove. Collapsed northern route toward room 5 can be excavated with serious effort. Ceiling slimes threaten searchers.

### Room 5 - Grave hall
Large grave area with unstable/difficult floor. Disturbing it awakens twelve skeletons. Contains a magical fishing relic and a plant-control spellbook.

### Room 6 - Flooded passage
Eroded, stagnant flooded route, roughly four feet deep in the middle, inhabited by cave fish/giant-rockfish equivalents.

### Room 7 - Boarded undead chamber
Moans audible outside. South door was reinforced by Kyran; west door locked; north door stuck. Contains a hidden precious-stone cache and 2d6 trapped zombies.

### Room 8 - Overgrown roof-break chamber
Sunlight enters through roof holes. Dense vines impede movement. South door stuck; east door open/free. Contains a long-dead grave robber's protected spellbook and three magical arrows. Biting flies impair action economy and 1d6 plant-overgrown zombies inhabit the room.

### Room 9 - Fire-death memorial chamber
Resting place for townsfolk killed in a terrible fire. Entering triggers sensory/vision haunting of fire, smoke, shadows and screams. Characters must resist or gain Fatigue and repeat resistance while affected. A surviving shield remains here.

### Room 10 - Bertram's chamber
Bertram manifests here as a Draugr with 1d6 skeletons. West door free; south route was boarded by Kyran. Bertram's sarcophagus is the focus of the true resolution. His journal documents early town history. Defeated undead can re-form if proper rest is not completed.
