# D&D 5e 2014 vs Revised 2024 Rules-Engine Differences

**Research/access date:** 2026-08-23

**Compared open rules:** SRD 5.1 (2014 generation) and SRD 5.2.1 (revised 2024 generation)

## Evidence vocabulary and comparison limits

- `[OFFICIAL SOURCE]` means an official Wizards of the Coast/D&D Beyond artifact.
- `[REPOSITORY FACT]` means a fact verified in the frozen DungeonMasterOS branch at `176456af5f79400bafe39836bbe01faebdfd4913`.
- `[DERIVED SOURCE]` and `[SECONDARY SOURCE]` are not used to establish any mechanical difference in this report.
- `[INFERENCE]` means a proposed engineering or migration consequence of cited official rules.
- `[OPEN QUESTION]` means the official material reviewed does not support a safe deterministic conclusion yet.
- `[EXECUTION ATTESTATION]` records what this documentation-only task did or did not change.

`[OFFICIAL SOURCE]` The conversion guide labels changes as New Name, New Rule, Revised Rule, or Omitted Rule. It is the strongest official migration inventory, but it is not an exhaustive semantic diff for every spell, class feature, condition, item, or monster. Every deterministic implementation still needs an entry-level source anchor.

`[INFERENCE]` “Backward compatible” does not mean “mechanically identical.” Shared infrastructure is appropriate only when a paired test proves the behavior for both rulesets. Same name is never sufficient evidence.

## Migration analysis

### Safely automatable scaffolding

`[INFERENCE]` Subject to preview, audit, and the constrained rollback policy below, a future conversion workflow can:

- duplicate a campaign/character projection while preserving the original immutable snapshot;
- retain narrative identity, notes, and currency; preserve source XP/level as immutable evidence and propose target XP/level for review; retain inventory quantities, instance identity, and source provenance without making a legacy canonical definition executable under the target ruleset;
- apply proven display aliases while retaining original canonical/source IDs in history;
- ignore legacy species ASI contributions automatically only when stored provenance separates base scores, species contributions, later ASIs, magic/effects, and manual overrides; otherwise create unresolved ability-score reconstruction choices rather than subtracting guessed values;
- create pending revised-background ASI and Origin-feat choice slots;
- recalculate derived values only after explicit revised definitions and choices are selected;
- suggest official mapping candidates and identify omissions/conflicts;
- require every retained inventory instance to receive an explicit target disposition: mapped to an exact revised definition, retained as inert/reference-only legacy property, removed from the revised draft, or held for authorized manual review; executable cross-ruleset references remain prohibited unless a future hybrid policy explicitly permits them;
- produce a deterministic before/after diff and a manual-review checklist.

### Requires player or DM review

`[OFFICIAL SOURCE]` Official campaign guidance recommends group agreement, a planned transition, duplicating the old character sheet, and rebuilding class/background/species/ability choices rather than silently updating the original [O23].

`[INFERENCE]` Require review for:

- whether and when the campaign changes generation, and whether any hybrid content policy is allowed;
- revised class/subclass and every feature/resource choice;
- background ability allocation, Origin feat, languages, and species when no exact equivalent exists;
- prepared spells, changed/removed spells, feats, and weapon Mastery choices;
- same-name equipment whose properties/action cost changed;
- every inventory disposition that is not an exact evidence-backed mapping, including whether a legacy-only item remains inert/reference-only or is omitted from the revised projection;
- monster/encounter replacement and tactics;
- current resource translation after maximum/recovery rules change;
- any nonzero Exhaustion level;
- active Grappled, Hidden, Surprised, Stunned, Invisible, or concentration state;
- active suffocation, falling/liquid-impact reaction, or knockout Short Rest/first-aid state;
- Inspiration/Heroic Inspiration token carryover;
- any conversion during combat, an unfinished rest, a pending reaction, or a partially spent turn.

`[INFERENCE]` Default product policy should permit conversion only at a scene/session boundary, preferably after a completed Long Rest, with no active encounter or pending rules transaction. That boundary is an engineering safeguard, not an official D&D rule.

`[INFERENCE]` A one-step rollback to the pre-conversion snapshot is safe only while no later state-changing event exists. Once revised-rule play has produced damage, rolls, resource use, inventory changes, advancement, or other dependent events, “rollback” must be a new previewed forward conversion/reconciliation transaction; switching the snapshot alone would leave downstream state with the wrong rules provenance.

## Open questions and uncertainty register

- `[OPEN QUESTION]` The conversion guide marks many entries “Revised Rule” without enumerating every semantic delta. Perform canonical entry-level diffs before declaring any condition, spell, class feature, item action, or monster behavior equivalent.
- `[OPEN QUESTION]` Exact spell/monster/item mappings must come from official 5.2.1 records and conversion tables. Normalized-name matching is only a candidate generator.
- `[OPEN QUESTION]` Official material does not define a deterministic hybrid policy. DungeonMasterOS must decide whether a campaign can enable 2014 and revised character options together and, if so, which exact evaluator applies to each event.
- `[OPEN QUESTION]` Official conversion guidance does not supply algorithms for active combat state, incomplete rests, current class resources, or in-flight effects. These remain prohibited/manual-review states unless later contracts prove a safe mapping.
- `[OPEN QUESTION]` Legacy final ability scores without contribution provenance do not support deterministic removal of species ASIs. Such characters require player reconstruction; do not infer base scores from current totals.
- `[OPEN QUESTION]` A strict target-ruleset campaign cannot execute retained other-generation inventory definitions. Each instance needs a reviewed target disposition unless a separately specified hybrid mode later authorizes cross-generation content.
- `[OPEN QUESTION]` SRD 5.2.1 is an open subset of revised commercial books. Never infer a missing open entity or rule from the commercial corpus.
- `[OPEN QUESTION]` Full attunement/rarity semantic equivalence and every unchanged core rule still require manifest-level source comparisons; absence from this matrix is not proof of sameness.

## Conclusion

`[INFERENCE]` DungeonMasterOS can share orchestration, storage envelopes, dice, events, targeting, and effect machinery, but it must never share mutable canonical definitions or implicit default behavior across these generations. `dnd5e2014` and `dnd5e2024` need independent corpora, evaluators, prompt context, adapters, manifests, revisions, and paired tests. Existing `dnd5e` state remains legacy/ambiguous until an explicit migration decision is made.
