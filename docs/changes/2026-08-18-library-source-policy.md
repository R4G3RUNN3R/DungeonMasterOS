# Library source policy

- Public/open SRD mechanics may be stored and rendered where the applicable license permits.
- Official books in the owner's private Drive are verification/reference sources, not bulk-publication sources.
- Closed-source text should be represented as structured facts, DungeonMasterOS-authored concise summaries, and source/page references rather than copied passages.
- The UI may evoke the usability of a tabletop sourcebook, but should retain original DungeonMasterOS layout/art direction rather than reproduce protected trade dress or page artwork.
